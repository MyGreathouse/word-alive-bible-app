# WABV: 2 John

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-AGAPE\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Second John is the twenty-fourth book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the fifth of the seven general letters. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It is the shorter of the two shortest books of the New Testament (thirteen verses, a single page), and it is written by "the elder" (verse 1) to "the elect lady and her children", who may be an assembly or a person, and it has the same vocabulary as 1 John (truth, love, to abide, the antichrist) and the same warning against those who do not confess Jesus Christ as come in the flesh. Who the elder is is not stated, and the tradition names John, and the WABV text does not decide, and who the lady is is also not decided. The chapter has no division, and the verse numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text of 2 John is drafted with the voice standard (Part 46 of the review package). Where the text of a critical edition is not certain, the draft says so in the footnote and asks the team to confirm the reading. It makes the same fixed choices as 1 John for the words that the two letters share. *Meno* is rendered "abide". *Agape* is rendered "love" and is tagged G-AGAPE. *Aletheia* is rendered "truth" and is tagged G-ALETHEIA. *Charis*, *eleos* and *eirene* are rendered "grace", "mercy" and "peace" and are tagged G-CHARIS, G-ELEOS and G-EIRENE. *Antichristos* (verse 7) is rendered "antichrist". *Didache* (verses 9-10) is rendered "teaching". *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## The letter, verses 1 to 13

**1** The elder to the elect lady and her children, whom I love \[G-AGAPE\] in truth \[G-ALETHEIA\], and not only I, but also all who know the truth,

**2** because of the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] that abides in us and will be with us for ever:

**3** grace \[G-CHARIS\], mercy \[G-ELEOS\], and peace \[G-EIRENE\] will be with us, from God the Father and from Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] the Father's Son, in truth and love \[G-AGAPE\].

**4** I rejoiced greatly to find some of your children walking in the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\], just as we were commanded by the Father.

**5** And now I ask you, lady, not as though I were writing you a new commandment, but the one we have had from the beginning, that we love \[G-AGAPE\] one another.

**6** And this is love \[G-AGAPE\], that we walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, just as you have heard from the beginning, so that you should walk in it.

**7** For many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not confess Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] as coming in the flesh. Such a person is the deceiver and the antichrist.

**8** Watch yourselves, so that you may not lose what we have worked for, but may win a full reward.

**9** Everyone who goes ahead and does not abide in the teaching of Christ does not have God. Whoever abides in the teaching has both the Father and the Son.

**10** If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into your house or give him any greeting,

**11** for whoever greets him takes part in his wicked works.

**12** Though I have much to write to you, I would rather not use paper and ink. Instead I hope to come to you and talk face to face, so that our joy may be complete.

**13** The children of your elect sister greet you.

*Review note: 2 John was drafted in WABV wording, from the Greek, using the fixed choices set out in the introduction to this book, and with the WABV voice standard (Part 46 of the review package) applied. It has the greeting (verses 1-3), the joy over the children who walk in the truth (4), the commandment to love (5-6), the deceivers and the antichrist (7-8), abiding in the teaching of Christ (9-11) and the closing (12-13). The numbering is the usual numbering. No new G- marker is used, and the markers are G-AGAPE (1-6), G-ALETHEIA (1-4), G-CHARIS (3), G-ELEOS (3), G-EIRENE (3) and G-CHRISTOS (3, 7). Several readings are drafted with some uncertainty and are to be confirmed against the critical edition in use: verse 5 ("lady" as a title or the name Kyria), verse 7 ("as coming" or "as having come"), verse 8 ("you may not lose what we have worked for", where the manuscripts differ between "we" and "you") and verse 12 ("our joy" or "your joy"). The main points are these, and the footnotes carry the Greek: "the elder" and "the elect lady and her children" are rendered as written, and who they are is debated, and the WABV text does not decide; verse 7 "those who do not confess Jesus Christ as coming in the flesh" translates *erchomenon*, the present participle, and is rendered as written; verses 10 and 11 "do not receive him into your house or give him any greeting" are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. There are no departures from the critical Greek text in 2 John. The Greek is hard in some places, including verses 1, 7, 8 and 12. The words on the elect lady (1), truth and love (3-6), the deceiver and the antichrist (7), the teaching of Christ (9) and the refusal of greeting (10-11) are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. The section needs a check against the Greek by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked. With this the book of 2 John is complete in first draft.*

## Appendix A: Footnotes

- **1** "The elder" translates *ho presbuteros*, and who he is is not stated, and the tradition names John. "The elect lady and her children" is rendered as written, and may be an assembly and its members or a woman and her children, and the WABV text does not decide. See entries G-AGAPE and G-ALETHEIA.
- **2** "The truth that abides in us and will be with us for ever" is rendered as written.
- **3** "Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us" is the form of greeting here, and it is a statement and not a wish. See entries G-CHARIS, G-ELEOS, G-EIRENE and G-CHRISTOS.
- **4** "Walking in the truth" is rendered as written.
- **5** "Lady" translates *kuria*, and may be read as a title or as a name (Kyria), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use. "A new commandment, but the one we have had from the beginning" is as in 1 John 2:7-8.
- **6** "This is love, that we walk according to his commandments" is rendered as written.
- **7** "Those who do not confess Jesus Christ as coming in the flesh" translates *erchomenon*, the present participle, and may be read "as coming" or "as having come", and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use. "The deceiver and the antichrist" is as in 1 John 2:18 and 4:1-3.
- **8** "You may not lose what we have worked for" is the reading that the draft follows, and the manuscripts differ between "we" and "you" in the verbs, and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **9** "Goes ahead" translates *proagon*, and some manuscripts read "transgresses" (*parabainon*), and "the teaching of Christ" may mean the teaching that Christ gave or the teaching about Christ, and the WABV text does not decide.
- **10** "Do not receive him into your house or give him any greeting" is rendered as written, and "house" may mean an assembly that meets in a house.
- **12** "Paper and ink" is rendered as written, and "our joy" is the reading that the draft follows, and some manuscripts read "your joy", and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **13** "The children of your elect sister" is rendered as written, and some manuscripts add "Amen".
