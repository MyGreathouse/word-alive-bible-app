# WABV: Mark

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-PNEUMA\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Mark is the second book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. The book is a gospel, the shortest of the four, and it is written in plain, fast Greek, with short sentences joined by "and", with the word *euthys* ("immediately") very often, and with many verbs in the historic present. The WABV text keeps these features where the Greek has them, and does not smooth them away, and the Gospel voice sheet (Part 40 of the review package) applies. The book is anonymous in the Greek, and the name "Mark" comes from the title and from early tradition. The WABV text does not decide on the questions of authorship, date or sources, and the footnotes record only the language. The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. Some verses that the Byzantine tradition has are not in the critical Greek text, and the end of the book is a special case: the critical text ends at 16:8, and 16:9-20 is a longer ending that is in most manuscripts but not in the earliest, and the WABV text gives it in a separate section with a footnote that sets out the other endings. The words "Son of Man", "brothers" and "Christ" are open decisions, and they are rendered literally in the draft and flagged in the footnotes for the team. The book falls into six parts in the WABV text: the beginning and the conflicts in Galilee (chapters 1 to 3), the parables and the mighty acts (4 to 6), what makes unclean and the road with the disciples (7 to 9), the way to Jerusalem and the questions in the temple (10 to 12), the teaching on the end and the passion (13 and 14), and the death, the burial and the empty tomb (15 and 16). The headings of the parts are not in the Greek. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System, and the new ones are listed in the review note of the part where each first appears. The names of God and of Jesus are given as the Greek has them: "God" for *theos*, "the Lord" for *kyrios*, "Jesus" and "Christ". The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Part One: Chapters 1 to 3, the beginning and the conflicts in Galilee

### Chapter 1

**1** The beginning of the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], the Son of God.

**2** As it is written in Isaiah the prophet: "Look, I am sending my messenger ahead of you, who will prepare your road,

**3** a voice shouting in the wilderness: Prepare the road of the Lord, make his paths straight."

**4** John the baptiser appeared in the wilderness, announcing a baptism of repentance \[G-METANOIA\] for the forgiveness of sins.

**5** And all the Judean countryside went out to him, and all the people of Jerusalem, and they were baptised by him in the river Jordan, confessing their sins.

**6** John was dressed in camel hair, with a leather belt round his waist, and he ate locusts and wild honey.

**7** He announced, "After me comes one who is stronger than I am. I am not fit to bend down and untie the strap of his sandals.

**8** I baptised you in water, but he will baptise you in the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\]."

**9** In those days Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee and was baptised by John in the Jordan.

**10** Straight away, as he came up out of the water, he saw the heavens being torn open and the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] coming down on him like a dove.

**11** And a voice came out of the heavens: "You are my Son, the beloved \[G-AGAPE\]. In you I take delight."

**12** And straight away the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] drives him out into the wilderness.

**13** He was in the wilderness forty days, tested by Satan. He was with the wild animals, and the angels looked after him.

**14** After John was handed over, Jesus came into Galilee announcing the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of God

**15** and saying, "The time has been fulfilled, and the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of God has come near. Repent \[G-METANOIA\], and believe in the good news \[G-EUANGELION\]."

**16** As he walked along the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew, Simon's brother, casting a net in the sea, for they were fishermen.

**17** Jesus said to them, "Come after me, and I will make you become fishers of people."

**18** And straight away they left their nets and followed him.

**19** Going a little further, he saw James son of Zebedee and his brother John, who were in the boat mending their nets.

**20** And straight away he called them. They left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired workers and went off after him.

**21** They go into Capernaum. And straight away on the Sabbath \[G-SABBATON\] he went into the synagogue and began teaching.

**22** They were astonished at his teaching, for he taught them as one having authority, and not as the scribes.

**23** Straight away there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], and he cried out,

**24** "What do we have to do with you, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are: the Holy One \[G-HAGIOS\] of God."

**25** Jesus rebuked him: "Be silent and come out of him."

**26** The unclean spirit threw him into convulsions, cried out in a loud voice and came out of him.

**27** They were all amazed, so that they asked one another, "What is this? A new teaching, with authority. He even gives orders to the unclean spirits, and they obey him."

**28** His reputation spread at once everywhere, through the whole region around Galilee.

**29** And straight away, when they left the synagogue, they went into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.

**30** Simon's mother-in-law was lying in bed with a fever, and straight away they tell him about her.

**31** He came, took her by the hand and raised her up. The fever left her, and she began to serve them.

**32** When evening came, after the sun had set, they brought him all who were sick and those possessed by demons.

**33** The whole town was gathered at the door.

**34** He healed many who were sick with various diseases and drove out many demons, and he would not let the demons speak, because they knew him.

**35** Very early in the morning, while it was still dark, he got up, went out and went away to a deserted place, and there he prayed.

**36** Simon and those with him hunted for him,

**37** and they found him and say to him, "Everyone is looking for you."

**38** He says to them, "Let us go somewhere else, to the neighbouring towns, so that I can announce the message there also. That is why I came out."

**39** He went through the whole of Galilee, announcing in their synagogues and driving out demons.

**40** A man with a skin disease comes to him, begging him and kneeling, and saying, "If you want to, you can cleanse me."

**41** Moved with compassion \[G-SPLANCHNIZOMAI\], he reached out his hand, touched him and says to him, "I want to. Be cleansed."

**42** Straight away the skin disease left him, and he was cleansed.

**43** Sternly warning him, he sent him away at once

**44** and said to him, "See that you say nothing to anyone. But go, show yourself to the priest and offer for your cleansing what Moses commanded, as evidence for them."

**45** But he went out and began to proclaim it freely and to spread the word, so that Jesus could no longer enter a town openly but stayed outside in deserted places, and people came to him from everywhere.

### Chapter 2

**1** When he came back to Capernaum after some days, it was heard that he was at home.

**2** Many gathered, so that there was no more room, not even near the door, and he spoke the word to them.

**3** They come bringing a paralysed man to him, carried by four.

**4** Since they could not get near him because of the crowd, they removed the roof above him, dug through it, and lowered the mat on which the paralysed man was lying.

**5** When Jesus saw their faith \[G-PISTIS\], he says to the paralysed man, "Child, your sins are forgiven."

**6** Some of the scribes were sitting there, thinking in their hearts,

**7** "Why does this man speak like this? He is blaspheming. Who can forgive sins except God alone?"

**8** Straight away Jesus knew in his spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] that they were thinking this among themselves and said to them, "Why are you thinking these things in your hearts?

**9** Which is easier, to say to the paralysed man, 'Your sins are forgiven,' or to say, 'Get up, pick up your mat and walk'?

**10** But so that you may know that the Son of Man \[G-HUIOS-ANTHROPOU\] has authority on earth to forgive sins" (he says to the paralysed man)

**11** "I tell you, get up, pick up your mat and go to your house."

**12** He got up, and straight away picked up the mat and went out in front of them all, so that they were all astonished and glorified God, saying, "We have never seen anything like this."

**13** He went out again beside the sea. The whole crowd came to him, and he taught them.

**14** As he passed on, he saw Levi the son of Alphaeus sitting at the tax booth and says to him, "Follow me." And he got up and followed him.

**15** And while he was reclining at table in his house, many tax collectors and sinners were reclining with Jesus and his disciples, for there were many of them who followed him.

**16** When the scribes of the Pharisees saw that he was eating with sinners and tax collectors, they said to his disciples, "Why does he eat with tax collectors and sinners?"

**17** Jesus heard it and says to them, "Healthy people have no need of a doctor, but those who are ill. I did not come to call the righteous but sinners."

**18** John's disciples and the Pharisees were fasting. They come and say to him, "Why do John's disciples and the disciples of the Pharisees fast, but your disciples do not fast?"

**19** Jesus said to them, "Can the wedding guests fast while the bridegroom is with them? As long as they have the bridegroom with them, they cannot fast.

**20** But days will come when the bridegroom is taken from them, and then, on that day, they will fast.

**21** No one sews a patch of unshrunk cloth on an old garment. Otherwise the new piece pulls away from it, the new from the old, and the tear becomes worse.

**22** And no one pours new wine into old wineskins. Otherwise the wine will burst the skins, and the wine is lost, and the skins as well. But new wine goes into fresh skins."

**23** On the Sabbath \[G-SABBATON\] he was going through the grainfields, and his disciples began to make their way by picking heads of grain.

**24** The Pharisees said to him, "Look, why are they doing on the Sabbath what is not permitted?"

**25** He says to them, "Have you never read what David did when he was in need and was hungry, he and those with him:

**26** how he went into the house of God in the time of Abiathar the high priest and ate the loaves of the presence, which it is not permitted for anyone to eat except the priests, and gave some also to those with him?"

**27** And he said to them, "The Sabbath was made for people, and not people for the Sabbath.

**28** So the Son of Man \[G-HUIOS-ANTHROPOU\] is lord even of the Sabbath."

### Chapter 3

**1** Again he went into the synagogue, and there was a man there with a withered hand.

**2** They were watching him closely to see whether he would heal him on the Sabbath \[G-SABBATON\], so that they could accuse him.

**3** He says to the man with the withered hand, "Get up and stand in the middle."

**4** He says to them, "Is it permitted on the Sabbath to do good or to do harm, to save \[G-SOZO\] a life \[G-PSYCHE\] or to kill?" But they were silent.

**5** He looked around at them with anger, deeply grieved at the hardness of their hearts, and says to the man, "Stretch out your hand." He stretched it out, and his hand was restored.

**6** The Pharisees went out and at once began plotting with the Herodians against him, how to destroy him.

**7** Jesus withdrew with his disciples to the sea, and a large crowd from Galilee followed. Also from Judea,

**8** from Jerusalem, from Idumea, from beyond the Jordan, and from around Tyre and Sidon, a large crowd, hearing what he was doing, came to him.

**9** He told his disciples to have a small boat ready for him because of the crowd, so that they would not crush him.

**10** For he had healed many, so that all who had afflictions pushed forward to touch him.

**11** And the unclean spirits \[G-PNEUMA\], whenever they saw him, fell down before him and cried out, "You are the Son of God."

**12** And he sternly ordered them not to make him known.

**13** He goes up the mountain and calls to him those he wanted, and they came to him.

**14** He appointed twelve, whom he also named apostles, to be with him and to be sent out to announce

**15** and to have authority to drive out demons.

**16** He appointed the twelve: Simon, to whom he gave the name Peter;

**17** James son of Zebedee and John the brother of James, to whom he gave the name Boanerges, that is, Sons of Thunder;

**18** Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot

**19** and Judas Iscariot, who handed him over.

**20** He goes home, and the crowd gathers again, so that they could not even eat bread.

**21** When his family heard, they went out to seize him, for they said, "He is out of his mind."

**22** The scribes who had come down from Jerusalem said, "He has Beelzebul," and, "By the ruler of the demons he drives out the demons."

**23** He called them to him and spoke to them in parables: "How can Satan drive out Satan?

**24** If a kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.

**25** And if a house is divided against itself, that house will not be able to stand.

**26** And if Satan has risen against himself and is divided, he cannot stand, but is finished.

**27** But no one can enter the house of a strong man and plunder his goods unless he first ties up the strong man. Then he can plunder his house.

**28** Amen, I tell you, all sins will be forgiven the sons of people, and whatever blasphemies they blaspheme,

**29** but whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] never has forgiveness, but is guilty of an eternal sin."

**30** For they were saying, "He has an unclean spirit \[G-PNEUMA\]."

**31** His mother and his brothers come, and standing outside they sent to him, calling him.

**32** A crowd was sitting around him, and they say to him, "Look, your mother and your brothers and your sisters are outside, looking for you."

**33** He answered them, "Who are my mother and my brothers?"

**34** Looking at those sitting in a circle around him, he says, "Here are my mother and my brothers.

**35** Whoever does the will of God, this is my brother and sister and mother."

*Review note: Mark 1 to 3 were drafted in WABV wording, from the Greek, from the Gospel voice sheet (Part 40 of the review package). They are the beginning of the good news, John the baptiser, the baptism and the testing of Jesus (1:1-13), the proclamation in Galilee and the call of the first four disciples (1:14-20), the day in Capernaum, with the man in the synagogue, the healings and the skin disease cleansed (1:21-45), the paralysed man, the call of Levi, the questions on fasting and the Sabbath controversy (2:1-28), the withered hand, the crowds, the appointing of the twelve, the charge about Beelzebul and the true family of Jesus (3:1-35). The numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text keeps the features of Mark's Greek: the short sentences joined by "and", the word *euthys*, which is rendered "straight away" or "at once" (it is used 1:10, 1:12, 1:18, 1:20, 1:21, 1:23, 1:28, 1:29, 1:30, 1:42, 1:43, 2:8, 2:12 and 3:6), and the historic present (1:12, 1:21, 1:30, 1:37, 1:38, 1:40, 1:41, 2:3, 2:5, 2:14, 2:17, 2:18, 2:25, 3:3, 3:4, 3:5, 3:13, 3:20, 3:31, 3:32 and 3:34). No new G- marker is used in this part, and the markers are G-EUANGELION, G-CHRISTOS, G-METANOIA, G-PNEUMA, G-AGAPE, G-BASILEIA, G-SABBATON, G-HAGIOS, G-SPLANCHNIZOMAI, G-PISTIS, G-HUIOS-ANTHROPOU, G-SOZO and G-PSYCHE. In 1:1 some early manuscripts omit "the Son of God", and the footnote records the matter. In 1:2-3 the quotation is attributed to Isaiah, and it joins Malachi 3:1 and Exodus 23:20 with Isaiah 40:3. In 1:41 some early manuscripts have "angry" in place of "moved with compassion", and the footnote records the matter, and the WABV text follows the reading that most manuscripts have and notes the other. In 1:24 "the Holy One of God" translates *ho hagios tou theou*. In 2:26 "in the time of Abiathar the high priest" is the Greek of the text, and the footnote records that 1 Samuel 21:1-6 names Ahimelech. In 3:21 "his family" translates *hoi par' autou*, which may also be read "his friends" or "his people", and the footnote records the matter. In 3:28 "the sons of people" is literal, as the Greek has it. In 3:29 "an eternal sin" translates *aioniou hamartematos*, and some manuscripts read "eternal judgement". The words "brothers" in 3:31-35 are literal, and the WABV text does not decide who they were. There are no departures from the critical Greek text in chapters 1 to 3. The Greek is hard in some places, including 1:2-3, 1:41, 2:26, 3:17 (*Boanerges*), 3:21 and 3:29. The footnotes record the main differences and the uncertain terms. The words on the unclean spirit in the synagogue (1:23-26), the family who thought he was out of his mind (3:21) and the sin that is not forgiven (3:28-30) are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. The section needs a check against the Greek by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked.*

## Part Two: Chapters 4 to 6, the parables, the mighty acts and the mission

### Chapter 4

**1** Again he began to teach beside the sea. A very large crowd gathers to him, so that he got into a boat and sat in it on the sea, and the whole crowd was on the land beside the sea.

**2** He taught them many things in parables, and in his teaching he said to them,

**3** "Listen. Look, the sower went out to sow.

**4** As he sowed, some seed fell beside the path, and the birds came and ate it up.

**5** Other seed fell on rocky ground where it did not have much soil, and straight away it sprang up, because it had no depth of soil.

**6** When the sun rose it was scorched, and because it had no root it withered.

**7** Other seed fell among the thorns, and the thorns came up and choked it, and it gave no fruit.

**8** Other seed fell into good soil and gave fruit, growing up and increasing, and bore thirty, sixty and a hundred times."

**9** And he said, "Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear."

**10** When he was alone, those around him with the twelve asked him about the parables.

**11** He said to them, "To you has been given the secret of the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of God, but to those outside everything comes in parables,

**12** so that 'looking they may look and not see, and hearing they may hear and not understand, so that they do not turn and be forgiven.'"

**13** He says to them, "Do you not understand this parable? How then will you understand all the parables?

**14** The sower sows the word.

**15** These are the ones beside the path where the word is sown: when they hear, Satan comes straight away and takes away the word that was sown in them.

**16** And these are the ones sown on rocky ground: when they hear the word, they straight away receive it with joy,

**17** but they have no root in themselves and last only a short time. Then when trouble or hounding comes because of the word, they straight away trip up.

**18** Others are those sown among thorns. These are the ones who hear the word,

**19** but the anxieties of the age, the deceit of riches and the desires for other things come in and choke the word, and it becomes fruitless.

**20** And those sown on the good soil are the ones who hear the word and welcome it and bear fruit, thirty, sixty and a hundred times."

**21** He said to them, "Is the lamp brought in to be put under a basket or under a bed, and not to be put on a stand?

**22** For there is nothing hidden except to be made known, and nothing has become secret except to come into the open.

**23** If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear."

**24** He said to them, "Pay attention to what you hear. With the measure you use, it will be measured to you, and more will be added to you.

**25** For to the one who has, more will be given, and from the one who has not, even what he has will be taken away."

**26** He said, "The kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of God is like a man who scatters seed on the ground

**27** and sleeps and rises night and day, and the seed sprouts and grows, he does not know how.

**28** The earth produces by itself, first the blade, then the ear, then the full grain in the ear.

**29** But when the fruit is ready, he sends out the sickle at once, because the harvest has come."

**30** He said, "How shall we compare the kingdom of God, or what parable shall we use for it?

**31** It is like a mustard seed, which, when it is sown on the ground, is the smallest of all the seeds on earth,

**32** yet when it is sown it grows up and becomes the largest of all garden plants and puts out large branches, so that the birds of the sky can nest in its shade."

**33** With many such parables he spoke the word to them, as they were able to hear it.

**34** He did not speak to them without a parable, but privately to his own disciples he explained everything.

**35** On that day, when evening had come, he says to them, "Let us cross over to the other side."

**36** They left the crowd and take him with them in the boat, just as he was. Other boats were with him.

**37** A great windstorm arises, and the waves were breaking into the boat, so that the boat was already filling.

**38** He was in the stern, asleep on the cushion. They wake him and say to him, "Teacher, do you not care that we are perishing?"

**39** He woke up, rebuked the wind and said to the sea, "Quiet. Be still." The wind dropped, and there was a great calm.

**40** He said to them, "Why are you afraid? Do you still not have faith \[G-PISTIS\]?"

**41** They were filled with great fear and said to one another, "Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"

### Chapter 5

**1** They came to the other side of the sea, to the region of the Gerasenes.

**2** When he got out of the boat, straight away a man with an unclean spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] met him from the tombs.

**3** He lived among the tombs, and no one could bind him any more, not even with a chain,

**4** because he had often been bound with shackles and chains, and he had torn the chains apart and broken the shackles, and no one had the strength to subdue him.

**5** Night and day among the tombs and in the mountains he was always crying out and cutting himself with stones.

**6** Seeing Jesus from a distance, he ran and bowed down before him,

**7** and crying out in a loud voice he says, "What do I have to do with you, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I put you under oath by God: do not torment me."

**8** For he had been saying to him, "Come out of the man, you unclean spirit."

**9** And he asked him, "What is your name?" He says to him, "My name is Legion, for we are many."

**10** He begged him earnestly not to send them out of the region.

**11** Now there was a large herd of pigs feeding there on the hillside.

**12** They begged him, "Send us into the pigs, so that we may enter them."

**13** He gave them permission. The unclean spirits came out and entered the pigs, and the herd, about two thousand, rushed down the steep bank into the sea and drowned in the sea.

**14** Those who tended them fled and reported it in the town and in the countryside, and people came to see what had happened.

**15** They come to Jesus and see the man who had been possessed by demons, sitting, clothed and in his right mind, the man who had had the legion, and they were afraid.

**16** Those who had seen it described to them what had happened to the demon-possessed man and to the pigs.

**17** And they began to beg him to leave their region.

**18** As he was getting into the boat, the man who had been possessed begged him to be with him.

**19** But he did not let him, and says to him, "Go home to your own people and tell them how much the Lord has done for you, and how he has had mercy \[G-ELEOS\] on you."

**20** So he went away and began to proclaim in the Decapolis how much Jesus had done for him, and everyone was amazed.

**21** When Jesus had crossed again in the boat to the other side, a large crowd gathered to him, and he was beside the sea.

**22** One of the synagogue leaders, named Jairus, comes, and seeing him falls at his feet

**23** and begs him earnestly, "My little daughter is at the point of death. Come and lay your hands on her, so that she may be saved \[G-SOZO\] and live."

**24** He went with him, and a large crowd followed and pressed in on him.

**25** There was a woman who had had a flow of blood for twelve years,

**26** and had suffered much under many doctors and had spent all she had, and was no better but rather worse.

**27** She had heard about Jesus, and she came up behind him in the crowd and touched his cloak,

**28** for she said, "If I touch even his clothes, I will be saved \[G-SOZO\]."

**29** Straight away her flow of blood dried up, and she knew in her body that she was healed of her affliction.

**30** And straight away Jesus, aware in himself that power had gone out from him, turned around in the crowd and said, "Who touched my clothes?"

**31** His disciples said to him, "You see the crowd pressing in on you, and you say, 'Who touched me?'"

**32** He looked around to see who had done it.

**33** The woman, afraid and trembling, knowing what had happened to her, came and fell down before him and told him the whole truth.

**34** He said to her, "Daughter, your faith \[G-PISTIS\] has saved \[G-SOZO\] you. Go in peace \[G-EIRENE\] and be healed of your affliction."

**35** While he was still speaking, they come from the synagogue leader's house and say, "Your daughter has died. Why trouble the teacher any further?"

**36** Overhearing what was said, Jesus says to the synagogue leader, "Do not be afraid, only believe."

**37** He did not let anyone follow him except Peter, James and John the brother of James.

**38** They come to the house of the synagogue leader, and he sees a commotion, and people weeping and wailing loudly.

**39** He went in and says to them, "Why are you making a commotion and weeping? The child is not dead but asleep."

**40** And they laughed at him. But he put them all out, took the child's father and mother and those with him, and goes in where the child was.

**41** Taking the child by the hand he says to her, "Talitha koum," which means, "Little girl, I tell you, get up."

**42** Straight away the girl got up and began to walk about, for she was twelve years old. And they were immediately overcome with great amazement.

**43** He gave strict orders that no one should know this, and told them to give her something to eat.

### Chapter 6

**1** He went out from there and comes to his home town, and his disciples follow him.

**2** When the Sabbath \[G-SABBATON\] came he began to teach in the synagogue, and many who heard were astonished and said, "Where did this man get these things? What is this wisdom given to him, and such mighty acts done through his hands?

**3** Is this not the carpenter, the son of Mary and brother of James and Joses and Judas and Simon? And are his sisters not here with us?" And they were offended at him.

**4** Jesus said to them, "A prophet is not without honour except in his home town, among his relatives and in his own house."

**5** And he could do no mighty act there, except that he laid his hands on a few sick people and healed them.

**6** And he was amazed at their unbelief. He went around the villages teaching.

**7** He calls the twelve to him and began to send them out two by two, and gave them authority over the unclean spirits \[G-PNEUMA\].

**8** He instructed them to take nothing for the road except a staff: no bread, no bag, no copper coins in their belts,

**9** but to wear sandals and not to put on two tunics.

**10** He said to them, "Wherever you enter a house, stay there until you leave from there.

**11** And if any place does not receive you and they do not listen to you, as you leave from there shake off the dust under your feet as a testimony against them."

**12** So they went out and announced that people should repent \[G-METANOIA\].

**13** They drove out many demons and anointed many sick people with oil and healed them.

**14** King Herod heard of it, for his name had become known. People were saying, "John the baptiser has been raised from the dead, and that is why these powers are at work in him."

**15** But others said, "He is Elijah." And others said, "He is a prophet, like one of the prophets of old."

**16** But when Herod heard it, he said, "John, whom I beheaded, has been raised."

**35** James and John, the sons of Zebedee, come to him and say to him, "Teacher, we want you to do for us whatever we ask of you."

**36** He said to them, "What do you want me to do for you?"

**37** They said to him, "Grant us to sit, one at your right and one at your left, in your glory."

**38** Jesus said to them, "You do not know what you are asking. Can you drink the cup that I drink, or be baptised with the baptism that I am baptised with?"

**40** but to sit at my right or at my left is not mine to give, but is for those for whom it has been prepared."

**41** When the ten heard this, they began to be angry with James and John.

**42** Jesus called them to him and says to them, "You know that those who are regarded as rulers of the nations lord it over them, and their great ones exercise authority over them.

**43** But it is not so among you. Whoever wants to become great among you will be your servant,

**45** For the Son of Man \[G-HUIOS-ANTHROPOU\] also did not come to be served but to serve and to give his life \[G-PSYCHE\] as a ransom \[G-LYTRON\] for many."

**47** When he heard that it was Jesus of Nazareth, he began to cry out and say, "Son of David, Jesus, have mercy \[G-ELEOS\] on me."

**49** Jesus stopped and said, "Call him." They call the blind man and say to him, "Take courage. Get up. He is calling you."

**51** Jesus answered him, "What do you want me to do for you?" The blind man said to him, "Rabbouni, let me see again."

**52** Jesus said to him, "Go. Your faith \[G-PISTIS\] has saved \[G-SOZO\] you." Straight away he saw again and followed him on the way.

### Chapter 11

**1** When they near Jerusalem, at Bethphage and Bethany, near the Mount of Olives, he sends two of his disciples

**2** and says to them, "Go into the village opposite you, and as soon as you enter it you will find a colt tied, on which no one has yet sat. Untie it and bring it.

**3** If anyone says to you, 'Why are you doing this?' say, 'The Lord needs it and will send it back here at once.'"

**4** They went away and found a colt tied at a door outside in the street, and they untie it.

**5** Some of those standing there said to them, "What are you doing, untying the colt?"

**6** They told them what Jesus had said, and they let them go.

**7** They bring the colt to Jesus and put their cloaks on it, and he sat on it.

**8** Many spread their cloaks on the road, and others spread branches cut from the fields.

**10** Blessed is the coming kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of our father David. Hosanna in the highest."

**12** The next day, when they had left Bethany, he was hungry.

**13** Seeing in the distance a fig tree in leaf, he went to see whether he could find anything on it. When he came to it, he found nothing but leaves, for it was not the season for figs.

**23** Amen, I tell you, whoever says to this mountain, 'Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,' and does not doubt in his heart but believes that what he says will happen, it will be done for him.

**24** So I tell you, whatever you pray and ask for, believe that you have received it, and it will be yours.

### Chapter 12

**3** They took him, beat him and sent him away empty-handed.

**4** Again he sent them another slave, and they struck him on the head and treated him shamefully.

**5** And he sent another, and that one they killed, and many others, beating some and killing some.

**6** He still had one, a beloved \[G-AGAPE\] son. He sent him to them last of all, saying, 'They will respect my son.'

**7** But those tenants said to one another, 'This is the heir. Come, let us kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.'

**8** They took him and killed him and threw him out of the vineyard.

**9** What will the owner of the vineyard do? He will come and destroy the tenants and give the vineyard to others.

**11** This was the Lord's doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes'?"

**12** They were seeking to seize him, but they feared the crowd, for they knew that he had told the parable against them. So they left him and went away.

**13** They send to him some of the Pharisees and the Herodians to trap him in his words.

**14** They come and say to him, "Teacher, we know that you are truthful and do not care about anyone, for you do not look at people's status but teach the way of God in truth. Is it permitted to pay the poll tax to Caesar or not? Should we pay or not pay?"

**15** But knowing their hypocrisy, he said to them, "Why do you test me? Bring me a denarius to look at."

**16** They brought one. He says to them, "Whose image and inscription is this?" They said to him, "Caesar's."

**17** Jesus said to them, "Give to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's." And they were amazed at him.

**18** Sadducees come to him, who say there is no resurrection, and they asked him,

**19** "Teacher, Moses wrote for us that if a man's brother dies and leaves a wife but no child, the man must take the wife and raise up offspring for his brother.

**20** There were seven brothers. The first took a wife, and when he died left no offspring.

**21** The second took her and died, leaving no offspring, and the third likewise.

**27** He is not the God of the dead but of the living. You are very wrong."

**28** One of the scribes came up and heard them disputing, and seeing that he had answered them well, asked him, "Which commandment is the first of all?"

**29** Jesus answered, "The first is: 'Hear, O Israel, the Lord our God, the Lord is one,

**30** and you will love \[G-AGAPE\] the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul \[G-PSYCHE\] and with all your mind and with all your strength.'

**31** The second is this: 'You will love \[G-AGAPE\] your neighbour as yourself.' There is no other commandment greater than these."

**32** The scribe said to him, "Well said, Teacher. You have truly said that he is one, and there is no other besides him,

**34** When Jesus saw that he had answered wisely, he said to him, "You are not far from the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of God." And no one dared to ask him any more questions.

**36** David himself said by the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], 'The Lord said to my Lord: Sit at my right hand until I put your enemies under your feet.'

**37** David himself calls him Lord. So how is he his son?" And the large crowd heard him gladly.

**38** In his teaching he said, "Beware of the scribes, who like to walk around in long robes and to have greetings in the marketplaces

**40** who devour widows' houses and for a show make long prayers. These will receive greater condemnation."

**41** He sat down opposite the treasury and watched how the crowd put money into the treasury. Many rich people put in large sums.

**42** A poor widow came and put in two small copper coins, which make a penny.

## Part Five: Chapters 13 and 14, the teaching on the end and the passion

### Chapter 13

**1** As he was going out of the temple, one of his disciples says to him, "Teacher, look, what stones and what buildings."

**2** Jesus said to him, "Do you see these great buildings? Not one stone will be left here upon another that will not be thrown down."

**3** As he was sitting on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter, James, John and Andrew asked him privately,

**4** "Tell us, when will these things be, and what will be the sign when all these things are about to be accomplished?"

**5** Jesus began to say to them, "Watch out that no one deceives you.

**6** Many will come in my name, saying, 'I am he,' and they will deceive many.

**7** When you hear of wars and rumours of wars, do not be alarmed. These things must happen, but the end is not yet.

**8** For nation will rise against nation and kingdom against kingdom. There will be earthquakes in various places. There will be famines. These are the beginning of birth pains.

**9** But watch yourselves. They will hand you over to councils, and you will be beaten in synagogues, and you will stand before governors and kings for my sake, as a testimony to them.

**10** And the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] must first be proclaimed to all the nations.

**11** When they bring you to trial and hand you over, do not be anxious beforehand about what you will say, but say whatever is given you in that hour, for it is not you who speak but the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

**12** Brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his child, and children will rise against parents and have them put to death.

**13** You will be hated by all because of my name. But the one who endures to the end will be saved \[G-SOZO\].

**14** But when you see the abomination of desolation standing where it should not (let the reader understand), then let those in Judea flee to the mountains.

**15** Let the one on the housetop not go down or enter to take anything out of his house,

**16** and let the one in the field not turn back to take his cloak.

**17** Woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing in those days.

**18** Pray that it may not happen in winter.

**19** For those days will be a tribulation such as has not been from the beginning of the creation that God created until now, and never will be.

**20** And if the Lord had not cut short the days, no flesh would be saved \[G-SOZO\]. But for the sake of the chosen, whom he chose, he cut short the days.

**21** And then if anyone says to you, 'Look, here is the Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\],' or 'Look, there he is,' do not believe it.

**22** For false christs and false prophets will arise and perform signs and wonders, to lead astray, if possible, the chosen.

**23** But you, watch out. I have told you everything beforehand.

**24** But in those days, after that tribulation, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light,

**25** and the stars will be falling from heaven, and the powers in the heavens will be shaken.

**26** And then they will see the Son of Man \[G-HUIOS-ANTHROPOU\] coming in clouds with great power and glory.

**27** And then he will send out the angels and gather his chosen from the four winds, from the end of the earth to the end of heaven.

**28** From the fig tree learn its lesson: as soon as its branch becomes tender and puts out its leaves, you know that summer is near.

**29** So also, when you see these things happening, know that he is near, at the doors.

**30** Amen, I tell you, this generation will not pass away until all these things have happened.

**31** Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away.

**32** But concerning that day or that hour, no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but only the Father.

**33** Watch out, keep awake, for you do not know when the time will come.

**34** It is like a man going on a journey, who left his house and gave authority to his slaves, each with his work, and commanded the doorkeeper to keep watch.

**35** So keep watch, for you do not know when the master of the house is coming, in the evening, or at midnight, or at cockcrow, or in the morning,

**36** lest he come suddenly and find you asleep.

**37** And what I say to you I say to all: Keep watch."

### Chapter 14

**1** It was two days before the Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread. The chief priests and the scribes were seeking how to seize him by stealth and kill him,

**2** for they said, "Not during the feast, lest there be an uproar of the people."

**3** While he was in Bethany at the house of Simon the man with the skin disease, as he was reclining at table, a woman came with an alabaster jar of ointment, pure nard, very costly. She broke the jar and poured it over his head.

**4** There were some who said to one another indignantly, "Why has the ointment been wasted like this?

**5** This ointment could have been sold for more than three hundred denarii and the money given to the poor." And they scolded her.

**6** But Jesus said, "Leave her alone. Why do you trouble her? She has done a good thing for me.

**7** For you always have the poor with you, and whenever you want you can do good for them, but you do not always have me.

**8** She has done what she could. She has anointed my body beforehand for burial.

**9** Amen, I tell you, wherever the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] is proclaimed in the whole world, what she has done will also be told in memory of her."

**10** Judas Iscariot, who was one of the twelve, went to the chief priests to hand him over to them.

**11** When they heard it, they were glad and promised to give him money. And he began looking for an opportunity to hand him over.

**12** On the first day of Unleavened Bread, when they sacrificed the Passover lamb, his disciples say to him, "Where do you want us to go and prepare for you to eat the Passover?"

**13** He sends two of his disciples and says to them, "Go into the city, and a man carrying a jar of water will meet you. Follow him,

**14** and wherever he enters, say to the master of the house, 'The teacher says: Where is my guest room, where I may eat the Passover with my disciples?'

**15** He will show you a large upper room furnished and ready. Prepare for us there."

**17** When it was evening he comes with the twelve.

**18** While they were reclining at table and eating, Jesus said, "Amen, I tell you, one of you will hand me over, one who is eating with me."

**20** He said to them, "It is one of the twelve, one who is dipping bread into the same dish with me.

**21** For the Son of Man \[G-HUIOS-ANTHROPOU\] goes as it is written of him, but woe to that man by whom the Son of Man is handed over. It would have been better for that man if he had not been born."

**22** While they were eating, he took bread, and after blessing it he broke it and gave it to them and said, "Take. This is my body."

**23** He took a cup, and after giving thanks he gave it to them, and they all drank from it.

**24** He said to them, "This is my blood of the covenant \[G-DIATHEKE\], which is poured out for many.

**25** Amen, I tell you, I will not drink again of the fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of God."

**26** After singing the hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.

**27** Jesus says to them, "You will all trip up, for it is written, 'I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered.'

**28** But after I am raised, I will go ahead of you to Galilee."

**29** Peter said to him, "Even if all trip up, I will not."

**30** Jesus says to him, "Amen, I tell you, this very night, before a rooster crows twice, you will deny me three times."

**31** But he said emphatically, "If I must die with you, I will never deny you." And they all said the same.

**32** They come to a place called Gethsemane, and he says to his disciples, "Sit here while I pray."

**33** He takes with him Peter and James and John, and began to be greatly distressed and troubled.

**34** He says to them, "My soul \[G-PSYCHE\] is deeply grieved, to the point of death. Stay here and keep watch."

**35** Going a little further, he fell on the ground and prayed that, if it were possible, the hour might pass from him.

**36** He said, "Abba, Father, all things are possible for you. Take this cup from me. Yet not what I want, but what you want."

**37** He comes and finds them sleeping, and says to Peter, "Simon, are you asleep? Could you not keep watch for one hour?

**38** Keep watch and pray that you may not come into testing. The spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] is willing, but the flesh is weak."

**39** Again he went away and prayed, saying the same words.

**40** And again he came and found them sleeping, for their eyes were very heavy, and they did not know what to answer him.

**41** He comes the third time and says to them, "Are you still sleeping and resting? It is enough. The hour has come. Look, the Son of Man \[G-HUIOS-ANTHROPOU\] is being handed over into the hands of sinners.

**42** Get up, let us go. Look, the one who hands me over is near."

**43** Straight away, while he was still speaking, Judas, one of the twelve, comes, and with him a crowd with swords and clubs, from the chief priests and the scribes and the elders.

**44** The one who handed him over had given them a signal: "The one I kiss is the man. Seize him and lead him away under guard."

**45** When he came, he went straight up to him and says, "Rabbi," and kissed him.

**46** They laid hands on him and seized him.

**47** But one of those standing by drew his sword and struck the slave of the high priest and cut off his ear.

**48** Jesus said to them, "Have you come out with swords and clubs to arrest me as though I were a bandit?

**49** Day after day I was with you in the temple teaching, and you did not seize me. But let the scriptures be fulfilled."

**50** And they all left him and fled.

**51** A young man was following him, wearing nothing but a linen cloth over his naked body. They seize him,

**52** but he left the linen cloth and ran away naked.

**53** They led Jesus away to the high priest, and all the chief priests and the elders and the scribes come together.

**54** Peter had followed him at a distance, right into the courtyard of the high priest, and he was sitting with the guards, warming himself at the fire.

**55** The chief priests and the whole council were seeking testimony against Jesus to put him to death, but they found none.

**56** For many gave false testimony against him, but their testimony did not agree.

**57** And some stood up and gave false testimony against him, saying,

**58** "We heard him say, 'I will destroy this temple made with hands, and in three days I will build another, not made with hands.'"

**59** Yet even on this their testimony did not agree.

**60** The high priest stood up in the middle and asked Jesus, "Have you no answer? What is it that these men testify against you?"

**61** But he was silent and made no answer. Again the high priest asked him, "Are you the Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], the Son of the Blessed?"

**62** Jesus said, "I am, and you will see the Son of Man \[G-HUIOS-ANTHROPOU\] seated at the right hand of Power and coming with the clouds of heaven."

**63** The high priest tore his garments and says, "What further need do we have of witnesses?

**64** You have heard the blasphemy. What is your decision?" And they all condemned him as deserving death.

**65** Some began to spit on him and to cover his face and to strike him, saying to him, "Prophesy." And the guards received him with slaps.

**66** While Peter was below in the courtyard, one of the high priest's servant girls comes,

**67** and seeing Peter warming himself, she looked at him and says, "You also were with the Nazarene, Jesus."

**68** But he denied it, saying, "I neither know nor understand what you are saying." And he went out into the forecourt, and a rooster crowed.

**69** And the servant girl saw him and began again to say to those standing by, "This man is one of them."

**70** But again he denied it. And after a little while the bystanders again said to Peter, "Surely you are one of them, for you are a Galilean."

**71** But he began to curse and to swear, "I do not know this man of whom you speak."

**72** And straight away a rooster crowed a second time. And Peter remembered how Jesus had said to him, "Before a rooster crows twice, you will deny me three times." And he broke down and wept.
