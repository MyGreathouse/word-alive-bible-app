# WABV: Amos

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[H-RUACH\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Amos is the third of the twelve books of the Minor Prophets in the Protestant order that the WABV text follows. It is in Hebrew, mostly poetry, with prose in 7:10-17 and in parts of chapters 1, 7 and 8. The book is dated in 1:1 to the reigns of Uzziah king of Judah and Jeroboam son of Joash king of Israel, and to the earthquake, and the footnotes give the dates in the modern count. Amos is called one of the shepherds of Tekoa, and the footnotes record the word. The chapter and verse numbering is the English numbering, and in Amos it is the same as the Hebrew numbering. The book falls into four parts in the WABV text: the roar of the LORD and the oracles against the nations and Israel (chapters 1 and 2), the word against Israel (3 and 4), the laments and the woes (5 and 6) and the visions, the dispute with Amaziah and the promise (7 to 9). The headings of the parts are not in the Hebrew. The Hebrew of Amos is mostly clear, with a few verses that are very hard (for example 3:12, 4:2-3 and 5:26), and the footnotes record the other readings. The names of God are given as in the earlier books: "the LORD" for YHWH, "Lord" for Adonai, "the Lord GOD" for Adonai YHWH, and "the LORD, the God of hosts" for YHWH Elohei tseva'ot. The WABV text does not decide on questions of how the book grew, and the footnotes record only the language.*

## Part One: Chapters 1 and 2, the roar of the LORD and the oracles

### Chapter 1

**1** The words of Amos, who was among the sheep breeders of Tekoa, which he saw concerning Israel in the days of Uzziah king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.

**2** He said: The LORD roars from Zion and utters his voice from Jerusalem. The pastures of the shepherds mourn, and the top of Carmel dries up.

**3** Thus says the LORD: For three transgressions of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn it back \[H-SHUV\]. Because they threshed Gilead with threshing sledges of iron,

**4** I will send a fire on the house of Hazael, and it will devour the strongholds of Ben-hadad.

**5** I will break the gate bar of Damascus, and cut off the ruler from the Valley of Aven, and the one who holds the sceptre from Beth-eden. And the people of Aram will go into exile to Kir, says the LORD.

**6** Thus says the LORD: For three transgressions of Gaza, and for four, I will not turn it back \[H-SHUV\]. Because they carried into exile a whole people to hand them over to Edom,

**7** I will send a fire on the wall of Gaza, and it will devour her strongholds.

**8** I will cut off the ruler from Ashdod, and the one who holds the sceptre from Ashkelon. I will turn my hand \[H-SHUV\] against Ekron, and the remnant of the Philistines will perish, says the Lord GOD.

**9** Thus says the LORD: For three transgressions of Tyre, and for four, I will not turn it back \[H-SHUV\]. Because they handed over a whole people to Edom, and did not remember the covenant \[H-BERIT\] of brothers,

**10** I will send a fire on the wall of Tyre, and it will devour her strongholds.

**11** Thus says the LORD: For three transgressions of Edom, and for four, I will not turn it back \[H-SHUV\]. Because he pursued his brother with the sword and cast off all compassion, and his anger tore for ever, and he kept his wrath without end,

**12** I will send a fire on Teman, and it will devour the strongholds of Bozrah.

**13** Thus says the LORD: For three transgressions of the Ammonites, and for four, I will not turn it back \[H-SHUV\]. Because they ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead in order to enlarge their territory,

**14** I will kindle a fire on the wall of Rabbah, and it will devour her strongholds, with shouting on the day of battle, with a storm on the day of the whirlwind.

**15** And their king will go into exile, he and his officials together, says the LORD.

### Chapter 2

**1** Thus says the LORD: For three transgressions of Moab, and for four, I will not turn it back \[H-SHUV\]. Because he burned to lime the bones of the king of Edom,

**2** I will send a fire on Moab, and it will devour the strongholds of Kerioth, and Moab will die in uproar, with shouting and the sound of the trumpet.

**3** I will cut off the ruler from its midst, and kill all its officials with him, says the LORD.

**4** Thus says the LORD: For three transgressions of Judah, and for four, I will not turn it back \[H-SHUV\]. Because they have rejected the teaching \[H-TORAH\] of the LORD and have not kept his statutes, and their lies have led them astray, the lies after which their fathers walked,

**5** I will send a fire on Judah, and it will devour the strongholds of Jerusalem.

**6** Thus says the LORD: For three transgressions of Israel, and for four, I will not turn it back \[H-SHUV\]. Because they sell the righteous \[H-TSEDAQAH\] for silver and the needy for a pair of sandals,

**7** they trample the head of the poor into the dust of the earth and push the afflicted out of the way. A man and his father go in to the same girl, so that my holy \[H-QADOSH\] name is profaned.

**8** They stretch themselves beside every altar on garments taken in pledge, and in the house of their God they drink wine bought with fines.

**9** Yet it was I who destroyed the Amorite before them, whose height was like the height of cedars and who was as strong as oaks. I destroyed his fruit above and his roots beneath.

**10** It was I who brought you up out of the land of Egypt and led you forty years in the wilderness, to take possession of the land of the Amorite.

**11** And I raised up some of your sons as prophets and some of your young men as Nazirites \[H-NAZIR\]. Is it not so, people of Israel? declares the LORD.

**12** But you made the Nazirites \[H-NAZIR\] drink wine, and commanded the prophets, saying, 'You must not prophesy.'

**13** See, I will press you down in your place, as a cart presses down when it is full of sheaves.

**14** Flight will perish from the swift, and the strong will not retain his strength, nor will the mighty save his life \[H-NEPHESH\].

**15** The one who handles the bow will not stand, and the one who is swift of foot will not save himself, nor will the one who rides the horse save his life \[H-NEPHESH\].

**16** And the one who is stout of heart among the mighty will flee away naked in that day, declares the LORD.

The date in 1:1 is by the kings of Judah and of Israel and by the earthquake, and the footnote records the matter. In 1:3, 1:6, 1:8, 1:9, 1:11, 1:13, 2:1, 2:4 and 2:6 forms of shuv ("I will not turn it back", "I will turn my hand") are tagged H-SHUV. In 1:9 "covenant" is tagged H-BERIT. In 2:4 "teaching" is tagged H-TORAH. In 2:6 "the righteous" is tagged H-TSEDAQAH. In 2:7 "my holy name" is tagged H-QADOSH. In 2:11 and 2:12 "Nazirites" is tagged H-NAZIR. In 2:14 and 2:15 "his life" translates nafsho and is tagged H-NEPHESH. The words "all compassion" in 1:11 (*rachamav*) are not tagged. There are no departures from the Hebrew in chapters 1 and 2. ## Part Two: Chapters 3 and 4, the word against Israel

### Chapter 3

**1** Hear this word that the LORD has spoken against you, people of Israel, against the whole family that I brought up out of the land of Egypt:

**2** You only have I known of all the families of the earth. Therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.

**3** Do two walk together unless they have agreed to meet?

**4** Does a lion roar in the forest when it has no prey? Does a young lion cry out from its den if it has caught nothing?

**5** Does a bird fall in a trap on the ground when there is no bait for it? Does a trap spring up from the ground when it has taken nothing?

**6** If a trumpet is blown in a city, will the people not tremble? If disaster falls on a city, has not the LORD done it?

**7** For the Lord GOD does nothing without revealing his secret to his servants the prophets.

**8** The lion has roared. Who will not fear? The Lord GOD has spoken. Who can but prophesy?

**9** Proclaim to the strongholds in Ashdod and to the strongholds in the land of Egypt, and say, "Assemble on the mountains of Samaria, and see the great tumults within her, and the oppressed in her midst."

**10** They do not know how to do what is right, declares the LORD, those who store up violence and robbery in their strongholds.

**11** Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: An enemy will surround the land and strip you of your defence, and your strongholds will be plundered.

**12** Thus says the LORD: As the shepherd rescues from the mouth of the lion two legs or a piece of an ear, so will the people of Israel who live in Samaria be rescued, with the corner of a couch and part of a bed.

**13** Hear, and testify against the house of Jacob, declares the Lord GOD, the God of hosts:

**14** On the day I punish Israel for his transgressions, I will punish the altars of Bethel, and the horns of the altar will be cut off and fall to the ground.

**15** I will strike the winter house along with the summer house, and the houses of ivory will perish, and the great houses will come to an end, declares the LORD.

### Chapter 4

**1** Hear this word, you cows of Bashan who are on the hill of Samaria, who oppress the poor, who crush the needy, who say to your husbands, "Bring, that we may drink."

**2** The Lord GOD has sworn by his holiness \[H-QADOSH\]: See, the days are coming on you when they will take you away with hooks, even the last of you with fishhooks.

**3** And you will go out through the breaches, each one straight ahead, and you will be cast out toward Harmon, declares the LORD.

**4** Come to Bethel, and transgress. To Gilgal, and multiply transgression. Bring your sacrifices every morning, your tithes every three days,

**5** offer a thank offering of leavened bread, and proclaim freewill offerings, announce them, for so you love \[H-AHAV\] to do, people of Israel, declares the Lord GOD.

**6** I gave you cleanness of teeth in all your cities, and lack of bread in all your places, yet you did not return \[H-SHUV\] to me, declares the LORD.

**7** I also withheld the rain from you when there were still three months to the harvest. I would send rain on one city, and send no rain on another city. One field would have rain, and the field on which it did not rain would dry up.

**8** So two or three cities would wander to one city to drink water, and would not be satisfied, yet you did not return \[H-SHUV\] to me, declares the LORD.

**9** I struck you with blight and mildew. Your many gardens and your vineyards, your fig trees and your olive trees the locust devoured, yet you did not return \[H-SHUV\] to me, declares the LORD.

**10** I sent among you a plague like that of Egypt. I killed your young men with the sword, and carried away your horses, and I made the stench of your camp go up into your nostrils, yet you did not return \[H-SHUV\] to me, declares the LORD.

**11** I overthrew some of you, as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and you were like a brand snatched from the burning, yet you did not return \[H-SHUV\] to me, declares the LORD.

**12** Therefore thus I will do to you, Israel. Because I will do this to you, prepare to meet your God, Israel.

**13** For see, the one who forms the mountains and creates the wind \[H-RUACH\], and declares to humankind what is his thought, who makes the dawn into darkness and treads on the heights of the earth, the LORD, the God of hosts, is his name.

*Review note: Amos 3 and 4 were drafted in WABV wording. They are the word against the whole family brought up from Egypt and the questions of cause and effect (3:1-8), the call to the strongholds and the end of the altars and the houses (3:9-15), and the cows of Bashan, the false worship at Bethel and Gilgal, the five refusals to return and the call to prepare to meet God (4:1-13). The numbering is the same as the Hebrew. The whole of the part is in poetry, and the verses are set in lines. In 4:2 "his holiness" translates beqodsho and is tagged H-QADOSH. In 4:5 "you love" translates ahavtem and is tagged H-AHAV. In 4:6, 4:8, 4:9, 4:10 and 4:11 "you did not return" translates lo shavtem and is tagged H-SHUV each time. In 4:13 "the wind" translates ruach and is tagged H-RUACH. The words of 4:13 ("the one who forms the mountains") are the first of three songs of praise in the book (see also 5:8-9 and 9:5-6) and are given the same wording where the Hebrew repeats the phrases. There are no departures from the Hebrew in chapters 3 and 4. The Hebrew is hard in some places, including 3:4, 3:9, 3:12 (the saving of the legs and the ear, and the couch), 4:1 (the cows of Bashan), 4:2-3 (the hooks and *Harmon*) and 4:4-5. The footnotes record the main differences and the uncertain terms. The words on the oppression of the poor by the women of Samaria (4:1) and the plagues and the overthrow like Sodom (4:6-11) are rendered as written, with no comment. The section needs a check against the Hebrew by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked.*

## Part Three: Chapters 5 and 6, the lament and the woe

### Chapter 5

**1** Hear this word that I take up over you in lamentation, house of Israel:

**2** Fallen, no more to rise, is the young woman Israel. Forsaken on her land, with none to raise her up.

**3** For thus says the Lord GOD: The city that went out a thousand will have a hundred left, and that which went out a hundred will have ten left to the house of Israel.

**4** For thus says the LORD to the house of Israel: Seek me and live.

**5** But do not seek Bethel, and do not enter Gilgal or cross over to Beersheba. For Gilgal will surely go into exile, and Bethel will come to nothing.

**6** Seek the LORD and live, lest he break out like fire in the house of Joseph, and it devour with none to quench it for Bethel,

**7** you who turn justice to wormwood and cast righteousness \[H-TSEDAQAH\] to the ground.

**8** He who made the Pleiades and Orion, and turns deep darkness into the morning and darkens the day into night, who calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out on the surface of the earth, the LORD is his name,

**9** who makes destruction flash forth against the strong, so that destruction comes on the fortress.

**10** They hate the one who reproves in the gate, and they abhor the one who speaks the truth.

**11** Therefore because you trample on the poor and take from him levies of grain, you have built houses of hewn stone, but you will not live in them. You have planted pleasant vineyards, but you will not drink their wine.

**12** For I know how many are your transgressions and how great are your sins, you who afflict the righteous \[H-TSEDAQAH\], who take a bribe, and turn aside the needy in the gate.

**13** Therefore the prudent will keep silent in such a time, for it is an evil time.

**14** Seek good, and not evil, that you may live. And so the LORD, the God of hosts, will be with you, as you have said.

**15** Hate evil and love \[H-AHAV\] good, and establish justice in the gate. It may be that the LORD, the God of hosts, will be gracious to the remnant of Joseph.

**16** Therefore thus says the LORD, the God of hosts, the Lord: In all the squares there will be wailing, and in all the streets they will say, "Alas, alas." They will call the farmer to mourning and those skilled in lamentation to wailing,

**17** and in all vineyards there will be wailing, for I will pass through your midst, says the LORD.

**18** Alas for you who desire the day of the LORD. Why would you have the day of the LORD? It is darkness and not light,

**19** as if a man fled from a lion, and a bear met him, or went into the house and leaned his hand against the wall, and a snake bit him.

**20** Is not the day of the LORD darkness and not light, and gloom with no brightness in it?

**21** I hate, I despise your feasts, and I take no delight in your solemn assemblies.

**22** Even though you offer me your burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them, and the peace offerings of your fattened animals I will not look on.

**23** Take away from me the noise of your songs. To the melody of your harps I will not listen.

**24** But let justice roll down like waters, and righteousness \[H-TSEDAQAH\] like an ever-flowing stream.

**25** Did you bring me sacrifices and offerings during the forty years in the wilderness, house of Israel?

**26** You took up Sakkuth your king and Kaiwan your star-god, your images, which you made for yourselves,

**27** and I will send you into exile beyond Damascus, says the LORD, whose name is the God of hosts.

### Chapter 6

**1** Alas for those who are at ease in Zion, and for those who feel secure on the mountain of Samaria, the notable men of the first of the nations, to whom the house of Israel comes.

**2** Cross over to Calneh and see, and from there go to Hamath the great, then go down to Gath of the Philistines. Are you better than these kingdoms, or is their territory greater than your territory?

**3** Those who put far away the day of disaster and bring near the seat of violence,

**4** who lie on beds of ivory and stretch themselves on their couches, and eat lambs from the flock and calves from the stall,

**5** who sing idle songs to the sound of the harp and like David invent for themselves instruments of music,

**6** who drink wine in bowls and anoint themselves with the finest oils, but are not grieved over the ruin of Joseph.

**7** Therefore they will now be the first of those to go into exile, and the revelry of those who stretch themselves out will pass away.

**8** The Lord GOD has sworn by himself \[H-NEPHESH\], declares the LORD, the God of hosts: I abhor the pride of Jacob and hate his strongholds, and I will deliver up the city and all that is in it.

**9** And if ten people remain in one house, they will die.

**10** And when one's uncle, the one who burns the body, takes up the bones to carry them out of the house, and says to someone in the innermost parts of the house, "Is anyone with you?" the answer will be, "No." And he will say, "Hush. We must not mention the name of the LORD."

**11** For see, the LORD commands, and the great house will be struck down into fragments, and the little house into bits.

**12** Do horses run on rocks? Does one plough the sea with oxen? But you have turned justice into poison and the fruit of righteousness \[H-TSEDAQAH\] into wormwood,

**13** you who rejoice in Lo-davar, who say, "Have we not by our own strength captured Karnaim for ourselves?"

**14** For see, I will raise up against you a nation, house of Israel, declares the LORD, the God of hosts, and they will oppress you from Lebo-hamath to the Brook of the Arabah.

The numbering is the same as the Hebrew. The whole of the part is in poetry, apart from 6:9-10, and the verses are set in lines. ## Part Four: Chapters 7 to 9, the visions and the promise

### Chapter 7

**1** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: he was forming locusts at the time the latter growth began to sprout, and see, it was the latter growth after the king's mowings.

**2** When they had finished eating the grass of the land, I said, "Lord GOD, forgive, I beg you. How can Jacob stand? He is so small."

**3** The LORD relented \[H-NACHAM\] concerning this. "It will not be," said the LORD.

**4** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: the Lord GOD was calling for judgement by fire, and it devoured the great deep and was devouring the land.

**5** Then I said, "Lord GOD, cease, I beg you. How can Jacob stand? He is so small."

**6** The LORD relented \[H-NACHAM\] concerning this. "This also will not be," said the Lord GOD.

**7** This is what he showed me: the Lord was standing beside a wall built with a plumb line, with a plumb line in his hand.

**8** And the LORD said to me, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "A plumb line." Then the Lord said, "See, I am setting a plumb line in the midst of my people Israel. I will never again pass by them.

**9** The high places of Isaac will be made desolate, and the sanctuaries \[H-QADOSH\] of Israel will be laid waste, and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword."

**10** Then Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, "Amos has conspired against you in the midst of the house of Israel. The land is not able to bear all his words.

**11** For thus Amos has said, 'Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel must go into exile away from his land.'"

**12** And Amaziah said to Amos, "Seer, go, flee away to the land of Judah, and eat bread there, and prophesy there,

**13** but never again prophesy at Bethel, for it is the king's sanctuary \[H-QADOSH\], and it is a temple of the kingdom."

**14** Then Amos answered and said to Amaziah, "I am no prophet \[H-NAVI\], nor a prophet's son \[H-NAVI\], but I am a herdsman and a dresser of sycamore figs.

**15** But the LORD took me from following the flock, and the LORD said to me, 'Go, prophesy to my people Israel.'

**16** Now therefore hear the word of the LORD. You say, 'Do not prophesy against Israel, and do not preach against the house of Isaac.'

**17** Therefore thus says the LORD: 'Your wife will be a prostitute in the city, and your sons and your daughters will fall by the sword, and your land will be divided up by line, and you yourself will die in an unclean land, and Israel will surely go into exile away from its land.'"

### Chapter 8

**1** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: a basket of summer fruit.

**2** He said, "Amos, what do you see?" And I said, "A basket of summer fruit." Then the LORD said to me, "The end has come on my people Israel. I will never again pass by them.

**3** The songs of the palace will become wailings in that day," declares the Lord GOD. "Many will be the corpses, thrown everywhere in silence."

**4** Hear this, you who trample on the needy and bring the poor of the land to an end,

**5** saying, "When will the new moon be over, that we may sell grain? And the Sabbath \[H-SHABBAT\], that we may offer wheat for sale, that we may make the ephah small and the shekel great and deal deceitfully with false balances,

**6** that we may buy the poor for silver and the needy for a pair of sandals and sell the refuse of the wheat?"

**7** The LORD has sworn by the pride of Jacob: Never will I forget any of their deeds.

**8** Will not the land tremble on this account, and everyone mourn who lives in it, and all of it rise like the Nile, and be tossed about and sink again, like the Nile of Egypt?

**9** And on that day, declares the Lord GOD, I will make the sun go down at noon, and darken the earth in broad daylight.

**10** I will turn your feasts into mourning and all your songs into lamentation. I will bring sackcloth on all loins and baldness on every head. I will make it like the mourning for an only son, and the end of it like a bitter day.

**11** See, the days are coming, declares the Lord GOD, when I will send a famine on the land, not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the LORD.

**12** They will wander from sea to sea, and from north to east. They will run to and fro to seek the word of the LORD, but they will not find it.

**13** In that day the beautiful young women and the young men will faint for thirst.

**14** Those who swear by the guilt of Samaria, and say, "As your god lives, Dan," and, "As the way of Beersheba lives," they will fall, and never rise again.

### Chapter 9

**1** I saw the Lord standing beside the altar, and he said: Strike the capitals until the thresholds shake, and shatter them on the heads of all the people. And those who are left I will kill with the sword. Not one of them will flee away. Not one of them will escape.

**2** Though they dig into Sheol \[H-SHEOL\], from there my hand will take them. Though they climb up to heaven, from there I will bring them down.

**3** Though they hide themselves on the top of Carmel, from there I will search them out and take them. And though they hide from my sight at the bottom of the sea, there I will command the serpent, and it will bite them.

**4** And though they go into captivity before their enemies, there I will command the sword, and it will kill them. And I will fix my eyes on them for evil and not for good.

**5** The Lord GOD of hosts, he who touches the earth and it melts, and all who live in it mourn, and all of it rises like the Nile, and sinks again, like the Nile of Egypt,

**6** who builds his upper chambers in the heavens and founds his vault on the earth, who calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out on the surface of the earth, the LORD is his name.

**7** Are you not like the Cushites to me, people of Israel? declares the LORD. Did I not bring up Israel from the land of Egypt, and the Philistines from Caphtor and the Syrians from Kir?

**8** See, the eyes of the Lord GOD are on the sinful kingdom, and I will destroy it from the surface of the ground, except that I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob, declares the LORD.

**9** For see, I will command, and shake the house of Israel among all the nations as one shakes with a sieve, but no pebble will fall to the ground.

**10** All the sinners of my people will die by the sword, who say, "Disaster will not overtake or meet us."

**11** In that day I will raise up the booth of David that is fallen, and repair its breaches, and raise up its ruins, and rebuild it as in the days of old,

**12** that they may possess the remnant of Edom and all the nations who are called by my name, declares the LORD who does this.

**13** See, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when the ploughman will overtake the reaper and the treader of grapes the one who sows the seed. The mountains will drip sweet wine, and all the hills will flow with it.

**14** I will restore \[H-SHUV\] the fortunes of my people Israel, and they will rebuild the ruined cities and live in them. They will plant vineyards and drink their wine, and they will make gardens and eat their fruit.

**15** I will plant them on their land, and they will never again be uprooted out of the land that I have given them, says the LORD your God.

They are the visions of the locusts, the fire and the plumb line (7:1-9), the dispute between Amaziah the priest of Bethel and Amos (7:10-17), the basket of summer fruit, the greed of the merchants and the famine of the word (8:1-14), and the vision of the LORD beside the altar, the song of praise, the sifting of Israel and the restoration of the booth of David (9:1-15). The numbering is the same as the Hebrew. The chapters are poetry and prose, with 7:1-9 and 7:10-17 mostly prose in form and the rest set in lines. In 7:3 and 7:6 "relented" translates nicham and is tagged H-NACHAM. In 7:9 and 7:13 "sanctuaries" and "sanctuary" translate miqdeshei and miqdash and are tagged H-QADOSH. In 7:14 "prophet" and "a prophet's son" are tagged H-NAVI. In 8:5 "Sabbath" is tagged H-SHABBAT. In 8:13 "the beautiful young women" translates ha-betulot and the rendering is open to the team's decision with the other cases. In 9:2 "Sheol" is tagged H-SHEOL. In 9:14 "I will restore the fortunes" translates shavti et-shevut and is tagged H-SHUV. The words of 9:5-6 are the third of three songs of praise in the book (see also 4:13 and 5:8-9), and they are given the same wording where the Hebrew repeats the phrases. In 7:14 the Hebrew has no verb, and "I am no prophet" follows the sense of the present, and the footnote gives the other reading ("I was no prophet"). There are no departures from the Hebrew in chapters 7 to 9, and none in the whole book. The Greek differs from the Hebrew in some places, notably in 9:11-12 ("that the rest of humankind may seek", quoted in Acts 15:16-17), and the footnote records the matter. The Hebrew is hard in some places, including 7:1, 7:4, 7:14, 8:1-3, 8:8, 9:1, 9:6 and 9:12. The footnotes record the main differences and the uncertain terms. The words on the end of Israel (8:2), the wife of Amaziah and his sons and daughters (7:17), the trampling of the poor by the merchants (8:4-6) and the sifting of Israel (9:9-10) are rendered as written, with no comment. The section needs a check against the Hebrew by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked.*
