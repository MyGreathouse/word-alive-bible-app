# WABV: Galatians

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-PISTIS\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Galatians is the ninth book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the fourth of the letters. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It is a letter from Paul and the brothers with him to the assemblies of Galatia, written to answer those who were telling the believers among the nations that they must be circumcised and keep the law. It is the shortest and the sharpest of the great letters of Paul, and it has no thanksgiving, and it opens with astonishment (1:6). Its date and its addressees are debated: the north of the province (the old Galatian territory) or the south (the cities of Acts 13 and 14), and the date is placed anywhere from the late 40s to the mid-50s, and the WABV text does not decide. The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text of Galatians is drafted with the voice standard (Part 46 of the review package), and makes a number of fixed choices that follow from its Greek, and they are written down here so that they are used throughout, and they are the same as the choices in Romans. *Dikaioo* is rendered "declare righteous" (and in the passive "be declared righteous") and *dikaiosune* "righteousness", and both are tagged G-DIKAIOSUNE, and the team should decide between "declare righteous" and "justify". *Erga nomou* (2:16, 3:2, 3:5, 3:10) is rendered "works of the law", and the footnote records the debate about what the phrase means. *Pistis Christou* (2:16, 2:20, 3:22) is rendered "faith in Christ" and "faith in the Son of God", and the footnote records "the faithfulness of Christ", and the team should decide. *Pistis* is rendered "faith" and *pisteuo* "believe", and both are tagged G-PISTIS. *Nomos* is rendered "law" and is tagged G-NOMOS. *Euangelion* is rendered "good news" and is tagged G-EUANGELION, and *euangelizomai* "proclaim the good news". *Charis* is rendered "grace" and is tagged G-CHARIS. *Pneuma* is rendered "Spirit" or "spirit" and is tagged G-PNEUMA. *Sarx* is rendered "flesh". *Stoicheia* (4:3, 4:9) is rendered "elemental forces", and the footnote records "elements", "basic principles" and "elemental spirits". *Huiothesia* is rendered "adoption as sons". *Paidagogos* (3:24-25) is rendered "guardian", and the footnote records the nature of the office. *Anathema* is rendered "accursed". *Ekklesia* is rendered "assembly" and is tagged G-EKKLESIA, and the team should decide between "assembly" and "church". *Adelphoi* is rendered "brothers", and the footnote records that in many places it may include the sisters, and the team should decide. *Ioudaios* is rendered "Jew", and *ethne* "the nations", and the footnote records that *ethne* may also be read "Gentiles". *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. *Agape* is rendered "love" and is tagged G-AGAPE. The book falls into two parts in the WABV text: the one good news, the call of the apostle, the meeting at Jerusalem, Antioch, and Abraham, the law and faith (chapters 1 to 3), and sons and slaves, Hagar and Sarah, freedom, the flesh and the Spirit and the new creation (4 to 6). The headings of the parts are not in the Greek. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System, and the new ones are listed in the review note of the part where each first appears. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Part One: Chapters 1 to 3, the one good news, the call of the apostle, Abraham, the law and faith

### Chapter 1

**1** Paul, an apostle (not from people nor through a human being, but through Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] and God the Father who raised him from the dead),

**2** and all the brothers who are with me, to the assemblies \[G-EKKLESIA\] of Galatia:

**3** grace \[G-CHARIS\] to you and peace \[G-EIRENE\] from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ,

**4** who gave himself for our sins to rescue us from the present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,

**5** to whom be the glory \[G-DOXA\] for ever and ever. Amen.

**6** I am astonished that you are so quickly turning away from the one who called you in the grace \[G-CHARIS\] of Christ and going over to a different good news \[G-EUANGELION\],

**7** not that there is another one, but there are some who are troubling you and want to twist the good news of Christ.

**8** But even if we or an angel from heaven were to proclaim to you a good news contrary to what we proclaimed to you, let him be accursed.

**9** As we have said before, so now I say again: if anyone proclaims to you a good news contrary to what you received, let him be accursed.

**10** For am I now seeking the approval of people, or of God? Or am I trying to please people? If I were still trying to please people, I would not be a slave of Christ.

**11** For I want you to know, brothers, that the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] that was proclaimed by me is not of human origin.

**12** For I did not receive it from any human being, nor was I taught it, but it came through a revelation of Jesus Christ.

**13** For you have heard of my former way of life in Judaism, how I persecuted the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] of God violently and tried to destroy it.

**14** And I advanced in Judaism beyond many of my own age among my people, being far more zealous for the traditions of my fathers.

**15** But when he who set me apart before I was born, and called me by his grace \[G-CHARIS\],

**16** was pleased to reveal his Son in me, so that I might proclaim him among the nations, I did not immediately consult with flesh and blood,

**17** nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went away into Arabia, and returned again to Damascus.

**18** Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to visit Cephas and stayed with him fifteen days.

**19** But I saw none of the other apostles except James the Lord's brother.

**20** (In what I am writing to you, look, before God, I do not lie.)

**21** Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.

**22** And I was still unknown in person to the assemblies \[G-EKKLESIA\] of Judea that are in Christ.

**23** They only were hearing it said, "The one who used to persecute us is now proclaiming the faith \[G-PISTIS\] he once tried to destroy."

**24** And they glorified \[G-DOXA\] God because of me.

### Chapter 2

**1** Then after fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus along with me.

**2** I went up because of a revelation, and I set before them (though privately before those who seemed influential) the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] that I proclaim among the nations, in order to make sure that I was not running, or had not run, in vain.

**3** But even Titus, who was with me, was not forced to be circumcised, though he was a Greek.

**4** Yet because of false brothers secretly brought in, who slipped in to spy out our freedom that we have in Christ Jesus, so that they might bring us into slavery,

**5** we did not yield to them even for a moment, so that the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] of the good news might remain with you.

**6** And from those who seemed to be influential (what they were makes no difference to me; God shows no partiality) those, I say, who seemed influential added nothing to me.

**7** On the contrary, when they saw that I had been entrusted with the good news for the uncircumcised, just as Peter was for the circumcised,

**8** (for he who worked through Peter for his apostleship to the circumcised worked also through me for mine to the nations),

**9** and when James and Cephas and John, who seemed to be pillars, recognised the grace \[G-CHARIS\] that had been given to me, they gave to Barnabas and me the right hand of fellowship, that we should go to the nations and they to the circumcised.

**10** Only, they asked us to remember the poor, the very thing I was eager to do.

**11** But when Cephas came to Antioch, I opposed him to his face, because he stood condemned.

**12** For before certain men came from James, he was eating with the nations. But when they came he drew back and separated himself, fearing the circumcision party.

**13** And the rest of the Jews acted hypocritically along with him, so that even Barnabas was led astray by their hypocrisy.

**14** But when I saw that their conduct was not in step with the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] of the good news, I said to Cephas before them all, "If you, though a Jew, live as the nations do and not as Jews do, how can you force the nations to live as Jews do?"

**15** We ourselves are Jews by birth and not sinners from the nations,

**16** yet we know that a person is not declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by works of the law \[G-NOMOS\] but through faith in Jesus Christ \[G-PISTIS\], so we also have believed \[G-PISTIS\] in Christ Jesus, in order to be declared righteous by faith in Christ and not by works of the law, because by works of the law no flesh will be declared righteous.

**17** But if, in our endeavour to be declared righteous in Christ, we ourselves were found to be sinners, is Christ then a servant of sin? Certainly not.

**18** For if I rebuild what I tore down, I prove myself a lawbreaker.

**19** For through the law I died to the law, so that I might live to God.

**20** I have been crucified with Christ. It is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me. And the life I now live in the flesh I live by faith \[G-PISTIS\] in the Son of God, who loved \[G-AGAPE\] me and gave himself for me.

**21** I do not set aside the grace \[G-CHARIS\] of God, for if righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] were through the law \[G-NOMOS\], then Christ died for nothing.

### Chapter 3

**1** You foolish Galatians. Who has put you under a spell? It was before your eyes that Jesus Christ was publicly displayed as crucified.

**2** Let me ask you only this: did you receive the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] by works of the law \[G-NOMOS\], or by hearing with faith \[G-PISTIS\]?

**3** Are you so foolish? Having begun with the Spirit, are you now being completed by the flesh?

**4** Did you experience so much in vain, if indeed it was in vain?

**5** Does he who supplies the Spirit to you and works miracles among you do so by works of the law, or by hearing with faith?

**6** Just as Abraham "believed \[G-PISTIS\] God, and it was counted to him as righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\],"

**7** so you see that it is those of faith who are the sons of Abraham.

**8** And the scripture, foreseeing that God would declare the nations righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by faith, proclaimed the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] beforehand to Abraham, saying, "In you all the nations will be blessed."

**9** So then, those who are of faith are blessed along with Abraham, the man of faith.

**10** For all who rely on works of the law are under a curse, for it is written, "Cursed is everyone who does not abide by all things written in the book of the law, and do them."

**11** Now it is evident that no one is declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] before God by the law, for "The righteous one will live by faith \[G-PISTIS\]."

**12** But the law is not of faith, rather "The one who does them will live by them."

**13** Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] redeemed \[G-LYTRON\] us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us, for it is written, "Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree,"

**14** so that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the nations, so that we might receive the promised Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] through faith.

**15** Brothers, I speak in human terms: even with a human covenant \[G-DIATHEKE\], no one annuls it or adds to it once it has been ratified.

**16** Now the promises were made to Abraham and to his offspring. It does not say, "And to offsprings," referring to many, but referring to one, "And to your offspring," who is Christ.

**17** This is what I mean: the law, which came four hundred and thirty years afterward, does not annul a covenant \[G-DIATHEKE\] previously ratified by God, so as to make the promise void.

**18** For if the inheritance comes by the law, it no longer comes by promise, but God gave it to Abraham by a promise.

**19** Why then the law? It was added because of transgressions, until the offspring should come to whom the promise had been made, and it was put in place through angels by an intermediary.

**20** Now an intermediary implies more than one, but God is one.

**21** Is the law then contrary to the promises of God? Certainly not. For if a law had been given that could give life \[G-ZOE\], then righteousness would indeed be by the law.

**22** But the scripture imprisoned everything under sin, so that the promise by faith in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe \[G-PISTIS\].

**23** Now before faith \[G-PISTIS\] came, we were held captive under the law, imprisoned until the coming faith would be revealed.

**24** So then, the law was our guardian until Christ came, so that we might be declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by faith.

**25** But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian,

**26** for in Christ Jesus you are all sons of God, through faith.

**27** For as many of you as were baptised into Christ have put on Christ.

**28** There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is no male and female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus.

**29** And if you are Christ's, then you are Abraham's offspring, heirs according to promise.

## Part Two: Chapters 4 to 6, sons and slaves, freedom, the flesh and the Spirit, and the new creation

### Chapter 4

**1** I mean that as long as the heir is a child, he is no different from a slave, though he is the owner of everything,

**2** but he is under guardians and managers until the date set by his father.

**3** So also we, when we were children, were enslaved to the elemental forces of the world.

**4** But when the fullness of time had come, God sent his Son, born of a woman, born under the law \[G-NOMOS\],

**5** to redeem \[G-LYTRON\] those who were under the law, so that we might receive adoption as sons.

**6** And because you are sons, God has sent the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of his Son into our hearts, crying, "Abba, Father."

**7** So you are no longer a slave but a son, and if a son, then an heir through God.

**8** Formerly, when you did not know God, you were enslaved to beings that by nature are not gods.

**9** But now that you have come to know God, or rather to be known by God, how can you turn back again to the weak and worthless elemental forces, to which you want to be enslaved all over again?

**10** You observe days and months and seasons and years.

**11** I am afraid I may have laboured over you in vain.

**12** I beg you, brothers, become as I am, for I also have become as you are. You did me no wrong.

**13** You know that it was because of a bodily weakness that I first proclaimed the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] to you,

**14** and though my condition was a trial to you, you did not scorn or despise me, but received me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus.

**15** What then has become of your blessing \[G-MAKARIOS\]? For I testify that, if possible, you would have gouged out your eyes and given them to me.

**16** Have I then become your enemy by telling you the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\]?

**17** They make much of you, but not for good. They want to shut you out, so that you may make much of them.

**18** It is always good to be made much of in a good cause, and not only when I am present with you,

**19** my little children, for whom I am again in the pains of childbirth until Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] is formed in you.

**20** I wish I could be present with you now and change my tone, for I am perplexed about you.

**21** Tell me, you who desire to be under the law \[G-NOMOS\], do you not listen to the law?

**22** For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by the slave woman and one by the free woman.

**23** But the son of the slave was born according to the flesh, while the son of the free woman was born through promise.

**24** Now this may be interpreted allegorically: these women are two covenants \[G-DIATHEKE\]. One is from Mount Sinai, bearing children for slavery. She is Hagar.

**25** Now Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia, and she corresponds to the present Jerusalem, for she is in slavery with her children.

**26** But the Jerusalem above is free, and she is our mother.

**27** For it is written: "Rejoice, O barren one who does not bear, break forth and cry aloud, you who are not in labour, for the children of the desolate one are more than those of the one who has a husband."

**28** Now you, brothers, like Isaac, are children of promise.

**29** But just as at that time the one who was born according to the flesh persecuted the one who was born according to the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], so also it is now.

**30** But what does the scripture say? "Cast out the slave woman and her son, for the son of the slave woman shall not inherit with the son of the free woman."

**31** So, brothers, we are not children of the slave but of the free woman.

### Chapter 5

**1** For freedom Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] has set us free. Stand firm, then, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery.

**2** Look, I, Paul, say to you that if you accept circumcision, Christ will be of no benefit to you.

**3** I testify again to every man who accepts circumcision that he is obligated to keep the whole law \[G-NOMOS\].

**4** You are severed from Christ, you who would be declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by the law. You have fallen away from grace \[G-CHARIS\].

**5** For through the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], by faith \[G-PISTIS\], we eagerly wait for the hope of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

**6** For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts for anything, but only faith \[G-PISTIS\] working through love \[G-AGAPE\].

**7** You were running well. Who hindered you from obeying the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\]?

**8** This persuasion is not from him who calls you.

**9** A little yeast works through the whole lump.

**10** I have confidence in the Lord that you will take no other view, and the one who is troubling you will bear the penalty, whoever he is.

**11** But if I, brothers, still proclaim circumcision, why am I still being persecuted? In that case the offence of the cross has been removed.

**12** I wish those who unsettle you would go the whole way and castrate themselves.

**13** For you were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through love \[G-AGAPE\] serve one another.

**14** For the whole law \[G-NOMOS\] is fulfilled in one word: "You shall love \[G-AGAPE\] your neighbour as yourself."

**15** But if you bite and devour one another, watch out that you are not consumed by one another.

### Chapter 6

**1** Brothers, if anyone is caught in any transgression, you who are spiritual should restore him in a spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of gentleness. Keep watch on yourself, lest you too be tempted.

**2** Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law \[G-NOMOS\] of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\].

**3** For if anyone thinks he is something, when he is nothing, he deceives himself.

**4** But let each one test his own work, and then his reason to boast will be in himself alone and not in his neighbour.

**5** For each will have to bear his own load.

**6** Let the one who is taught the word share all good things with the one who teaches.

**7** Do not be deceived: God is not mocked, for whatever one sows, that will he also reap.

**8** For the one who sows to his own flesh will from the flesh reap corruption, but the one who sows to the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] will from the Spirit reap eternal life \[G-ZOE\].

**9** And let us not grow weary of doing good, for in due season we will reap, if we do not give up.

**10** So then, as we have opportunity, let us do good to everyone, and especially to those who are of the household of faith \[G-PISTIS\].
