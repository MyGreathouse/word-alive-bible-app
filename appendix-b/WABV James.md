# WABV: James

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-PISTIS\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. James is the twentieth book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the first of the seven general letters (James, 1 and 2 Peter, 1, 2 and 3 John and Jude). It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It is a letter from James, a slave of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes in the Dispersion (1:1), and it is a short, direct and practical writing, in a Greek that is good and plain, with many sayings and images and with a strong likeness to the wisdom writings and to the teaching of Jesus in the Sermon on the Mount. Who James is is debated: the tradition is that he is James the brother of Jesus, who led the assembly in Jerusalem (Acts 15, Galatians 1:19, 2:9), and some scholars hold that the letter was written in his name, and the WABV text does not decide. Its main themes are testing and steadfastness (1:2-18), hearing and doing the word (1:19-27), partiality and the rich and the poor (2:1-13), faith and works (2:14-26), the tongue (3:1-12), the two kinds of wisdom (3:13-18), pride and quarrels and the world (4:1-12), the rich and the merchants (4:13-5:6), patience and oaths (5:7-12) and prayer for the sick and for one another (5:13-20). Its words on faith and works are the most debated, because they seem to differ from Paul's words on being declared righteous by faith (Romans 3-4, Galatians 2-3), and the WABV text renders both as written, with the same word, *dikaioo*, rendered "declare righteous" in both, and the footnote records the matter. The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text of James is drafted with the voice standard (Part 46 of the review package). Where the text of a critical edition is not certain, the draft says so in the footnote and asks the team to confirm the reading. It makes a number of fixed choices that follow from its Greek, and they are written down here so that they are used throughout. *Peirasmos* and *peirazo* are rendered "trial", "test" and "tempt", and the footnote records the choice in each place, since the same Greek word means a testing from outside and a temptation from within. *Dikaioo* is rendered "declare righteous" (2:21, 2:24, 2:25) and *dikaiosune* "righteousness", and both are tagged G-DIKAIOSUNE. *Pistis* is rendered "faith" and *pisteuo* "believe", and both are tagged G-PISTIS. *Teleioo* and its family are rendered "bring to completion", "complete" and "perfect", as in Hebrews. *Nomos* is rendered "law" and is tagged G-NOMOS, and *nomos eleutherias* (1:25, 2:12) "the law of liberty". *Eleos* is rendered "mercy" and is tagged G-ELEOS. *Sozo* is rendered "save" and is tagged G-SOZO. *Zoe* is rendered "life" and is tagged G-ZOE. *Psyche* is rendered "soul" and is tagged G-PSYCHE. *Makarios* is rendered "blessed" and is tagged G-MAKARIOS. *Agape* is rendered "love" and is tagged G-AGAPE. *Charis* is rendered "grace" and is tagged G-CHARIS. *Basileia* is rendered "kingdom" and is tagged G-BASILEIA. *Aletheia* is rendered "truth" and is tagged G-ALETHEIA. *Eirene* is rendered "peace" and is tagged G-EIRENE. *Pneuma* is rendered "spirit" and is tagged G-PNEUMA. *Ekklesia* (5:14) is rendered "assembly" and is tagged G-EKKLESIA, and the team should decide between "assembly" and "church". *Sunagoge* (2:2) is rendered "assembly" and is not tagged. *Geenna* (3:6) is rendered "Gehenna". *Parousia* (5:7-8) is rendered "coming". *Anthropos* is rendered "person" or "human being", and *aner* "man". *Adelphoi* is rendered "brothers", and the footnote records that in many places it may include the sisters, and the team should decide. *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Chapters 1 to 5, testing, the word, faith and works, the tongue and prayer

### Chapter 1

**1** James, a slave of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], to the twelve tribes in the Dispersion: greetings.

**2** Count it all joy, my brothers, when you meet trials of various kinds,

**3** for you know that the testing of your faith \[G-PISTIS\] produces steadfastness.

**4** And let steadfastness have its full effect, that you may be complete and whole, lacking in nothing.

**5** If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God, who gives generously to all without reproach, and it will be given him.

**6** But let him ask in faith \[G-PISTIS\], with no doubting, for the one who doubts is like a wave of the sea that is driven and tossed by the wind.

**7** For that person must not suppose that he will receive anything from the Lord.

**8** He is a double-minded person, unstable in all his ways.

**9** Let the lowly brother boast in his exaltation,

**10** and the rich in his humiliation, because like a flower of the grass he will pass away.

**11** For the sun rises with its scorching heat and withers the grass. Its flower falls, and its beauty perishes. So also will the rich person fade away in the midst of his pursuits.

**12** Blessed \[G-MAKARIOS\] is the one who remains steadfast under trial, for when he has stood the test he will receive the crown of life \[G-ZOE\], which God has promised to those who love \[G-AGAPE\] him.

**13** Let no one say when he is tempted, "I am being tempted by God," for God cannot be tempted with evil, and he himself tempts no one.

**14** But each person is tempted when he is lured and enticed by his own desire.

**15** Then desire when it has conceived gives birth to sin, and sin when it is fully grown brings forth death.

**16** Do not be deceived, my beloved brothers.

**17** Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom there is no variation or shadow due to change.

**18** Of his own will he brought us forth by the word of truth \[G-ALETHEIA\], that we should be a kind of firstfruits of his creatures.

**19** Know this, my beloved brothers: let every person be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger,

**20** for the anger of a person does not produce the righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] of God.

**21** Therefore put away all filthiness and rampant wickedness and receive with meekness the implanted word, which is able to save \[G-SOZO\] your souls \[G-PSYCHE\].

**22** But be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves.

**23** For if anyone is a hearer of the word and not a doer, he is like a person who looks intently at his natural face in a mirror.

**24** For he looks at himself and goes away and at once forgets what he was like.

**25** But the one who looks into the perfect law \[G-NOMOS\], the law of liberty, and perseveres, being no hearer who forgets but a doer who acts, he will be blessed \[G-MAKARIOS\] in his doing.

**26** If anyone thinks he is religious and does not bridle his tongue but deceives his heart, this person's religion is worthless.

**27** Religion that is pure and undefiled before God the Father is this: to visit orphans and widows in their affliction, and to keep oneself unstained from the world.

### Chapter 2

**1** My brothers, show no partiality as you hold the faith \[G-PISTIS\] in our glorious \[G-DOXA\] Lord Jesus Christ.

**2** For if a man wearing a gold ring and fine clothing comes into your assembly, and a poor man in shabby clothing also comes in,

**3** and if you pay attention to the one who wears the fine clothing and say, "You sit here in a good place," while you say to the poor man, "You stand over there," or, "Sit down at my feet,"

**4** have you not then made distinctions among yourselves and become judges with evil thoughts?

**5** Listen, my beloved brothers, has not God chosen those who are poor in the world to be rich in faith \[G-PISTIS\] and heirs of the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\], which he has promised to those who love \[G-AGAPE\] him?

**6** But you have dishonoured the poor man. Are not the rich the ones who oppress you, and the ones who drag you into court?

**7** Are they not the ones who blaspheme the honourable name by which you were called?

**8** If you really fulfil the royal law according to the scripture, "You shall love \[G-AGAPE\] your neighbour as yourself," you are doing well.

**9** But if you show partiality, you are committing sin and are convicted by the law \[G-NOMOS\] as transgressors.

**10** For whoever keeps the whole law but fails in one point has become accountable for all of it.

**11** For he who said, "Do not commit adultery," also said, "Do not murder." If you do not commit adultery but do murder, you have become a transgressor of the law.

**12** So speak and so act as those who are to be judged under the law of liberty.

**13** For judgement is without mercy \[G-ELEOS\] to one who has shown no mercy. Mercy triumphs over judgement.

**14** What good is it, my brothers, if someone says he has faith \[G-PISTIS\] but does not have works? Can that faith save \[G-SOZO\] him?

**15** If a brother or sister is poorly clothed and lacking in daily food,

**16** and one of you says to them, "Go in peace \[G-EIRENE\], be warmed and filled," without giving them the things needed for the body, what good is that?

**17** So also faith \[G-PISTIS\] by itself, if it does not have works, is dead.

**18** But someone will say, "You have faith and I have works." Show me your faith \[G-PISTIS\] apart from your works, and I will show you my faith by my works.

**19** You believe \[G-PISTIS\] that God is one. You do well. Even the demons believe, and shudder.

**20** Do you want to be shown, you foolish person, that faith \[G-PISTIS\] apart from works is useless?

**21** Was not Abraham our father declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by works when he offered up his son Isaac on the altar?

**22** You see that faith \[G-PISTIS\] was active along with his works, and faith was brought to completion by his works,

**23** and the scripture was fulfilled that says, "Abraham believed \[G-PISTIS\] God, and it was counted to him as righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\]," and he was called a friend of God.

**24** You see that a person is declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by works and not by faith \[G-PISTIS\] alone.

**25** And in the same way was not also Rahab the prostitute declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by works when she received the messengers and sent them out by another way?

**26** For as the body apart from the spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] is dead, so also faith \[G-PISTIS\] apart from works is dead.

### Chapter 3

**1** Not many of you should become teachers, my brothers, for you know that we who teach will be judged with greater strictness.

**2** For we all stumble in many ways. If anyone does not stumble in what he says, he is a complete person, able also to bridle his whole body.

**3** If we put bits into the mouths of horses so that they obey us, we guide their whole bodies as well.

**4** Look at the ships also: though they are so large and are driven by strong winds, they are guided by a very small rudder wherever the will of the pilot directs.

**5** So also the tongue is a small member, yet it boasts of great things. How great a forest is set ablaze by such a small fire.

**6** And the tongue is a fire, a world of unrighteousness. The tongue is set among our members, staining the whole body, setting on fire the entire course of life, and set on fire by Gehenna.

**7** For every kind of beast and bird, of reptile and sea creature, can be tamed and has been tamed by human beings,

**8** but no human being can tame the tongue. It is a restless evil, full of deadly poison.

**9** With it we bless our Lord and Father, and with it we curse people who are made in the likeness of God.

**10** From the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brothers, these things ought not to be so.

**11** Does a spring pour forth from the same opening both fresh and salt water?

**12** Can a fig tree, my brothers, bear olives, or a grapevine produce figs? Neither can a salt pond yield fresh water.

**13** Who is wise and understanding among you? By his good conduct let him show his works in the meekness of wisdom.

**14** But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast and be false to the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\].

**15** This is not the wisdom that comes down from above, but is earthly, unspiritual, demonic.

**16** For where jealousy and selfish ambition exist, there will be disorder and every vile practice.

**17** But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, open to reason, full of mercy \[G-ELEOS\] and good fruits, impartial and sincere.

**18** And a harvest of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] is sown in peace \[G-EIRENE\] by those who make peace.

### Chapter 4

**1** What causes quarrels and what causes fights among you? Is it not this, that your passions are at war within you?

**2** You desire and do not have, so you murder. You covet and cannot obtain, so you fight and quarrel. You do not have, because you do not ask.

**3** You ask and do not receive, because you ask wrongly, to spend it on your passions.

**4** You adulterous people. Do you not know that friendship with the world is enmity with God? Therefore whoever wishes to be a friend of the world makes himself an enemy of God.

**5** Or do you suppose it is to no purpose that the scripture says, "He yearns jealously over the spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] that he has made to dwell in us"?

**6** But he gives more grace \[G-CHARIS\]. Therefore it says, "God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble."

**7** Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

**8** Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded.

**9** Be wretched and mourn and weep. Let your laughter be turned to mourning and your joy to gloom.

**10** Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will exalt you.

**11** Do not speak evil against one another, brothers. The one who speaks against a brother or judges his brother speaks evil against the law \[G-NOMOS\] and judges the law. But if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law but a judge.

**12** There is only one lawgiver and judge, he who is able to save \[G-SOZO\] and to destroy. But who are you to judge your neighbour?

**13** Come now, you who say, "Today or tomorrow we will go into such and such a town and spend a year there and trade and make a profit,"

**14** yet you do not know what tomorrow will bring. What is your life \[G-ZOE\]? For you are a mist that appears for a little time and then vanishes.

**15** Instead you ought to say, "If the Lord wills, we will live and do this or that."

**16** As it is, you boast in your arrogance. All such boasting is evil.

**17** So whoever knows the right thing to do and fails to do it, for him it is sin.

### Chapter 5

**1** Come now, you rich, weep and howl for the miseries that are coming upon you.

**2** Your riches have rotted and your garments are moth-eaten.

**3** Your gold and silver have corroded, and their corrosion will be evidence against you and will eat your flesh like fire. You have laid up treasure in the last days.

**4** Look, the wages of the labourers who mowed your fields, which you kept back by fraud, are crying out against you, and the cries of the harvesters have reached the ears of the Lord of hosts.

**5** You have lived on the earth in luxury and in self-indulgence. You have fattened your hearts in a day of slaughter.

**6** You have condemned and murdered the righteous person. He does not resist you.

**7** Be patient, therefore, brothers, until the coming of the Lord. See how the farmer waits for the precious fruit of the earth, being patient about it, until it receives the early and the late rains.

**8** You also, be patient. Establish your hearts, for the coming of the Lord is at hand.

**9** Do not grumble against one another, brothers, so that you may not be judged. Look, the Judge is standing at the door.

**10** As an example of suffering and patience, brothers, take the prophets who spoke in the name of the Lord.

**11** Look, we call those blessed \[G-MAKARIOS\] who remained steadfast. You have heard of the steadfastness of Job, and you have seen the purpose of the Lord, how the Lord is very compassionate and merciful \[G-ELEOS\].

**12** But above all, my brothers, do not swear, either by heaven or by earth or by any other oath, but let your "Yes" be yes and your "No" be no, so that you may not fall under condemnation.

**13** Is anyone among you suffering? Let him pray. Is anyone cheerful? Let him sing praise.

**14** Is anyone among you sick? Let him call for the elders of the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\], and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord.

**15** And the prayer of faith \[G-PISTIS\] will save \[G-SOZO\] the one who is sick, and the Lord will raise him up. And if he has committed sins, he will be forgiven.

**16** Therefore, confess your sins to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] person has great power as it is working.

**17** Elijah was a person with a nature like ours, and he prayed fervently that it might not rain, and for three years and six months it did not rain on the earth.

**18** Then he prayed again, and heaven gave rain, and the earth bore its fruit.

**19** My brothers, if anyone among you wanders from the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] and someone brings him back,

**20** let him know that whoever brings back a sinner from the error of his way will save \[G-SOZO\] his soul \[G-PSYCHE\] from death and will cover a multitude of sins.

The numbering is the usual numbering. The main points are these, and the footnotes carry the Greek: *peirasmos* and *peirazo* are rendered "trial", "test" and "tempt" and the footnote records the choice; the *dikaioo* of 2:21, 2:24 and 2:25 is rendered "declare righteous", as in Paul, and "declared righteous by works and not by faith alone" is rendered as written, with no comment, and the footnote records that it is debated how James and Paul are to be read together, and the WABV text does not decide; 2:1 "our glorious Lord Jesus Christ" is rendered as written, and the Greek is hard; 3:6 and the "course of life" are rendered as written, and the Greek is hard; 5:14-16 the prayer for the sick is rendered as written, and "save" at 5:15 is tagged G-SOZO and may mean heal or save, and the footnote records the matter; 5:11 "very compassionate" translates *polusplanchnos*, and it is not tagged G-SPLANCHNIZOMAI. There are no departures from the critical Greek text in chapters 1 to 5. The Greek is hard in some places, including 1:17, 2:1, 2:14-26, 3:6, 4:5, 4:14 and 5:20. The section needs a check against the Greek by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked. With this the book of James is complete in first draft.*

## Appendix A: Footnotes

- **1:1** "James" translates *Iakobos*, and who is meant is debated. "The twelve tribes in the Dispersion" is rendered as written, and may be read of the Jewish believers or of all believers. "Greetings" translates *chairein*. See entry G-CHRISTOS.
- **1:2** "Trials" translates *peirasmois*, and "trial" and "test" (verse 12) and "tempt" (verses 13 and 14) translate the same Greek word and its verb, which means a testing from outside and a temptation from within, and the WABV text keeps the one word and varies the English by the sense.
- **1:3** "The testing of your faith" translates *to dokimion humon tes pisteos*, and *dokimion* may also be read "the genuineness" or "the means of testing". See entry G-PISTIS.
- **1:4** "Complete and whole" translates *teleioi kai holokleroi*, and the first is from the *teleioo* family, as in Hebrews.
- **1:5** "Without reproach" is rendered as written.
- **1:8** "A double-minded person" translates *aner dipsuchos*, a word that is not found before James.
- **1:12** "Blessed is the one who remains steadfast under trial" is rendered as written, and "the crown of life" is rendered as written. See entries G-MAKARIOS, G-ZOE and G-AGAPE.
- **1:13** "God cannot be tempted with evil" translates *apeirastos kakon*, and may be read "is untried by evil" or "cannot be tested by evil", and the footnote records the matter.
- **1:17** "With whom there is no variation or shadow due to change" translates *par' ho ouk eni parallage e tropes aposkiasma*, and the Greek is hard and the manuscripts differ, and "the Father of lights" is rendered as written.
- **1:18** "Brought us forth by the word of truth" is rendered as written, and "a kind of firstfruits" translates *aparchen tina*. See entry G-ALETHEIA.
- **1:19** "Know this" is the reading that the draft follows (*iste*), and some manuscripts read "so then" (*hoste*), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **1:20** "The righteousness of God" is rendered as written. See entry G-DIKAIOSUNE.
- **1:21** "The implanted word, which is able to save your souls" is rendered as written. See entries G-SOZO and G-PSYCHE.
- **1:25** "The perfect law, the law of liberty" is rendered as written, and "perfect" is *teleion*. See entries G-NOMOS and G-MAKARIOS.
- **1:26** "Religious" translates *threskos*, and "religion" (verses 26 and 27) translates *threskeia*, which means the outward acts of worship.
- **1:27** "To visit orphans and widows in their affliction" is rendered as written.
- **2:1** "Our glorious Lord Jesus Christ" translates *tou kuriou hemon Iesou Christou tes doxes*, and the Greek is hard, and it may be read "our Lord Jesus Christ, the Lord of glory" or "the glory". See entries G-PISTIS and G-DOXA.
- **2:2** "Assembly" translates *sunagogen*, and may be read "synagogue", and it is not tagged G-EKKLESIA.
- **2:5** "Heirs of the kingdom, which he has promised to those who love him" is rendered as written. See entries G-BASILEIA and G-AGAPE.
- **2:8** "The royal law" is rendered as written, and the words are from Leviticus 19:18.
- **2:10** "Whoever keeps the whole law but fails in one point has become accountable for all of it" is rendered as written. See entry G-NOMOS.
- **2:12** "The law of liberty" is as at 1:25.
- **2:13** "Judgement is without mercy to one who has shown no mercy. Mercy triumphs over judgement" is rendered as written. See entry G-ELEOS.
- **2:14** The passage on faith and works in verses 14 to 26 is rendered as written, and it is debated how it is to be read with Paul's words on being declared righteous by faith (Romans 3 to 4, Galatians 2 to 3), and the WABV text does not decide. "Can that faith save him?" is rendered as written. See entry G-SOZO.
- **2:16** "Go in peace" is rendered as written. See entry G-EIRENE.
- **2:18** "But someone will say" may be read as an objector or as an ally, and the speaker of the words that follow is not named, and the WABV text does not decide.
- **2:19** "God is one" translates *heis ho theos estin*, and some manuscripts read "there is one God" (*heis theos estin*), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **2:20** "Useless" is the reading that the draft follows (*arge*), and some manuscripts read "dead" (*nekra*), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **2:21** "Declared righteous by works" translates *edikaiothe ex ergon*, and *dikaioo* is rendered "declare righteous" here and in verses 24 and 25, as in Paul.
- **2:22** "Faith was brought to completion by his works" translates *he pistis eteleiothe*, and the verb is *teleioo*.
- **2:23** The words are from Genesis 15:6, and "a friend of God" is as in Isaiah 41:8 and 2 Chronicles 20:7.
- **2:24** "A person is declared righteous by works and not by faith alone" is rendered as written.
- **2:25** "Rahab the prostitute" is rendered as written, and see Joshua 2:1-21.
- **2:26** "As the body apart from the spirit is dead, so also faith apart from works is dead" is rendered as written. See entry G-PNEUMA.
- **3:1** "Not many of you should become teachers" is rendered as written.
- **3:2** "A complete person" translates *teleios aner*, and "able also to bridle his whole body" is rendered as written.
- **3:6** "A world of unrighteousness" and "the entire course of life" translate *ho kosmos tes adikias* and *ton trochon tes geneseos*, and the Greek is hard, and *trochos* may be read "wheel" or "course", and "Gehenna" translates *geennes*, and it is the only use of the word in the New Testament outside the Gospels.
- **3:9** "People who are made in the likeness of God" is rendered as written, and see Genesis 1:26.
- **3:13** "The meekness of wisdom" is rendered as written.
- **3:14** "Be false to the truth" is rendered as written. See entry G-ALETHEIA.
- **3:15** "Demonic" translates *daimoniodes*.
- **3:17** "The wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable" is rendered as written. See entry G-ELEOS.
- **3:18** "A harvest of righteousness is sown in peace by those who make peace" is rendered as written. See entries G-DIKAIOSUNE and G-EIRENE.
- **4:4** "You adulterous people" is the reading of the critical text, and the Byzantine tradition adds "adulterers and" before it, and the footnote records the matter.
- **4:5** "He yearns jealously over the spirit that he has made to dwell in us" is rendered as written, and the words are not from a known text, and the Greek may be read "the spirit that dwells in us yearns jealously" or "the spirit that he made to dwell in us, he yearns for jealously", and the footnote records the matter. See entry G-PNEUMA.
- **4:6** The words are from Proverbs 3:34 as in the Septuagint. See entry G-CHARIS.
- **4:8** "You double-minded" translates *dipsuchoi*.
- **4:11** "Speaks evil against the law and judges the law" is rendered as written. See entry G-NOMOS.
- **4:12** "There is only one lawgiver and judge, he who is able to save and to destroy" is rendered as written. See entry G-SOZO.
- **4:14** "What is your life? For you are a mist" is the reading that the draft follows, and the Greek and the manuscripts differ in the wording ("for it is a mist"), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use. See entry G-ZOE.
- **4:17** "Whoever knows the right thing to do and fails to do it, for him it is sin" is rendered as written.
- **5:1** The words on the rich in verses 1 to 6 are rendered as written.
- **5:4** "The Lord of hosts" translates *kuriou sabaoth*, and it is the only use in the New Testament apart from Romans 9:29.
- **5:6** "The righteous person" may mean Jesus or any righteous person, and "he does not resist you" is rendered as written.
- **5:7** "The coming of the Lord" translates *parousias*, and is rendered "coming", as in the Thessalonian letters.
- **5:11** "Blessed" translates *makarizomen*, and "very compassionate" translates *polusplanchnos*, and is not tagged G-SPLANCHNIZOMAI. See entries G-MAKARIOS and G-ELEOS.
- **5:12** "Do not swear" is rendered as written, and see Matthew 5:33-37.
- **5:14** "Let him call for the elders of the assembly, and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord" is rendered as written. See entry G-EKKLESIA.
- **5:15** "The prayer of faith will save the one who is sick, and the Lord will raise him up" is rendered as written, and "save" (*sosei*) may mean heal or save, and "raise up" may mean from the sickbed or at the resurrection. See entries G-PISTIS and G-SOZO.
- **5:16** "The prayer of a righteous person has great power as it is working" is rendered as written, and "as it is working" translates *energoumene*, and may be read as a middle or a passive. See entry G-DIKAIOSUNE.
- **5:17** "Elijah was a person with a nature like ours" is rendered as written, and see 1 Kings 17 and 18.
- **5:20** "Will save his soul from death and will cover a multitude of sins" is the reading that the draft follows, and the manuscripts differ on "his soul", and whose soul and whose sins are meant is debated, and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use. See entries G-SOZO and G-PSYCHE.
