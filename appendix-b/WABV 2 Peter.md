# WABV: 2 Peter

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-DOXA\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Second Peter is the twenty-second book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the third of the seven general letters. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It is a letter from "Simeon Peter, a slave and apostle of Jesus Christ" (1:1), who says that he is soon to die (1:13-15), that he was with Jesus on the holy mountain (1:16-18), and that this is his second letter (3:1), and it warns against false teachers and against those who scoff at the promise of the coming of the Lord. Its authorship is among the most debated of any book of the New Testament: some scholars hold it to be by Peter and some to have been written in his name after his death, and its Greek is more elaborate than that of 1 Peter, and the WABV text does not decide. It shares much of its second chapter with the letter of Jude (compare 2:1-18 with Jude 4-13), and the footnote records the matter. It speaks of Paul's letters as scripture that is hard to understand (3:15-16). The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text of 2 Peter is drafted with the voice standard (Part 46 of the review package). Where the text of a critical edition is not certain, the draft says so in the footnote and asks the team to confirm the reading. It makes a number of fixed choices that follow from its Greek, and they are written down here so that they are used throughout. *Theias phuseos koinonoi* (1:4) is rendered "partakers of the divine nature". *Parousia* (1:16, 3:4, 3:12) is rendered "coming". *Tartaroo* (2:4) is rendered "cast into Tartarus". *Haireseis* (2:1) is rendered "destructive opinions". *Hagios* is rendered "holy" and is tagged G-HAGIOS. *Pistis* is rendered "faith" and is tagged G-PISTIS. *Agape* is rendered "love" and is tagged G-AGAPE, and *philadelphia* (1:7) "brotherly affection" and is not tagged. *Charis* is rendered "grace" and is tagged G-CHARIS. *Eirene* is rendered "peace" and is tagged G-EIRENE. *Doxa* is rendered "glory" and is tagged G-DOXA. *Zoe* is rendered "life" and is tagged G-ZOE. *Dikaiosune* and *dikaios* are rendered "righteousness" and "righteous" and are tagged G-DIKAIOSUNE. *Aletheia* is rendered "truth" and is tagged G-ALETHEIA. *Basileia* is rendered "kingdom" and is tagged G-BASILEIA. *Metanoia* is rendered "repentance" and is tagged G-METANOIA. *Soteria* is rendered "salvation" and is tagged G-SOZO. *Psyche* is rendered "soul" and is tagged G-PSYCHE. *Pneuma* is rendered "Spirit" and is tagged G-PNEUMA. *Anthropos* is rendered "person" or "people". *Adelphoi* is rendered "brothers", and the footnote records that in many places it may include the sisters, and the team should decide. *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Chapters 1 to 3, the divine nature, false teachers and the day of the Lord

### Chapter 1

**1** Simeon Peter, a slave and apostle of Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], to those who have obtained a faith \[G-PISTIS\] of equal standing with ours by the righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] of our God and Saviour Jesus Christ:

**2** grace \[G-CHARIS\] and peace \[G-EIRENE\] be multiplied to you in the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.

**3** His divine power has granted to us all things that pertain to life \[G-ZOE\] and godliness, through the knowledge of him who called us to his own glory \[G-DOXA\] and excellence,

**4** by which he has granted to us his precious and very great promises, so that through them you may become partakers of the divine nature, having escaped from the corruption that is in the world because of sinful desire.

**5** For this very reason, make every effort to supplement your faith \[G-PISTIS\] with excellence, and excellence with knowledge,

**6** and knowledge with self-control, and self-control with steadfastness, and steadfastness with godliness,

**7** and godliness with brotherly affection, and brotherly affection with love \[G-AGAPE\].

**8** For if these qualities are yours and are increasing, they keep you from being ineffective or unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**9** For whoever lacks these qualities is so nearsighted that he is blind, having forgotten that he was cleansed from his former sins.

**10** Therefore, brothers, be all the more diligent to make your calling and election sure, for if you practise these qualities you will never fall.

**11** For in this way there will be richly provided for you an entrance into the eternal kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.

**12** Therefore I intend always to remind you of these qualities, though you know them and are established in the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] that you have.

**13** I think it right, as long as I am in this body, to stir you up by way of reminder,

**14** since I know that the putting off of my body will be soon, as our Lord Jesus Christ made clear to me.

**15** And I will make every effort so that after my departure you may be able at any time to recall these things.

**16** For we did not follow cleverly devised myths when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, but we were eyewitnesses of his majesty.

**17** For when he received honour and glory \[G-DOXA\] from God the Father, and the voice was borne to him by the Majestic Glory, "This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased,"

**18** we ourselves heard this very voice borne from heaven, for we were with him on the holy \[G-HAGIOS\] mountain.

**19** And we have something more sure, the prophetic word, to which you will do well to pay attention as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts,

**20** knowing this first of all, that no prophecy of scripture comes from someone's own interpretation.

**21** For no prophecy was ever produced by human will, but people spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy \[G-HAGIOS\] Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

### Chapter 2

**1** But false prophets also arose among the people, just as there will be false teachers among you, who will secretly bring in destructive opinions, even denying the Master who bought them, bringing upon themselves swift destruction.

**2** And many will follow their sensuality, and because of them the way of the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] will be blasphemed.

**3** And in their greed they will exploit you with false words. Their condemnation from long ago is not idle, and their destruction is not asleep.

**4** For if God did not spare angels when they sinned, but cast them into Tartarus and committed them to chains of gloomy darkness to be kept until the judgement,

**5** if he did not spare the ancient world, but preserved Noah, a herald of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\], with seven others, when he brought a flood upon the world of the ungodly,

**6** if by turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to ashes he condemned them to extinction, making them an example of what is going to happen to the ungodly,

**7** and if he rescued righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] Lot, greatly distressed by the sensual conduct of the lawless

**8** (for as that righteous man lived among them day after day, he was tormenting his righteous soul \[G-PSYCHE\] over their lawless deeds that he saw and heard),

**9** then the Lord knows how to rescue the godly from trials, and to keep the unrighteous under punishment until the day of judgement,

**10** and especially those who indulge in the lust of defiling passion and despise authority. Bold and wilful, they do not tremble as they blaspheme the glorious ones \[G-DOXA\],

**11** whereas angels, though greater in might and power, do not pronounce a blasphemous judgement against them before the Lord.

**12** But these, like irrational animals, creatures of instinct, born to be caught and destroyed, blaspheming about matters of which they are ignorant, will also be destroyed in their destruction,

**13** suffering wrong as the wage for their wrongdoing. They count it pleasure to revel in the daytime. They are blots and blemishes, reveling in their deceptions, while they feast with you.

**14** They have eyes full of adultery, insatiable for sin. They entice unsteady souls. They have hearts trained in greed. Accursed children.

**15** Forsaking the right way, they have gone astray. They have followed the way of Balaam, the son of Bosor, who loved the wages of unrighteousness,

**16** but was rebuked for his own transgression. A speechless donkey spoke with human voice and restrained the prophet's madness.

**17** These are waterless springs and mists driven by a storm. For them the gloom of utter darkness has been reserved.

**18** For, speaking loud boasts of folly, they entice by sensual passions of the flesh those who are barely escaping from those who live in error.

**19** They promise them freedom, but they themselves are slaves of corruption. For whatever overcomes a person, to that he is enslaved.

**20** For if, after they have escaped the defilements of the world through the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, they are again entangled in them and overcome, the last state has become worse for them than the first.

**21** For it would have been better for them never to have known the way of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] than after knowing it to turn back from the holy \[G-HAGIOS\] commandment delivered to them.

**22** What the true proverb says has happened to them: "The dog returns to its own vomit," and, "The sow, after washing, returns to wallow in the mire."

### Chapter 3

**1** This is now the second letter that I am writing to you, beloved \[G-AGAPE\]. In both of them I am stirring up your sincere mind by way of reminder,

**2** that you should remember the predictions of the holy \[G-HAGIOS\] prophets and the commandment of the Lord and Saviour through your apostles,

**3** knowing this first of all, that scoffers will come in the last days with scoffing, following their own sinful desires.

**4** They will say, "Where is the promise of his coming? For ever since the fathers fell asleep, all things are continuing as they were from the beginning of creation."

**5** For they deliberately overlook this fact, that the heavens existed long ago, and the earth was formed out of water and through water by the word of God,

**6** and that by means of these the world that then existed was deluged with water and perished.

**7** But by the same word the heavens and earth that now exist are stored up for fire, being kept until the day of judgement and destruction of the ungodly.

**8** But do not overlook this one fact, beloved, that with the Lord one day is as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.

**9** The Lord is not slow to fulfil his promise as some count slowness, but is patient toward you, not wishing that any should perish, but that all should reach repentance \[G-METANOIA\].

**10** But the day of the Lord will come like a thief, and then the heavens will pass away with a roar, and the heavenly bodies will be burned up and dissolved, and the earth and the works that are done on it will be exposed.

**11** Since all these things are thus to be dissolved, what sort of people ought you to be in lives of holiness \[G-HAGIOS\] and godliness,

**12** waiting for and hastening the coming of the day of God, because of which the heavens will be set on fire and dissolved, and the heavenly bodies will melt as they burn.

**13** But according to his promise we are waiting for new heavens and a new earth in which righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] dwells.

**14** Therefore, beloved, since you are waiting for these, be diligent to be found by him without spot or blemish, and at peace \[G-EIRENE\].

**15** And count the patience of our Lord as salvation \[G-SOZO\], just as our beloved brother Paul also wrote to you according to the wisdom given him,

**16** as he does in all his letters when he speaks in them of these matters. There are some things in them that are hard to understand, which the ignorant and unstable twist to their own destruction, as they do the other scriptures.

**17** You therefore, beloved, knowing this beforehand, take care that you are not carried away with the error of the lawless and lose your own stability.

**18** But grow in the grace \[G-CHARIS\] and knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. To him be the glory \[G-DOXA\] both now and to the day of eternity. Amen.

*Review note: 2 Peter 1 to 3 were drafted in WABV wording, from the Greek, using the fixed choices set out in the introduction to this book, and with the WABV voice standard (Part 46 of the review package) applied. They are the greeting (1:1-2), the divine power and the promises, and the call to add to faith (1:3-11), the reminder of Peter near his death (1:12-15), the eyewitnesses of the majesty on the holy mountain (1:16-18), the prophetic word (1:19-21), the false teachers and their coming judgement (2:1-3), the examples of the angels, the flood and Sodom and Gomorrah and Lot (2:4-10), the character and the fate of the false teachers (2:10-22), the scoffers and the promise of the coming (3:1-7), the patience of the Lord and the day of the Lord (3:8-13) and the closing exhortation (3:14-18). The numbering is the usual numbering. No new G- marker is used, and the markers are G-CHRISTOS (1:1), G-PISTIS (1:1-5), G-DIKAIOSUNE (1:1, 2:5-21, 3:13), G-CHARIS (1:2, 3:18), G-EIRENE (1:2, 3:14), G-ZOE (1:3), G-DOXA (1:3, 1:17, 2:10, 3:18), G-AGAPE (1:7, 3:1), G-BASILEIA (1:11), G-ALETHEIA (1:12, 2:2), G-HAGIOS (1:18-21, 2:21, 3:2, 3:11), G-PNEUMA (1:21), G-PSYCHE (2:8), G-METANOIA (3:9) and G-SOZO (3:15). Several readings are drafted with some uncertainty and are to be confirmed against the critical edition in use: 1:3 (*idia doxe kai arete* or *dia doxes*), 1:10 (the Byzantine "through good works"), 1:12 (*mellesō* or another form), 1:21 (*hagioi theou anthropoi* or *apo theou*), 2:13 (*apatais* or *agapais*), 2:15 (*Bosor* or *Beor*) and 3:10 ("will be exposed" or other forms). The main points are these, and the footnotes carry the Greek: 1:1 "our God and Saviour Jesus Christ" has one article for "God" and "Saviour", and may be read as one person or two, as at Titus 2:13, and the rendering keeps the first reading; 1:4 "partakers of the divine nature" is rendered as written, with no comment on how it is read; 1:20 "no prophecy of scripture comes from someone's own interpretation" is rendered as written, and the footnote records that it may refer to the interpreter or to the origin of the prophecy, and the WABV text does not decide; 2:4 "Tartarus" is the only use in the Greek Bible of the word; 2:1-18 is closely linked with Jude 4-13, and the WABV text does not decide the direction of the borrowing; 3:8-13 the day of the Lord and the new heavens and earth are rendered as written; 3:15-16 "our beloved brother Paul" and "the other scriptures" are rendered as written. There are no departures from the critical Greek text in chapters 1 to 3. The Greek is hard in some places, including 1:1, 1:3-4, 1:19-21, 2:4, 2:12-14 and 3:10. The words on the divine nature (1:4), the eyewitnesses (1:16-18), the prophetic word (1:19-21), the false teachers (2:1-3), Lot (2:7-8), the scoffers (3:3-4), the thousand years (3:8), the day of the Lord (3:10), the new heavens and a new earth (3:13) and Paul's letters (3:15-16) are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. The section needs a check against the Greek by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked. With this the book of 2 Peter is complete in first draft.*

## Appendix A: Footnotes

- **1:1** "Simeon Peter" translates *Sumeon Petros*. "Our God and Saviour Jesus Christ" has one article for "God" and "Saviour", and may be read as one person or two, as at Titus 2:13, and the rendering keeps the first reading. See entries G-CHRISTOS, G-PISTIS and G-DIKAIOSUNE.
- **1:3** "To his own glory and excellence" is the reading that the draft follows (*idia doxe kai arete*), and some manuscripts read "through glory and excellence" (*dia doxes kai aretes*), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use. See entries G-ZOE and G-DOXA.
- **1:4** "Partakers of the divine nature" translates *theias koinonoi phuseos*, and it is rendered as written, with no comment on how it is read.
- **1:5** "Supplement your faith with excellence" and the list in verses 5 to 7 are rendered as written. See entry G-PISTIS.
- **1:7** "Brotherly affection" translates *philadelphian*, and is not tagged G-AGAPE. See entry G-AGAPE.
- **1:10** "Make your calling and election sure" is rendered as written, and the Byzantine tradition adds "through good works".
- **1:11** "The eternal kingdom" is rendered as written. See entry G-BASILEIA.
- **1:12** "I intend always to remind you" is the reading that the draft follows, and the manuscripts differ in the form of the verb, and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use. See entry G-ALETHEIA.
- **1:14** "The putting off of my body will be soon, as our Lord Jesus Christ made clear to me" is rendered as written, and see John 21:18-19.
- **1:16** "The power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ" translates *ten tou kuriou hemon Iesou Christou dunamin kai parousian*, and "coming" is as in the Thessalonian letters, and "eyewitnesses of his majesty" is rendered as written.
- **1:17** The words are as in Matthew 17:5. See entry G-DOXA.
- **1:18** "The holy mountain" is rendered as written, and see Matthew 17:1-8. See entry G-HAGIOS.
- **1:19** "The prophetic word" and "the morning star rises in your hearts" are rendered as written.
- **1:20** "No prophecy of scripture comes from someone's own interpretation" translates *pasa propheteia graphes idias epiluseos ou ginetai*, and may be read of the interpreter or of the origin of the prophecy, and the WABV text does not decide.
- **1:21** "People spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit" is the reading that the draft follows (*hagioi theou anthropoi*), and some manuscripts read "from God" (*apo theou*), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use. See entry G-PNEUMA.
- **2:1** "Destructive opinions" translates *haireseis apoleias*, and may be read "destructive heresies" or "destructive sects", and "the Master who bought them" is rendered as written. The second chapter is closely linked with Jude 4 to 13, and the WABV text does not decide the direction of the borrowing.
- **2:2** "The way of the truth" is rendered as written. See entry G-ALETHEIA.
- **2:4** "Cast them into Tartarus" translates *tartarosas*, and it is the only use of the word in the Greek Bible, and the angels are those of Genesis 6 or of the tradition of fallen angels, and the footnote records the matter.
- **2:5** "Noah, a herald of righteousness, with seven others" is rendered as written. See entry G-DIKAIOSUNE.
- **2:7** "Righteous Lot" is rendered as written, and see Genesis 19.
- **2:8** "Tormenting his righteous soul" is rendered as written. See entry G-PSYCHE.
- **2:10** "The glorious ones" translates *doxas*, and may mean angels or heavenly beings, and is as in Jude 8. See entry G-DOXA.
- **2:13** "Reveling in their deceptions" is the reading that the draft follows (*apatais*), and some manuscripts read "love feasts" (*agapais*), as in Jude 12, and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **2:15** "Balaam, the son of Bosor" is the reading that the draft follows, and some manuscripts read "Beor", and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use, and see Numbers 22 to 24.
- **2:16** "A speechless donkey spoke with human voice" is rendered as written.
- **2:19** "They promise them freedom, but they themselves are slaves of corruption" is rendered as written.
- **2:21** "The way of righteousness" and "the holy commandment" are rendered as written. See entries G-DIKAIOSUNE and G-HAGIOS.
- **2:22** The words are from Proverbs 26:11 and from a proverb of Ahikar.
- **3:1** "The second letter" may mean 1 Peter or a letter that is lost. See entry G-AGAPE.
- **3:2** "Through your apostles" is the reading of the critical text, and some manuscripts read "through us, the apostles", and the footnote records the matter. See entry G-HAGIOS.
- **3:4** "The promise of his coming" translates *he epangelia tes parousias autou*, and "the fathers fell asleep" may mean the first generation of believers or the fathers of Israel.
- **3:5** "The earth was formed out of water and through water by the word of God" is rendered as written.
- **3:8** "One day is as a thousand years" is from Psalm 90:4.
- **3:9** "The Lord is not slow to fulfil his promise" and "all should reach repentance" are rendered as written. See entry G-METANOIA.
- **3:10** "The day of the Lord will come like a thief" is as in 1 Thessalonians 5:2, and "the earth and the works that are done on it will be exposed" is the reading that the draft follows, and the manuscripts differ widely ("will be found", "will be burned up", "will not be found"), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **3:11** "Lives of holiness and godliness" is rendered as written. See entry G-HAGIOS.
- **3:12** "Hastening the coming of the day of God" may be read "waiting eagerly for", and "the heavenly bodies will melt as they burn" is rendered as written.
- **3:13** "New heavens and a new earth in which righteousness dwells" is from Isaiah 65:17 and 66:22. See entry G-DIKAIOSUNE.
- **3:15** "Count the patience of our Lord as salvation" and "our beloved brother Paul" are rendered as written. See entry G-SOZO.
- **3:16** "Some things in them that are hard to understand" and "the other scriptures" are rendered as written, and the "letters" may be a collection of the letters of Paul.
- **3:18** "Grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ" is rendered as written. See entries G-CHARIS and G-DOXA.
