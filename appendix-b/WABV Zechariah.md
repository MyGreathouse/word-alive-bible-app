# WABV: Zechariah

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[H-RUACH\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Zechariah is the eleventh of the twelve books of the Minor Prophets in the Protestant order that the WABV text follows. It is in Hebrew, in prose and poetry, and it is the longest of the books after Hosea. The book names Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, and dates its first messages to the second year of Darius (1:1, 1:7) and a later message to the fourth year (7:1), which are 520 to 518 before Christ, and the footnotes give the dates in the modern count. Chapters 1 to 8 are set in Jerusalem in the time of Zerubbabel and Joshua, with Haggai (Ezra 5:1-2), and chapters 9 to 14 are two undated oracles, each headed "an oracle" (9:1; 12:1). The WABV text does not decide on the questions of how the book grew or whether the whole is by one author, and the footnotes record only the language. The chapter and verse numbering is the English numbering. English 1:18-21 is Hebrew 2:1-4, so English 2:1-13 is Hebrew 2:5-17. The other chapters are numbered alike in English and Hebrew. The book falls into five parts in the WABV text: the call to return and the first four visions (chapters 1 to 3), the next four visions (4 to 6), the question about fasting and the promises for Jerusalem (7 and 8), the first oracle (9 to 11), and the second oracle (12 to 14). The headings of the parts are not in the Hebrew. Several lines are the same in the Hebrew as other books, and the wording of the lines that are the same is the same in the WABV text, as the footnotes record, in particular "the Branch" (3:8; 6:12) with Jeremiah 23:5 and 33:15, and "the signet ring" with Haggai 2:23 and Jeremiah 22:24. The Hebrew of Zechariah is mostly clear in the visions and very hard in places in chapters 9 to 14, and the footnotes record the other readings. The names of God are given as in the earlier books: "the LORD" for YHWH, "Lord" for Adonai, "the Lord GOD" for Adonai YHWH, and "the LORD of hosts" for YHWH tseva'ot.*

## Part One: Chapters 1 to 3, the call to return and the first visions

### Chapter 1

**1** In the eighth month of Darius's second year, the word of the LORD came to the prophet Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo:

**2** The LORD burned with anger against your fathers.

**3** So tell the people: This is what the LORD of hosts says. Return \[H-SHUV\] to me, declares the LORD of hosts, and I will return \[H-SHUV\] to you, says the LORD of hosts.

**4** Do not be like your fathers. The earlier prophets called out to them, "This is what the LORD of hosts says: Return \[H-SHUV\] from your evil ways and your evil deeds." But they would not listen, and they paid me no heed, declares the LORD.

**5** Your fathers, where are they now? And the prophets, did they live for ever?

**6** Yet my words and my statutes, which I gave to my servants the prophets, caught up with your fathers. They came back \[H-SHUV\] and said, "The LORD of hosts did to us exactly what he had planned, because of our ways and our deeds."

**7** On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, the month of Shebat, in Darius's second year, the word of the LORD came to the prophet Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo:

**8** In the night I saw a man riding a red horse. He stood among the myrtle trees in the hollow, and behind him were horses, red, brown and white.

**9** I asked, "Sir, what are these?" The angel who was speaking with me said, "I will show you what they are."

**10** The man standing among the myrtles explained, "These are the ones the LORD has sent to roam the earth."

**11** They reported to the angel of the LORD who stood among the myrtles, "We have roamed the earth, and the whole earth is still and at rest."

**12** Then the angel of the LORD said, "LORD of hosts, how long will you withhold compassion from Jerusalem and the towns of Judah, which have felt your anger these seventy years?"

**13** The LORD answered the angel who was speaking with me with kind words, words of comfort \[H-NACHAM\].

**14** Then the angel who was speaking with me said to me: Proclaim this. The LORD of hosts says: I am jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion, with a great jealousy.

**15** And I am fiercely angry with the nations that sit at ease. I was only a little angry, but they made the disaster worse.

**16** So the LORD says: I have come back \[H-SHUV\] to Jerusalem with compassion. My house will be built there, declares the LORD of hosts, and the measuring line will be stretched over Jerusalem.

**17** Proclaim again: The LORD of hosts says: My towns will again overflow with good things. The LORD will again comfort \[H-NACHAM\] Zion and again choose Jerusalem.

**18** I looked up and saw four horns.

**19** I asked the angel who was speaking with me, "What are these?" He answered, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, Israel and Jerusalem."

**20** Then the LORD showed me four metalworkers.

**21** I asked, "What have these come to do?" He said, "The horns scattered Judah so completely that no one could lift his head. These workers have come to terrify the horns, to cut down the horns of the nations that raised a horn against the land of Judah to scatter its people."

### Chapter 2

**1** I looked up and saw a man with a measuring line in his hand.

**2** I asked, "Where are you going?" He answered, "To measure Jerusalem, to find out how wide it is and how long."

**3** Then the angel who was speaking with me stepped forward, and another angel came out to meet him

**4** and said to him, "Run and tell that young man: Jerusalem will be a city without walls, spread out like villages, because of the many people and animals living in it.

**5** I myself will be a wall of fire all round her, declares the LORD, and I will be the glory within her."

**6** Up, up. Flee from the land of the north, declares the LORD. I have scattered you like the four winds \[H-RUACH\] of heaven, declares the LORD.

**7** Up, Zion. Escape, you who live with the daughter of Babylon.

**8** For this is what the LORD of hosts says: After glory he sent me against the nations that plundered you. Whoever touches you touches the pupil of his eye.

**9** See, I will raise my hand against them, and they will become loot for their own slaves. Then you will know that the LORD of hosts sent me.

**10** Sing and rejoice, daughter of Zion, for I am coming to live among you, declares the LORD.

**11** On that day many nations will attach themselves to the LORD and become my people. I will live among you, and you will know that the LORD of hosts sent me to you.

**12** The LORD will take Judah as his own share in the holy \[H-QADOSH\] land, and he will again choose Jerusalem.

**13** Be silent, all flesh, before the LORD, for he has stirred himself from his holy \[H-QADOSH\] dwelling.

### Chapter 3

**1** Then he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the LORD, with the adversary \[H-SATAN\] standing at his right to accuse him.

**2** The LORD said to the adversary, "The LORD rebuke you, adversary. The LORD, who has chosen Jerusalem, rebuke you. Is this man not a stick pulled from the fire?"

**3** Joshua was standing before the angel in filthy clothes.

**4** The angel spoke to those standing before him: "Take the filthy clothes off him." And to Joshua he said, "See, I have removed your guilt from you, and I will dress you in fine robes."

**5** I said, "Let them put a clean turban on his head." So they put a clean turban on his head and dressed him in robes, while the angel of the LORD stood by.

**6** Then the angel of the LORD gave Joshua this solemn charge:

**7** This is what the LORD of hosts says. If you walk in my ways and keep my charge, then you will govern my house and guard my courts, and I will give you a place to walk among these who are standing here.

**8** Listen, Joshua the high priest, you and your colleagues sitting before you, for they are people of good omen: I am bringing my servant the Branch.

**9** Look at the stone I have set before Joshua, a single stone with seven eyes. I will engrave its inscription, declares the LORD of hosts, and in one day I will remove the guilt of this land.

**10** On that day, declares the LORD of hosts, you will invite one another to sit under the vine and under the fig tree.

*Review note: Zechariah 1 to 3 were drafted in WABV wording. They are the call to return (1:1-6), the first vision of the horsemen among the myrtles and the word of comfort (1:7-17), the second vision of the four horns and the four craftsmen (1:18-21), the third vision of the man with the measuring line and the call to flee from Babylon (2:1-13) and the fourth vision of Joshua the high priest before the angel of the LORD (3:1-10). The numbering is the English numbering: English 1:18-21 is Hebrew 2:1-4, so English 2:1-13 is Hebrew 2:5-17. Chapter 1 begins in prose, and the visions are in prose with oracles in poetry (for example 1:14-17, 2:6-13), and the verses are set in lines where the Hebrew is poetry. The dates in 1:1 and 1:7 are by Darius, and the footnotes record the dates in the modern count. In 1:3, 1:4, 1:6 and 1:16 forms of shuv ("return", "turned", "have returned") are tagged H-SHUV, and the words "return to me ... and I will return to you" in 1:3 are the same in the Hebrew as Malachi 3:7, and are to be given the same wording when Malachi is drafted. In 1:13 and 1:17 "comforting" and "comfort" translate nichumim and ve-nicham and are tagged H-NACHAM. In 2:6 "the four winds" translates arba ruchot and is tagged H-RUACH. In 2:12 and 2:13 "holy" is tagged H-QADOSH. In 3:1 and 3:2 "the adversary" translates ha-satan, with the article, and is tagged H-SATAN and given the same rendering as the WABV text of Job 1 and 2, and the marker still needs to be confirmed (see Job). In 3:8 "the Branch" translates tsemach and is given the same wording as Jeremiah 23:5 and 33:15 ("a righteous Branch") and Isaiah 4:2. The words "be silent, all flesh, before the LORD" in 2:13 are the same in the Hebrew as Habakkuk 2:20 and Zephaniah 1:7, and the words "be silent before" are given the same wording. The seventy years in 1:12 are as in Jeremiah 25:11-12 and 29:10. There are no departures from the Hebrew in chapters 1 to 3. The Hebrew is hard in some places, including 1:8 (*ha-metsulah*, "the hollow"), 1:10 (the horses), 2:8 (*acharei kavod*, "after glory"), 2:12, 3:4 and 3:7 (*mahlekhim*, "the right of access"). The footnotes record the main differences and the uncertain terms. The words on the accusing of the high priest and the taking away of his iniquity (3:1-5) are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. The section needs a check against the Hebrew by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked.*

## Part Two: Chapters 4 to 6, the lampstand, the scroll, the chariots and the crown

### Chapter 4

**1** The angel who was speaking with me came back and woke me, as a sleeper is woken.

**2** He asked, "What do you see?" I said, "I see a lampstand made entirely of gold, with a bowl on top, and seven lamps on it, each of the lamps on top having seven spouts.

**3** Beside it stand two olive trees, one to the right of the bowl and one to its left."

**4** I asked the angel who was speaking with me, "Sir, what are these?"

**5** The angel who was speaking with me answered, "Do you not know what these are?" I said, "No, sir."

**6** He said to me, "This is the word of the LORD to Zerubbabel: Not by force, not by strength, but by my Spirit \[H-RUACH\], says the LORD of hosts.

**7** Who are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you will be level ground. He will bring out the top stone to shouts of 'Favour, favour to it.'"

**8** The word of the LORD came to me:

**9** "Zerubbabel's hands laid the foundation of this house, and Zerubbabel's hands will finish it. Then you will know that the LORD of hosts sent me to you.

**10** Who has despised the day of small things? They will rejoice when they see the plumb stone in Zerubbabel's hand. These seven are the eyes of the LORD, ranging over the whole earth."

**11** I asked him, "What are these two olive trees to the right and left of the lampstand?"

**12** And again I asked him, "What are these two olive branches beside the two golden spouts that pour out the golden oil?"

**13** He said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" I said, "No, sir."

**14** He said, "These are the two who are anointed with fresh oil, who stand beside the Lord of the whole earth."

### Chapter 5

**1** I looked up again and saw a scroll flying.

**2** He asked me, "What do you see?" I said, "I see a flying scroll, twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide."

**3** He said to me, "This is the curse going out over the whole land. Every thief will be swept away, as the writing on one side says, and everyone who swears falsely will be swept away, as the writing on the other side says.

**4** I have sent it out, declares the LORD of hosts. It will enter the house of the thief and the house of the one who swears falsely by my name, and it will stay inside his house and destroy it, timber and stones together."

**5** The angel who was speaking with me came forward and said to me, "Look up and see what is coming out."

**6** I asked, "What is it?" He said, "It is the basket coming out." And he said, "This is how they look throughout the land."

**7** Then a disc of lead was lifted, and there sat a woman inside the basket.

**8** He said, "This is Wickedness." He pushed her down into the basket and shoved the lead weight over its mouth.

**9** I looked up and saw two women coming, with the wind \[H-RUACH\] in their wings. They had wings like a stork's, and they lifted the basket between earth and sky.

**10** I asked the angel who was speaking with me, "Where are they taking the basket?"

**11** He said to me, "To the land of Shinar, to build a house for it. When it is ready, the basket will be set down there on its own base."

### Chapter 6

**1** Again I looked up and saw four chariots coming out from between two mountains, and the mountains were mountains of bronze.

**2** The first chariot had red horses, the second black horses,

**3** the third white horses, and the fourth dappled horses, all of them powerful.

**4** I asked the angel who was speaking with me, "Sir, what are these?"

**5** The angel answered me, "These are the four winds \[H-RUACH\] of heaven, going out after standing before the Lord of the whole earth.

**6** The chariot with the black horses is going to the northern land, the white follow behind them, and the dappled are going to the southern land."

**7** The powerful horses came out, eager to go and roam the earth. He said, "Go and roam the earth." And they roamed the earth.

**8** Then he called out to me, "Look, those going to the northern land have set my Spirit \[H-RUACH\] at rest in the northern land."

**9** The word of the LORD came to me:

**10** "Take silver and gold from the exiles, from Heldai, Tobijah and Jedaiah, who have come from Babylon, and go that same day to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah.

**11** Make crowns from the silver and gold, and put them on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

**12** Tell him: This is what the LORD of hosts says: Here is a man whose name is the Branch. He will sprout up from where he is, and he will build the temple of the LORD.

**13** He is the one who will build the temple of the LORD. He will carry royal splendour, and sit and rule on his throne. And there will be a priest on his throne, and the counsel of peace \[H-SHALOM\] will be between the two of them.

**14** The crowns will stay in the temple of the LORD as a reminder to Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah and Hen son of Zephaniah.

**15** Those who are far away will come and help build the temple of the LORD. Then you will know that the LORD of hosts sent me to you. This will happen if you truly obey the voice of the LORD your God."

*Review note: Zechariah 4 to 6 were drafted in WABV wording. They are the fifth vision of the lampstand and the two olive trees, with the word to Zerubbabel (4:1-14), the sixth vision of the flying scroll (5:1-4), the seventh vision of the woman in the basket (5:5-11), the eighth vision of the four chariots (6:1-8) and the crowns for Joshua and the Branch (6:9-15). The numbering is the same as the Hebrew. The visions are in prose, and the oracles are set in lines where the Hebrew is poetry (4:6-7, 4:9-10, 6:12-13). In 4:6 "my Spirit" translates ruchi and is tagged H-RUACH, and in 6:8 "my Spirit" is the same, and the capital letter is the draft's choice, as in Isaiah 63:10-11, Ezekiel 11:5, Joel 2:28-29 and Haggai 2:5. In 6:12 "the Branch" translates tsemach, and is given the same wording as 3:8, Jeremiah 23:5 and 33:15 and Isaiah 4:2. ## Part Three: Chapters 7 and 8, the question about fasting and the promises for Jerusalem

### Chapter 7

**1** In the fourth year of King Darius, on the fourth day of the ninth month, Chislev, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah.

**2** Bethel had sent Sharezer and Regemmelech and their men to seek the LORD's favour,

**3** and to ask the priests of the house of the LORD of hosts, and the prophets: "Should I go on weeping and fasting in the fifth month, as I have done for so many years?"

**4** Then the word of the LORD of hosts came to me:

**5** Tell all the people of the land and the priests: For these seventy years you fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh months. Was it really for me that you fasted?

**6** And when you eat and drink, is it not you who are eating and you who are drinking?

**7** Are these not the words the LORD proclaimed through the earlier prophets, when Jerusalem was settled and prosperous, with her towns around her, and the Negeb and the lowland were settled too?

**8** The word of the LORD came to Zechariah again:

**9** This is what the LORD of hosts says: Judge with true judgement. Show faithful love \[H-HESED\] and compassion to one another.

**10** Do not exploit the widow or the orphan, the foreigner or the poor, and do not plot evil against each other in your hearts.

**11** But they refused to listen. They turned a stubborn shoulder and blocked their ears so they could not hear.

**12** They made their hearts hard as flint, so that they could not hear the teaching \[H-TORAH\] and the words the LORD of hosts had sent by his Spirit \[H-RUACH\] through the earlier prophets. So the LORD of hosts burned with great anger.

**13** "Just as I called and they would not listen, so they will call and I will not listen," says the LORD of hosts.

**14** "I scattered them in a whirlwind among all the nations they had never known. The land was left empty behind them, with no one passing through, and they turned a pleasant land into a wasteland."

### Chapter 8

**1** The word of the LORD of hosts came:

**2** This is what the LORD of hosts says: I am jealous for Zion, with great jealousy. I am jealous for her with great fury.

**3** This is what the LORD says: I have come back \[H-SHUV\] to Zion and will live in Jerusalem. Jerusalem will be called the faithful \[H-EMET\] city, and the mountain of the LORD of hosts the holy \[H-QADOSH\] mountain.

**4** This is what the LORD of hosts says: Old men and women will again sit in Jerusalem's squares, each leaning on a staff, so old they are.

**5** The city's squares will be crowded with boys and girls at play.

**6** This is what the LORD of hosts says: It may seem impossible to the remnant of this people in those days, but will it seem impossible to me? declares the LORD of hosts.

**7** This is what the LORD of hosts says: I will rescue my people from the lands of the east and the west,

**8** and bring them back to live in Jerusalem. They will be my people, and I will be their God, in faithfulness \[H-EMET\] and in righteousness \[H-TSEDAQAH\].

**9** This is what the LORD of hosts says: Be strong, you who now hear these words from the prophets, the same words they spoke on the day the foundation of the house of the LORD of hosts was laid, so that the temple could be built.

**10** Before that time there were no wages for people or animals, and no one could come or go in safety because of the enemy, for I set everyone against his neighbour.

**11** But now I will not treat the remnant of this people as I did in the past, declares the LORD of hosts.

**12** Peace \[H-SHALOM\] will be sown: the vine will yield its fruit, the soil its harvest, and the heavens their dew. I will give all of this to the remnant of this people.

**13** You, house of Judah and house of Israel, have been a curse among the nations. So I will save you, and you will be a blessing. Do not fear \[H-YIRAH\]. Be strong.

**14** For this is what the LORD of hosts says: I resolved to bring disaster on you when your fathers provoked me, and I did not relent \[H-NACHAM\], says the LORD of hosts.

**15** In the same way I have now resolved to do good to Jerusalem and the house of Judah. Do not fear \[H-YIRAH\].

**16** This is what you are to do: Speak truth \[H-EMET\] to one another. Give judgement in your gates that is true and brings peace \[H-SHALOM\].

**17** Do not plot evil against each other in your hearts, and do not love \[H-AHAV\] a false oath, for I hate all of these things, declares the LORD.

**18** The word of the LORD of hosts came to me:

**19** This is what the LORD of hosts says: The fasts of the fourth, fifth, seventh and tenth months will become joy and gladness, happy festivals for the house of Judah. So love \[H-AHAV\] truth \[H-EMET\] and peace \[H-SHALOM\].

**20** This is what the LORD of hosts says: Peoples will yet come, the inhabitants of many cities.

**21** The inhabitants of one city will go to another and say, "Let us go at once to seek the LORD's favour and to look for the LORD of hosts. I am going too."

**22** Many peoples and strong nations will come to Jerusalem to look for the LORD of hosts and to seek the LORD's favour.

**23** This is what the LORD of hosts says: In those days ten people from nations of every language will take hold of one Judean by the hem of his robe and say, "Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you."

## Part Four: Chapters 9 to 11, the first oracle

### Chapter 9

**1** An oracle. The LORD's word is against the land of Hadrach and rests on Damascus, for the eyes of humankind and of all the tribes of Israel are on the LORD,

**2** and on Hamath, which borders it, and on Tyre and Sidon, for all their great skill.

**3** Tyre built herself a stronghold, piled up silver like dust and gold like street mud.

**4** But the Lord will dispossess her and hurl her power into the sea, and fire will devour her.

**5** Ashkelon will see it and be afraid \[H-YIRAH\]. Gaza will writhe in pain, and so will Ekron, for her hope has withered. Gaza will lose her king, and Ashkelon will be empty of people.

**6** A people of mixed blood will settle in Ashdod, and I will cut down the pride of the Philistines.

**7** I will take the blood out of their mouths and the abominations from between their teeth. Those who remain will belong to our God and become a clan in Judah, and Ekron will be like the Jebusites.

**8** I will camp around my house as its guard, so that no one passes through. No oppressor will overrun them again, for now I am watching with my own eyes.

**9** Celebrate loudly, daughter of Zion. Shout in triumph, daughter of Jerusalem. See, your king is coming to you, righteous \[H-TSEDAQAH\] and victorious, humble, riding on a donkey, on a colt, a donkey's foal.

**10** I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim and the war horse from Jerusalem. The bow of war will be cut off. He will speak peace \[H-SHALOM\] to the nations, and his rule will reach from sea to sea, from the River to the ends of the earth.

**11** And you, because of the blood of my covenant \[H-BERIT\] with you, I will free your prisoners from the pit with no water.

**12** Come back \[H-SHUV\] to the fortress, prisoners of hope. Today I declare that I will repay you double \[H-SHUV\].

**13** I have bent Judah like a bow and fitted Ephraim like an arrow. I will rouse your sons, Zion, against your sons, Greece, and make you a warrior's sword.

**14** The LORD will appear above them, and his arrow will flash like lightning. The Lord GOD will blow the horn and march out in the storms of the south.

**15** The LORD of hosts will shield them. They will devour and trample the slingstones, and they will drink and roar as if drunk on wine, filled like a bowl, drenched like the corners of the altar.

**16** On that day the LORD their God will save them, his people, like a flock. They will sparkle like jewels in a crown over his land.

**17** How good and how lovely it will be: grain will make the young men flourish, and new wine the young women.

### Chapter 10

## Part Five: Chapters 12 to 14, the second oracle

### Chapter 12

**1** An oracle. The LORD's word about Israel. A declaration of the LORD, who spread out the heavens, laid the foundation of the earth, and shaped the spirit \[H-RUACH\] within each person:

**2** I am making Jerusalem a cup that sends all the surrounding peoples reeling. Judah too will be caught up in the siege of Jerusalem.

**3** On that day I will make Jerusalem a boulder too heavy for any of the peoples. Everyone who tries to lift it will tear himself to pieces, and all the nations of the earth will gather against it.

**4** On that day, declares the LORD, I will strike every horse with terror and every rider with madness. But over the house of Judah I will keep my eyes open, while I strike every horse of the peoples blind.

**5** Then the clans of Judah will say to themselves, "The people of Jerusalem are strong, because the LORD of hosts is their God."

**6** On that day I will make the clans of Judah like a firepot among logs, like a burning torch among cut grain. They will burn up the peoples around them, right and left, and Jerusalem will go on living where she stands, in Jerusalem.

**7** The LORD will save the tents of Judah first, so that the splendour of David's house and of the people of Jerusalem will not rise above Judah's.

**8** On that day the LORD will shield the people of Jerusalem. Even the weakest among them will be like David on that day, and David's house will be like God, like the angel of the LORD at their head.

**9** On that day I will set out to destroy every nation that comes against Jerusalem.

**10** I will pour out on David's house and on the people of Jerusalem a spirit \[H-RUACH\] of favour and pleading. They will look to me, whom they have pierced, and mourn over him as people mourn for an only child, and grieve bitterly over him as people grieve for a firstborn.

**11** On that day the mourning in Jerusalem will be as great as the mourning at Hadad-rimmon in the valley of Megiddo.

**12** The land will mourn, every clan on its own: the clan of David's house on its own, and their wives on their own; the clan of Nathan's house on its own, and their wives on their own;

**13** the clan of Levi's house on its own, and their wives on their own; the clan of the Shimeites on its own, and their wives on their own;

**14** and all the other clans, each on its own, and their wives on their own.

### Chapter 13

**1** On that day a spring will be opened for David's house and for the people of Jerusalem, to wash away sin and impurity.

**2** On that day, declares the LORD of hosts, I will wipe the names of the idols from the land, and no one will remember them. I will also drive the prophets \[H-NAVI\] and the spirit \[H-RUACH\] of impurity out of the land.

**3** If anyone still prophesies, his own father and mother will say to him, "You will not live, because you have spoken falsehood in the LORD's name." And his father and mother, who gave him birth, will stab him as he prophesies.

**4** On that day each prophet will be ashamed of his vision as he prophesies. He will not put on the hairy cloak to deceive,

**5** but will say, "I am no prophet \[H-NAVI\]. I work the soil, for a man has owned me since I was young."

**6** And if someone asks him, "What are these wounds between your arms?" he will say, "I was wounded in the house of those who love \[H-AHAV\] me."

**7** Sword, wake up against my shepherd, against the man who stands beside me, declares the LORD of hosts. Strike the shepherd, and the flock will scatter. I will turn \[H-SHUV\] my hand against the little ones.

**8** Throughout the land, declares the LORD, two parts will be cut off and die, and one part will be left.

**9** I will bring that third through the fire, refine them as silver is refined, and test them as gold is tested. They will call on my name, and I will answer them. I will say, "They are my people," and they will say, "The LORD is my God."

### Chapter 14

**1** A day is coming for the LORD, when the loot taken from you will be shared out among you.

**2** I will gather all the nations to fight against Jerusalem. The city will fall, the houses will be looted, and the women will be raped. Half the city will go into exile, but the rest of the people will not be removed from the city.

**3** Then the LORD will go out and fight those nations, as he fights on a day of battle.

**4** On that day his feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem. The Mount of Olives will split in two from east to west, leaving a huge valley, as half the mountain shifts north and half south.

**5** You will flee through my mountain valley, for the valley between the mountains will reach as far as Azal. You will flee as you fled from the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. And the LORD my God will come, with all the holy ones \[H-QADOSH\] beside him.

**6** On that day there will be no light; the bright ones will thicken and stiffen.

**7** It will be a day unlike any other, known to the LORD alone, neither day nor night. And at evening there will be light.

**8** On that day living water will flow out of Jerusalem, half toward the eastern sea and half toward the western sea, in summer and in winter alike.

**9** The LORD will be king over the whole earth. On that day the LORD will be one, and his name one.

**10** The whole land will be flattened into a plain, from Geba to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem. But Jerusalem will stand high in her place, from Benjamin's Gate to the First Gate, to the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of Hananel to the king's winepresses.

**11** People will live in her, and there will be no more ban of destruction \[H-CHEREM\]. Jerusalem will live in safety.

**12** This is the plague with which the LORD will strike every people that has gone to war against Jerusalem: each one's flesh will waste away as he stands on his feet, his eyes will waste away in their sockets, and his tongue will waste away in his mouth.

**13** On that day a great confusion from the LORD will come over them. Each will grip his neighbour's hand, and each will turn his hand against his neighbour.

**14** Judah too will fight in Jerusalem. The wealth of all the nations around will be gathered in, gold and silver and clothing in huge amounts.

**15** The same plague will strike horse and mule, camel and donkey, and every animal in those camps.

**16** Then all who are left of the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up every year to bow down to the King, the LORD of hosts, and to celebrate the Feast of Booths.

**17** Any of the families of the earth that do not go up to Jerusalem to bow down to the King, the LORD of hosts, will have no rain.

**18** If the family of Egypt does not go up and come in, they too will have no rain. The plague with which the LORD strikes the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Feast of Booths will fall on them.

**19** This will be the penalty for Egypt, and the penalty for every nation that does not go up to celebrate the Feast of Booths.

**20** On that day the horses' bells will carry the words "Holy \[H-QADOSH\] to the LORD", and the cooking pots in the house of the LORD will be as holy as the bowls before the altar.

**21** Every pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be holy \[H-QADOSH\] to the LORD of hosts. All who come to sacrifice will take some and cook in them. And on that day there will be no more traders in the house of the LORD of hosts.

In 13:6 "those who love me" translates me'ahavai and is tagged H-AHAV.
