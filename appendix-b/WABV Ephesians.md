# WABV: Ephesians

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-EKKLESIA\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Ephesians is the tenth book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the fifth of the letters. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It is a letter that names Paul as its writer (1:1, 3:1), written from prison (3:1, 4:1, 6:20), and it is a letter of a different kind from Galatians and the Corinthian letters: it has no quarrel in it and no named person to greet, and its sentences are long and flowing, and the whole of 1:3-14 is a single sentence in the Greek, and 1:15-23 and 3:1-13 are almost as long. Two matters about the letter are debated, and the WABV text does not decide either. The first is the words "in Ephesus" at 1:1, which are missing from the oldest manuscripts and are placed in square brackets in the WABV text, and which have led many to think that the letter was a circular letter to several assemblies. The second is its authorship, which some scholars hold to be Paul and some a close follower of Paul, and the footnote records the matter. The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text of Ephesians is drafted with the voice standard (Part 46 of the review package), and keeps the build of the long sentences of the Greek but breaks them at the natural joints of their clauses, so that they can be read aloud and followed. It makes a number of fixed choices that follow from its Greek, and they are written down here so that they are used throughout. *Ekklesia* is rendered "assembly" and is tagged G-EKKLESIA, and the team should decide between "assembly" and "church". *Hagioi* is rendered "holy ones" and is tagged G-HAGIOS. *Charis* is rendered "grace" and is tagged G-CHARIS. *Pistis* is rendered "faith" and *pisteuo* "believe", and both are tagged G-PISTIS, and *pistos* is rendered "faithful". *Agape* is rendered "love" and is tagged G-AGAPE. *Eleos* is rendered "mercy" and is tagged G-ELEOS. *Apolutrosis* is rendered "redemption" and is tagged G-LYTRON. *Musterion* is rendered "mystery". *Oikonomia* is rendered "plan" or "stewardship", and the footnote records the matter. *Huiothesia* is rendered "adoption as sons". *Proorizo* is rendered "mark out beforehand", as in Romans. *Doxa* is rendered "glory" and is tagged G-DOXA. *Sozo* is rendered "save" and is tagged G-SOZO. *Pneuma* is rendered "Spirit" or "spirit" and is tagged G-PNEUMA. *Sarx* is rendered "flesh". *Nomos* is rendered "law" and is tagged G-NOMOS. *Eirene* is rendered "peace" and is tagged G-EIRENE. *Euangelion* is rendered "good news" and is tagged G-EUANGELION. *Diatheke* is rendered "covenant" and is tagged G-DIATHEKE. *Ethne* is rendered "the nations". *Adelphoi* is rendered "brothers", and the footnote records that in many places it may include the sisters, and the team should decide. *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. The book falls into two parts in the WABV text: the blessing, the prayer of thanksgiving, life in Christ, one new humanity, the mystery and the prayer for strength (chapters 1 to 3), and unity in the body, the old self and the new, walking in love and light, wives and husbands, children and parents, slaves and masters, and the armour of God (4 to 6). The headings of the parts are not in the Greek. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System, and the new ones are listed in the review note of the part where each first appears. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Part One: Chapters 1 to 3, the blessing, life in Christ, one new humanity and the mystery

### Chapter 1

**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] Jesus by the will of God, to the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\] who are \[in Ephesus and\] faithful in Christ Jesus:

**2** grace \[G-CHARIS\] to you and peace \[G-EIRENE\] from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**3** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places,

**4** just as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, to be holy and without blemish before him in love \[G-AGAPE\].

**5** He marked us out beforehand for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of his will,

**6** to the praise of the glory \[G-DOXA\] of his grace \[G-CHARIS\], with which he has favoured us in the Beloved.

**7** In him we have redemption \[G-LYTRON\] through his blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses, according to the riches of his grace \[G-CHARIS\],

**8** which he lavished on us, in all wisdom and insight,

**9** making known to us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure, which he set forth in Christ

**10** as a plan for the fullness of time, to bring everything together in Christ, things in heaven and things on earth.

**11** In him we have also received an inheritance, having been marked out beforehand according to the purpose of the one who works all things according to the counsel of his will,

**12** so that we who were the first to hope in Christ might be to the praise of his glory \[G-DOXA\].

**13** In him you also, when you heard the word of truth \[G-ALETHEIA\], the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of your salvation \[G-SOZO\], and believed \[G-PISTIS\] in him, were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\],

**14** who is the guarantee of our inheritance until we acquire possession of it, to the praise of his glory \[G-DOXA\].

**15** For this reason, because I have heard of your faith \[G-PISTIS\] in the Lord Jesus and your love \[G-AGAPE\] toward all the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\],

**16** I do not cease to give thanks for you, remembering you in my prayers,

**17** that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give you a spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him,

**18** having the eyes of your hearts enlightened, so that you may know what is the hope to which he has called you, what are the riches of his glorious inheritance among the holy ones,

**19** and what is the immeasurable greatness of his power toward us who believe \[G-PISTIS\], according to the working of his great might

**20** that he worked in Christ when he raised him from the dead and seated him at his right hand in the heavenly places,

**21** far above all rule and authority and power and dominion, and above every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the one to come.

**22** And he put all things under his feet and gave him as head over all things to the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\],

**23** which is his body, the fullness of him who fills all in all.

### Chapter 2

**1** And you were dead through your trespasses and sins

**2** in which you once walked, following the course of this world, following the ruler of the power of the air, the spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] that is now at work in the children of disobedience,

**3** among whom we all once lived in the passions of our flesh, carrying out the desires of the body and the mind, and were by nature children of wrath, like everyone else.

**4** But God, being rich in mercy \[G-ELEOS\], because of the great love \[G-AGAPE\] with which he loved us,

**5** even when we were dead through our trespasses, made us alive together with Christ (by grace \[G-CHARIS\] you have been saved \[G-SOZO\]),

**6** and raised us up with him and seated us with him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,

**7** so that in the coming ages he might show the surpassing riches of his grace \[G-CHARIS\] in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.

**8** For by grace \[G-CHARIS\] you have been saved \[G-SOZO\] through faith \[G-PISTIS\]. And this is not your own doing. It is the gift of God,

**9** not a result of works, so that no one may boast.

**10** For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them.

**11** Therefore remember that at one time you, the nations in the flesh, called "the uncircumcision" by what is called the circumcision, which is made in the flesh by hands,

**12** remember that you were at that time separated from Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], alienated from the commonwealth of Israel and strangers to the covenants \[G-DIATHEKE\] of promise, having no hope and without God in the world.

**13** But now in Christ Jesus you who once were far off have been brought near by the blood of Christ.

**14** For he himself is our peace \[G-EIRENE\], who has made both one and has broken down in his flesh the dividing wall of hostility,

**15** by abolishing the law \[G-NOMOS\] of commandments expressed in ordinances, so that he might create in himself one new humanity in place of the two, so making peace \[G-EIRENE\],

**16** and might reconcile us both to God in one body through the cross, thereby killing the hostility.

**17** And he came and proclaimed the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of peace \[G-EIRENE\] to you who were far off and peace to those who were near.

**18** For through him we both have access in one Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] to the Father.

**19** So then you are no longer strangers and aliens, but you are fellow citizens with the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\] and members of the household of God,

**20** built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus himself being the cornerstone,

**21** in whom the whole structure, being joined together, grows into a holy \[G-HAGIOS\] temple in the Lord.

**22** In him you also are being built together into a dwelling place for God by the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

### Chapter 3

**1** For this reason I, Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus on behalf of you nations,

**2** assuming that you have heard of the stewardship of God's grace \[G-CHARIS\] that was given to me for you,

**3** how the mystery was made known to me by revelation, as I have written briefly.

**4** When you read this you can perceive my understanding of the mystery of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\],

**5** which was not made known to human beings in other generations as it has now been revealed to his holy apostles and prophets by the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

**6** This mystery is that the nations are fellow heirs, members of the same body, and partners in the promise in Christ Jesus through the good news \[G-EUANGELION\].

**7** Of this I was made a minister according to the gift of God's grace \[G-CHARIS\], which was given me by the working of his power.

**8** To me, though I am the very least of all the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\], this grace was given, to proclaim to the nations the unsearchable riches of Christ,

**9** and to bring to light for everyone what is the plan of the mystery hidden for ages in God who created all things,

**10** so that through the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] the manifold wisdom of God might now be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places.

**11** This was according to the eternal purpose that he has realised in Christ Jesus our Lord,

**12** in whom we have boldness and access with confidence through our faith \[G-PISTIS\] in him.

**13** So I ask that you not lose heart over what I am suffering for you, which is your glory \[G-DOXA\].

**14** For this reason I bow my knees before the Father,

**15** from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named,

**16** that according to the riches of his glory \[G-DOXA\] he may grant you to be strengthened with power through his Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] in your inner being,

**17** so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith \[G-PISTIS\], that you, being rooted and grounded in love \[G-AGAPE\],

**18** may have strength to comprehend with all the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\] what is the breadth and length and height and depth,

**19** and to know the love \[G-AGAPE\] of Christ that surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.

**20** Now to him who is able to do far more abundantly than all that we ask or think, according to the power at work within us,

**21** to him be glory \[G-DOXA\] in the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] and in Christ Jesus throughout all generations, for ever and ever. Amen.

*Review note: Ephesians 1 to 3 were drafted in WABV wording, from the Greek, using the fixed choices set out in the introduction to this book, and with the WABV voice standard (Part 46 of the review package) applied. They are the greeting (1:1-2), the blessing of God for the choice, the adoption, the redemption and the sealing with the Spirit (1:3-14), the thanksgiving and the prayer for wisdom and revelation (1:15-23), alive with Christ and saved by grace through faith (2:1-10), one new humanity, the peace made by the cross (2:11-22), the mystery made known to Paul (3:1-13), the prayer for strength and love (3:14-19) and the doxology (3:20-21). The numbering is the usual numbering. No new G- marker is used in this part, and the markers are G-CHRISTOS (1:1-3:21), G-HAGIOS (1:1-3:18), G-CHARIS (1:2-3:8), G-EIRENE (1:2, 2:14-17), G-AGAPE (1:4, 1:15, 2:4, 3:17-19), G-DOXA (1:6-14, 3:13-21), G-LYTRON (1:7), G-ALETHEIA (1:13), G-EUANGELION (1:13, 2:17, 3:6), G-SOZO (1:13, 2:5-8), G-PISTIS (1:13-19, 2:8, 3:12-17), G-PNEUMA (1:13-3:16), G-EKKLESIA (1:22, 3:10-21), G-ELEOS (2:4), G-DIATHEKE (2:12) and G-NOMOS (2:15). In 1:1 "in Ephesus" is placed in square brackets, because the words are omitted by the oldest manuscripts (Papyrus 46 and the first hands of Sinaiticus and Vaticanus) and present in most others, and the footnote records the matter, and the Greek without the words reads "to the holy ones who are also faithful in Christ Jesus", and the WABV text does not decide whether the letter was a circular letter. In 1:3-14 the sentence is one sentence in the Greek, and the WABV text breaks it into verses at the joints of its clauses, and keeps the build of it. In 1:4 "in love" may be read with "holy and without blemish" or with "he marked us out beforehand" in verse 5, and the footnote records the matter. In 1:5 "marked out beforehand for adoption as sons" translates *proorisas eis huiothesian*, and it is as in Romans 8:29-30. In 1:6 "the Beloved" translates *ho egapemenos*, and the footnote records that it means Christ. In 1:7 "redemption" translates *apolutrosin*, and it is tagged G-LYTRON. In 1:9-10 "the mystery of his will" and "a plan for the fullness of time" are rendered as written, and "plan" translates *oikonomian*, and "to bring everything together" translates *anakephalaiosasthai*, which may also be read "to sum up" or "to head up", and the footnote records the matter. In 1:11 "we have also received an inheritance" translates *eklerothemen*, which may also be read "we were chosen as an inheritance" or "we were made a people of his own", and the footnote records the matter. In 1:13 "were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit" is rendered as written. In 1:14 "the guarantee" translates *arrabon*, as in 2 Corinthians 1:22. In 1:15 "and your love toward all the holy ones" is the reading of some manuscripts, and Papyrus 46 and Vaticanus omit "your love", and the footnote records the matter. In 1:17 "a spirit of wisdom and revelation" may be read of the Spirit of God or of the human spirit, and the footnote records the matter. In 1:18 "the eyes of your hearts enlightened" is the reading of the critical text (the accusative), and the footnote records the matter. In 1:20-23 "he worked in Christ when he raised him" is rendered as written. In 1:22 "gave him as head over all things to the assembly" is rendered as written. In 1:23 "the fullness of him who fills all in all" is rendered as written, and the footnote records that the Greek may be read in several ways, and the WABV text does not decide. In 2:2 "the ruler of the power of the air" is rendered as written, and the footnote records that it means Satan. In 2:3 "the desires of the body and the mind" translates *ton thelematon tes sarkos kai ton dianoion*, literally "of the flesh and of the thoughts", and "children of wrath" is rendered as written. In 2:5 "made us alive together with Christ (by grace you have been saved)" is rendered as written, and the footnote records that the parenthesis is repeated in verse 8. In 2:8 "and this is not your own doing" translates *kai touto*, and the footnote records that "this" may mean the salvation, or the faith, or the whole of it, and it is the Greek of the text. In 2:10 "his workmanship" translates *poiema*. In 2:11 "you, the nations in the flesh" is rendered as written. In 2:12 "the covenants of promise" translates *ton diathekon tes epangelias*, and it is tagged G-DIATHEKE. In 2:14 "the dividing wall of hostility" translates *to mesotoichon tou phragmou tes echthras*, and the footnote records the allusion to the wall in the temple that divided the court of the nations. In 2:15 "the law of commandments expressed in ordinances" is rendered as written, with no comment on how it is read. In 2:15 "one new humanity" translates *hena kainon anthropon*. In 2:16 "reconcile" translates *apokatallaxe*. In 2:20 "the cornerstone" translates *akrogoniaiou*, which may also be read "the capstone", and the footnote records the matter. In 2:21 "the whole structure" is the reading of the critical text (*pasa oikodome*), and some manuscripts read "every building" (*pasa he oikodome*), and the footnote records the matter. In 3:1 "for this reason I, Paul" begins a sentence that is not finished until verse 14, and the verses 2 to 13 are a long parenthesis, and the footnote records the matter. In 3:2 "the stewardship of God's grace" translates *ten oikonomian tes charitos tou theou*, and it is tagged G-CHARIS. In 3:3 "as I have written briefly" is rendered as written. In 3:5 "human beings" translates *huiois ton anthropon*, literally "sons of men". In 3:6 "the nations are fellow heirs, members of the same body, and partners in the promise" translates *sunkleronoma kai sussoma kai summetocha*, three words with the prefix *sun-*. In 3:8 "the very least of all the holy ones" translates *elachistoteroi panton hagion*, a comparative formed from a superlative. In 3:9 "the plan of the mystery" is the reading of the critical text (*he oikonomia tou musteriou*), and some manuscripts read *he koinonia*, "the fellowship", and the footnote records the matter. In 3:10 "the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places" is rendered as written. In 3:12 "through our faith in him" translates *dia tes pisteos autou*, which may also be read "through his faithfulness", and the team should decide, and the footnote records the matter. In 3:14 "I bow my knees before the Father" is the reading of the critical text, and the Byzantine tradition adds "of our Lord Jesus Christ", and the footnote records the matter. In 3:15 "from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named" translates *patria*, which may also be read "fatherhood", and the footnote records the matter. In 3:17 "that Christ may dwell in your hearts" is rendered as written. In 3:18-19 "the breadth and length and height and depth" is rendered as written, and the footnote records that the dimensions are not named as of anything. In 3:19 "the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge" is rendered as written. In 3:20-21 the doxology is rendered as written. In 3:21 "in the assembly and in Christ Jesus" is rendered as written, and some manuscripts omit "and", and the footnote records the matter. In 3:13 and elsewhere "brothers" does not occur in this part. There are no departures from the critical Greek text in chapters 1 to 3. The Greek is hard in some places, including 1:3-14, 1:15-23, 2:1-10, 2:14-18, 3:1-13 and 3:14-19. The footnotes record the main differences and the uncertain terms. The words on the blessing of God and the choice before the foundation of the world (1:3-14), the power that raised Christ (1:19-23), "by grace you have been saved through faith" (2:8-9), the dividing wall (2:14-16), the mystery of the nations (3:5-6), and the prayer for strength and love (3:14-19) are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. The section needs a check against the Greek by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked.*

## Part Two: Chapters 4 to 6, unity, the new self, walking in love, the household and the armour of God

### Chapter 4

**1** I, then, the prisoner in the Lord, urge you to walk in a way worthy of the calling to which you have been called,

**2** with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love \[G-AGAPE\],

**3** eager to keep the unity of the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] in the bond of peace \[G-EIRENE\].

**4** There is one body and one Spirit, just as you were called to the one hope that belongs to your calling,

**5** one Lord, one faith \[G-PISTIS\], one baptism,

**6** one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all.

**7** But grace \[G-CHARIS\] was given to each one of us according to the measure of Christ's \[G-CHRISTOS\] gift.

**8** Therefore it says, "When he ascended on high he led a host of captives, and he gave gifts to people."

**9** (In saying, "He ascended," what does it mean but that he had also descended into the lower parts of the earth?

**10** He who descended is the same one who ascended far above all the heavens, so that he might fill all things.)

**11** And he himself gave the apostles, the prophets, the evangelists, the shepherds and teachers,

**12** to equip the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\] for the work of ministry, for building up the body of Christ,

**13** until we all attain to the unity of the faith \[G-PISTIS\] and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to mature adulthood, to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ,

**14** so that we may no longer be children, tossed to and fro by the waves and carried about by every wind of teaching, by human cunning, by craftiness in deceitful scheming.

**15** Instead, speaking the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] in love \[G-AGAPE\], we are to grow up in every way into him who is the head, into Christ,

**16** from whom the whole body, joined and held together by every supporting ligament, when each part is working properly, makes the body grow so that it builds itself up in love \[G-AGAPE\].

**17** Now this I say and testify in the Lord, that you must no longer walk as the nations walk, in the futility of their minds.

**18** They are darkened in their understanding, alienated from the life \[G-ZOE\] of God because of the ignorance that is in them, due to their hardness of heart.

**19** They have become callous and have given themselves up to sensuality, greedy to practise every kind of impurity.

**20** But that is not the way you learned Christ,

**21** assuming that you have heard about him and were taught in him, as the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] is in Jesus,

**22** to put off your old self, which belongs to your former manner of life and is corrupt through deceitful desires,

**23** and to be renewed in the spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of your minds,

**24** and to put on the new self, created after the likeness of God in true righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] and holiness.

**25** Therefore, having put away falsehood, let each of you speak the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] with his neighbour, for we are members one of another.

**26** Be angry and do not sin. Do not let the sun go down on your anger,

**27** and give no opportunity to the devil.

**28** Let the thief no longer steal, but rather let him labour, doing honest work with his own hands, so that he may have something to share with anyone in need.

**29** Let no corrupting talk come out of your mouths, but only what is good for building up, as fits the occasion, that it may give grace \[G-CHARIS\] to those who hear.

**30** And do not grieve the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of God, with whom you were sealed for the day of redemption \[G-LYTRON\].

**31** Let all bitterness and wrath and anger and clamour and slander be put away from you, along with all malice.

**32** Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, as God in Christ forgave you.

### Chapter 5

**1** Therefore be imitators of God, as beloved \[G-AGAPE\] children.

**2** And walk in love \[G-AGAPE\], as Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] loved us and gave himself up for us, a fragrant offering and sacrifice to God.

**3** But sexual immorality and all impurity or greed must not even be named among you, as is proper among holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\].

**4** Let there be no filthiness nor foolish talk nor crude joking, which are out of place, but instead let there be thanksgiving.

**5** For you may be sure of this, that everyone who is sexually immoral or impure, or who is greedy (that is, an idolater), has no inheritance in the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of Christ and God.

**6** Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience.

**7** Therefore do not become partners with them,

**8** for at one time you were darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light

**9** (for the fruit of light is found in all that is good and right and true \[G-ALETHEIA\]),

**10** and try to discern what is pleasing to the Lord.

**11** Take no part in the unfruitful works of darkness, but instead expose them.

**12** For it is shameful even to speak of the things that they do in secret.

**13** But when anything is exposed by the light, it becomes visible, for anything that becomes visible is light.

**14** Therefore it says, "Awake, O sleeper, and arise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you."

**15** Look carefully then how you walk, not as unwise but as wise,

**16** making the best use of the time, because the days are evil.

**17** Therefore do not be foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is.

**18** And do not get drunk with wine, for that is debauchery, but be filled with the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\],

**19** addressing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody to the Lord with your heart,

**20** giving thanks always and for everything to God the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,

**21** submitting to one another out of reverence for Christ.

**22** Wives, submit to your own husbands, as to the Lord.

**23** For the husband is the head of the wife even as Christ is the head of the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\], his body, and is himself its Saviour.

**24** Now as the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] submits to Christ, so also wives should submit in everything to their husbands.

**25** Husbands, love \[G-AGAPE\] your wives, as Christ loved the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] and gave himself up for her,

**26** that he might make her holy \[G-HAGIOS\], having cleansed her by the washing of water with the word,

**27** so that he might present the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] to himself in splendour, without spot or wrinkle or any such thing, that she might be holy \[G-HAGIOS\] and without blemish.

**28** In the same way husbands should love \[G-AGAPE\] their wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself.

**29** For no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, just as Christ does the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\],

**30** because we are members of his body.

**31** "Therefore a man shall leave his father and mother and hold fast to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh."

**32** This mystery is profound, and I am saying that it refers to Christ and the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\].

**33** However, let each one of you love \[G-AGAPE\] his wife as himself, and let the wife see that she respects her husband.

### Chapter 6

**1** Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.

**2** "Honour your father and mother" (this is the first commandment with a promise),

**3** "that it may go well with you and that you may live long in the land."

**4** Fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.

**5** Slaves, obey your earthly masters with fear and trembling, with a sincere heart, as you would Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\],

**6** not by way of eye-service, as people-pleasers, but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart,

**7** rendering service with a good will as to the Lord and not to people,

**8** knowing that whatever good anyone does, this he will receive back from the Lord, whether he is a slave or free.

**9** Masters, do the same to them, and stop your threatening, knowing that he who is both their Master and yours is in heaven, and that there is no partiality with him.

**10** Finally, be strengthened in the Lord and in the strength of his might.

**11** Put on the whole armour of God, that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.

**12** For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places.

**13** Therefore take up the whole armour of God, that you may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand firm.

**14** Stand therefore, having fastened on the belt of truth \[G-ALETHEIA\], and having put on the breastplate of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\],
