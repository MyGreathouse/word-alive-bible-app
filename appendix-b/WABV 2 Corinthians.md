# WABV: 2 Corinthians

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-DIATHEKE\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Second Corinthians is the eighth book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the third of the letters. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It is a letter from Paul and Timothy to the assembly of God in Corinth and the holy ones in the whole of Achaia, written from Macedonia (2:13, 7:5, 9:2) in about AD 55 to 56, after the painful visit and the severe letter that it mentions (2:1-4, 7:8-12). It is the most personal of the letters of Paul, and its Greek is some of the hardest in the New Testament, with sentences that turn back on themselves and arguments that break off and begin again. Many scholars hold that it is made of more than one letter, and chapters 8 and 9 and chapters 10 to 13 in particular are often taken as separate pieces, and the WABV text keeps the letter in the order of the manuscripts and records the matter in the footnotes. The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text of 2 Corinthians is drafted with the voice standard (Part 46 of the review package), and makes a number of fixed choices that follow from its Greek, and they are written down here so that they are used throughout. *Ekklesia* is rendered "assembly" and is tagged G-EKKLESIA, and the team should decide between "assembly" and "church". *Hagioi* is rendered "holy ones" and is tagged G-HAGIOS. *Paraklesis* and *parakaleo* are rendered "comfort" and "comfort" (as a verb), and "urge" where the sense is to appeal, and the footnote records the link with *parakletos* in John. *Thlipsis* is rendered "affliction". *Kauchaomai* and *kauchesis* are rendered "boast". *Diatheke* is rendered "covenant" and is tagged G-DIATHEKE. *Doxa* is rendered "glory" and is tagged G-DOXA. *Katallage* and *katallasso* are rendered "reconciliation" and "reconcile". *Dikaiosune* is rendered "righteousness" and is tagged G-DIKAIOSUNE. *Charis* is rendered "grace" and is tagged G-CHARIS, and in chapters 8 and 9 it is also rendered "gift" and "generosity" where the sense of the collection requires it, and the footnote records the matter. *Diakonia* is rendered "ministry" or "service", and *diakonos* "minister" or "servant". *Doulos* is rendered "slave". *Pistis* is rendered "faith" and *pisteuo* "believe", and both are tagged G-PISTIS. *Pneuma* is rendered "Spirit" or "spirit" and is tagged G-PNEUMA. *Sarx* is rendered "flesh". *Adelphoi* is rendered "brothers", and the footnote records that in many places it may include the sisters, and the team should decide. *Ioudaios* is rendered "Jew", and *ethne* "the nations". *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. The book falls into four parts in the WABV text: the comfort in affliction, the change of plans, the new covenant and the treasure in jars of clay (chapters 1 to 4), the tent, the ministry of reconciliation and the open heart (5 to 7), the collection for the holy ones and the weapons of the apostle (8 to 10), and the fool's speech, the thorn and the final warnings (11 to 13). The headings of the parts are not in the Greek. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System, and the new ones are listed in the review note of the part where each first appears. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Part One: Chapters 1 to 4, comfort in affliction, the change of plans, the new covenant and the treasure in jars of clay

### Chapter 1

**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] Jesus by the will of God, and Timothy our brother, to the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] of God that is in Corinth, with all the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\] who are in the whole of Achaia:

**2** grace \[G-CHARIS\] to you and peace \[G-EIRENE\] from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**3** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassions and God of all comfort,

**4** who comforts us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to comfort those who are in any affliction, with the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God.

**5** For as the sufferings of Christ overflow into us, so through Christ our comfort overflows also.

**6** If we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation \[G-SOZO\]. If we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which you experience when you patiently endure the same sufferings that we suffer.

**7** Our hope for you is firm, since we know that as you share in our sufferings, you will also share in our comfort.

**8** For we do not want you to be unaware, brothers, of the affliction that came to us in Asia. We were burdened beyond our strength, so that we despaired of life \[G-ZOE\] itself.

**9** Indeed, we felt that we had received the sentence of death. But that was to make us rely not on ourselves but on God who raises the dead.

**10** He delivered us from so deadly a peril, and he will deliver us. On him we have set our hope that he will deliver us again,

**11** as you help us by your prayers, so that many will give thanks on our behalf for the gift \[G-CHARIS\] granted us through the prayers of many.

**12** For our boast is this, the testimony of our conscience, that we behaved in the world, and still more toward you, with the simplicity and sincerity that come from God, not by earthly wisdom but by the grace \[G-CHARIS\] of God.

**13** For we are writing to you nothing other than what you read and understand, and I hope you will understand fully,

**14** just as you did partly understand us, that on the day of our Lord Jesus you will boast of us just as we will boast of you.

**15** Because I was sure of this, I wanted to come to you first, so that you might have a second grace \[G-CHARIS\],

**16** to visit you on my way to Macedonia, and to come back to you from Macedonia, and to be sent on my way to Judea by you.

**17** Was I vacillating, then, when I wanted to do this? Do I make my plans in a worldly way, so that with me it is "Yes, yes" and "No, no" at the same time?

**18** As God is faithful, our word to you has not been "Yes and No."

**19** For the Son of God, Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], whom we proclaimed among you, Silvanus and Timothy and I, was not "Yes and No," but in him it is always "Yes."

**20** For all the promises of God find their "Yes" in him. That is why it is through him that we say our "Amen" to God for his glory \[G-DOXA\].

**21** And it is God who establishes us with you in Christ, and has anointed us,

**22** and who has also put his seal on us and given us his Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] in our hearts as a guarantee.

**23** But I call God to witness against me: it was to spare you that I did not come again to Corinth.

**24** Not that we rule over your faith \[G-PISTIS\], but we work with you for your joy, for you stand firm in faith.

### Chapter 2

**1** For I made up my mind not to make another painful visit to you.

**2** For if I cause you pain, who is there to make me glad but the one whom I have pained?

**3** And I wrote as I did so that when I came I would not suffer pain from those who should have made me rejoice, for I am confident in all of you that my joy is the joy of you all.

**4** For I wrote to you out of much affliction and anguish of heart and with many tears, not to cause you pain but to let you know the abundant love \[G-AGAPE\] that I have for you.

**5** But if anyone has caused pain, he has caused it not to me, but in some measure (not to put it too severely) to all of you.

**6** For such a person this punishment by the majority is enough,

**7** so that you should rather turn to forgive and comfort him, or he may be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow.

**8** So I beg you to reaffirm your love \[G-AGAPE\] for him.

**9** For this is why I wrote, that I might test you and know whether you are obedient in everything.

**10** Anyone whom you forgive, I also forgive. For what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, has been for your sake in the presence of Christ,

**11** so that we would not be outwitted by Satan, for we are not ignorant of his designs.

**12** When I came to Troas to announce the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of Christ, even though a door was opened for me in the Lord,

**13** my spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] was not at rest because I did not find my brother Titus there. So I said goodbye to them and went on to Macedonia.

**14** But thanks be to God, who in Christ always leads us in triumph, and through us spreads the fragrance of the knowledge of him everywhere.

**15** For we are the aroma of Christ to God among those who are being saved \[G-SOZO\] and among those who are perishing,

**16** to one a fragrance from death to death, to the other a fragrance from life \[G-ZOE\] to life. Who is sufficient for these things?

**17** For we are not, like so many, peddlers of God's word, but as people of sincerity, as sent from God and in the sight of God we speak in Christ.

### Chapter 3

**1** Are we beginning to commend ourselves again? Or do we need, like some people, letters of recommendation to you or from you?

**2** You yourselves are our letter, written on our hearts, known and read by all.

**3** And you show that you are a letter from Christ delivered by us, written not with ink but with the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets of human hearts.

**4** Such is the confidence that we have through Christ toward God.

**5** Not that we are sufficient in ourselves to claim anything as coming from us, but our sufficiency is from God,

**6** who has made us sufficient to be ministers of a new covenant \[G-DIATHEKE\], not of letter but of Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\]. For the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life \[G-ZOE\].

**7** Now if the ministry of death, carved in letters on stone, came with such glory \[G-DOXA\] that the people of Israel could not gaze at Moses' face because of its glory, which was being brought to an end,

**8** will not the ministry of the Spirit have even more glory?

**9** For if there was glory in the ministry of condemnation, the ministry of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] must far exceed it in glory.

**10** Indeed, in this case, what once had glory has come to have no glory at all, because of the glory that surpasses it.

**11** For if what was brought to an end came with glory, much more will what is permanent have glory.

**12** Since we have such a hope, we are very bold,

**13** not like Moses, who would put a veil over his face so that the people of Israel might not gaze at the end of what was being brought to an end.

**14** But their minds were hardened. For to this day, when they read the old covenant \[G-DIATHEKE\], that same veil remains unlifted, because only in Christ is it taken away.

**15** Yes, to this day whenever Moses is read a veil lies over their hearts.

**16** But when one turns to the Lord, the veil is removed.

**17** Now the Lord is the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.

**18** And we all, with unveiled face, gazing at the glory \[G-DOXA\] of the Lord as in a mirror, are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another. For this comes from the Lord who is the Spirit.

### Chapter 4

**1** Therefore, since we have this ministry by the mercy \[G-ELEOS\] shown us, we do not lose heart.

**2** We have renounced disgraceful, underhanded ways. We refuse to practise cunning or to tamper with God's word, but by the open statement of the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] we commend ourselves to everyone's conscience in the sight of God.

**3** And even if our good news \[G-EUANGELION\] is veiled, it is veiled to those who are perishing.

**4** In their case the god of this age has blinded the minds of the unbelievers, to keep them from seeing the light of the good news of the glory \[G-DOXA\] of Christ, who is the image of God.

**5** For what we proclaim is not ourselves, but Jesus Christ as Lord, with ourselves as your slaves for Jesus' sake.

**6** For God, who said, "Let light shine out of darkness," has shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.

**7** But we have this treasure in jars of clay, to show that the surpassing power belongs to God and not to us.

**8** We are afflicted in every way, but not crushed; perplexed, but not driven to despair;

**9** persecuted, but not forsaken; struck down, but not destroyed;

**10** always carrying in the body the death of Jesus, so that the life \[G-ZOE\] of Jesus may also be manifested in our bodies.

**11** For we who live are always being given over to death for Jesus' sake, so that the life of Jesus also may be manifested in our mortal flesh.

**12** So death is at work in us, but life in you.

**13** Since we have the same spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of faith \[G-PISTIS\] according to what has been written, "I believed, and so I spoke," we also believe, and so we also speak,

**14** knowing that he who raised the Lord Jesus will raise us also with Jesus and bring us with you into his presence.

**15** For it is all for your sake, so that as grace \[G-CHARIS\] extends to more and more people it may increase thanksgiving, to the glory \[G-DOXA\] of God.

**16** So we do not lose heart. Though our outer self is wasting away, our inner self is being renewed day by day.

**17** For this light momentary affliction is preparing for us an eternal weight of glory beyond all comparison,

**18** as we look not to the things that are seen but to the things that are unseen. For the things that are seen are transient, but the things that are unseen are eternal.

*Review note: 2 Corinthians 1 to 4 were drafted in WABV wording, from the Greek, using the fixed choices set out in the introduction to this book, and with the WABV voice standard (Part 46 of the review package) applied. They are the greeting (1:1-2), the God of all comfort (1:3-7), the affliction in Asia (1:8-11), the testimony of conscience and the change of plans (1:12-2:4), the one who caused pain and the call to forgive (2:5-11), Troas and Macedonia (2:12-13), the aroma of Christ (2:14-17), the letter written on hearts (3:1-3), the ministers of a new covenant (3:4-6), the glory of the two ministries and the veil of Moses (3:7-18), the ministry without cunning (4:1-6), the treasure in jars of clay (4:7-12), faith and speaking (4:13-15) and the inner self renewed (4:16-18). The numbering is the usual numbering. ## Part Two: Chapters 5 to 7, the tent, reconciliation, the open heart and godly grief

### Chapter 5

**1** For we know that if the earthly tent that is our house is torn down, we have a building from God, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.

**2** For in this tent we groan, longing to put on our heavenly dwelling,

**3** since, when we have put it on, we will not be found naked.

**4** For while we are in this tent, we groan under our burden, not that we would be unclothed but that we would be clothed, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life \[G-ZOE\].

**5** He who has prepared us for this very thing is God, who has given us the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] as a guarantee.

**6** So we are always confident. We know that while we are at home in the body we are away from the Lord,

**7** for we walk by faith \[G-PISTIS\], not by sight.

**8** Yes, we are confident, and we would rather be away from the body and at home with the Lord.

**9** So whether we are at home or away, we make it our aim to please him.

**10** For we must all appear before the judgement seat of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], so that each one may receive what is due for what he has done in the body, whether good or evil.

**11** Knowing, then, the fear of the Lord, we persuade others. But what we are is known to God, and I hope it is known also to your consciences.

**12** We are not commending ourselves to you again but giving you an occasion to boast about us, so that you may have an answer for those who boast about outward appearance and not about the heart.

**13** For if we are out of our minds, it is for God. If we are in our right mind, it is for you.

**14** For the love \[G-AGAPE\] of Christ controls us, because we have concluded this: that one has died for all, therefore all have died.

**15** And he died for all, so that those who live might no longer live for themselves but for him who for their sake died and was raised.

**16** From now on, therefore, we regard no one according to the flesh. Even though we once regarded Christ according to the flesh, we regard him thus no longer.

**17** Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, there is a new creation. The old has passed away. See, the new has come.

**18** All this is from God, who through Christ reconciled us to himself and gave us the ministry of reconciliation,

**19** that is, in Christ God was reconciling the world to himself, not counting their trespasses against them, and entrusting to us the message of reconciliation.

**20** So we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were making his appeal through us. We beg you on behalf of Christ: be reconciled to God.

**21** For our sake he made him to be sin who knew no sin, so that in him we might become the righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] of God.

### Chapter 6

**1** As God's fellow workers, then, we urge you also not to receive the grace \[G-CHARIS\] of God in vain.

**2** For he says, "In a favourable time I listened to you, and on a day of salvation \[G-SOZO\] I helped you." Look, now is the favourable time. Look, now is the day of salvation.

**3** We put no obstacle in anyone's way, so that no fault may be found with our ministry,

**4** but as servants of God we commend ourselves in every way: by great endurance, in afflictions, hardships, calamities,

**5** beatings, imprisonments, riots, labours, sleepless nights, hunger,

**6** by purity, knowledge, patience, kindness, the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], genuine love \[G-AGAPE\],

**7** by truthful speech \[G-ALETHEIA\], and the power of God, with the weapons of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] for the right hand and for the left,

**8** through glory \[G-DOXA\] and dishonour, through slander and praise. We are treated as impostors, and yet are true,

**9** as unknown, and yet well known, as dying, and see, we live, as punished, and yet not put to death,

**10** as sorrowful, yet always rejoicing, as poor, yet making many rich, as having nothing, yet possessing everything.

**11** We have spoken freely to you, Corinthians. Our heart is wide open.

**12** You are not restricted by us, but you are restricted in your own affections.

**13** In return (I speak as to children) open wide your hearts also.

**14** Do not be yoked together with unbelievers. For what partnership has righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] with lawlessness? Or what fellowship has light with darkness?

**15** What agreement has Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] with Beliar? Or what share does a believer \[G-PISTIS\] have with an unbeliever?

**16** What agreement has the temple of God with idols? For we are the temple of the living God, as God said: "I will live in them and walk among them, and I will be their God, and they will be my people."

**17** Therefore, "Come out from them and be separate from them, says the Lord, and touch nothing unclean, and I will welcome you,

**18** and I will be a father to you, and you will be sons and daughters to me, says the Lord Almighty."

### Chapter 7

**1** Since we have these promises, loved ones \[G-AGAPE\], let us cleanse ourselves from every defilement of body and spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], bringing holiness \[G-HAGIOS\] to completion in the fear of God.

**2** Make room for us in your hearts. We have wronged no one, we have corrupted no one, we have taken advantage of no one.

**3** I do not say this to condemn you, for I have said before that you are in our hearts, to die together and to live together.

**4** I have great confidence in you. I have great pride in you. I am filled with comfort. In all our affliction I am overflowing with joy.

**5** For even when we came into Macedonia, our flesh had no rest, but we were afflicted at every turn, with fighting without and fear within.

**6** But God, who comforts the downcast, comforted us by the coming of Titus,

**7** and not only by his coming but also by the comfort with which he was comforted by you, as he told us of your longing, your mourning, your zeal for me, so that I rejoiced still more.

**8** For even if I made you sorry with my letter, I do not regret it (though I did regret it), for I see that that letter made you sorry, though only for a while.

**9** Now I rejoice, not because you were made sorry, but because you were made sorry into repentance \[G-METANOIA\]. For you felt godly grief, so that you were not harmed by us in anything.

**10** For godly grief produces a repentance \[G-METANOIA\] that leads to salvation \[G-SOZO\] without regret, whereas worldly grief produces death.

**11** For see what earnestness this godly grief has produced in you, what eagerness to clear yourselves, what indignation, what alarm, what longing, what zeal, what punishment. At every point you have proved yourselves innocent in the matter.

**12** So although I wrote to you, it was not on account of the one who did the wrong, nor on account of the one who suffered the wrong, but in order that your earnestness for us might be revealed to you in the sight of God.

**13** Therefore we are comforted. And besides our own comfort, we rejoiced still more at the joy of Titus, because his spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] has been refreshed by all of you.

**14** For if I have boasted to him about you, I was not put to shame. But just as everything we said to you was true \[G-ALETHEIA\], so our boasting before Titus has proved true also.

**15** And his affection for you is even greater, as he remembers the obedience of all of you, how you received him with fear and trembling.

**16** I rejoice, because I have complete confidence in you.

## Part Four: Chapters 11 to 13, the fool's speech, the thorn and the final warnings

### Chapter 11

**2** For I am jealous for you with the jealousy of God, since I betrothed you to one husband, to present you as a pure virgin to Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\].

**5** Indeed, I consider that I am not in the least inferior to these super-apostles.

**6** Even if I am unskilled in speaking, I am not so in knowledge. In every way we have made this plain to you in all things.

**7** Or did I commit a sin in humbling myself so that you might be exalted, because I preached God's good news \[G-EUANGELION\] to you free of charge?

**8** I robbed other assemblies \[G-EKKLESIA\] by accepting support from them in order to serve you.

**9** And when I was with you and was in need, I did not burden anyone, for the brothers who came from Macedonia supplied my need. So I refrained and will refrain from burdening you in any way.

**10** As the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] of Christ is in me, this boast of mine will not be silenced in the regions of Achaia.

**11** And why? Because I do not love \[G-AGAPE\] you? God knows I do.

**12** And what I am doing I will continue to do, in order to undermine the claim of those who would like to claim that in their boasted mission they work on the same terms as we do.

**13** For such people are false apostles, deceitful workers, disguising themselves as apostles of Christ.

**14** And no wonder, for even Satan disguises himself as an angel of light.

**15** So it is no surprise if his servants also disguise themselves as servants of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\]. Their end will correspond to their deeds.

**16** I repeat, let no one think me foolish. But even if you do, accept me as a fool, so that I too may boast a little.

**18** Since many boast according to the flesh, I too will boast.

**19** For you gladly put up with fools, being wise yourselves.

**20** For you put up with it if someone makes slaves of you, or devours you, or takes advantage of you, or puts on airs, or strikes you in the face.

**21** To my shame, I must say that we were too weak for that. But whatever anyone dares to boast of (I am speaking as a fool), I also dare to boast of that.

**22** Are they Hebrews? So am I. Are they Israelites? So am I. Are they offspring of Abraham? So am I.

**23** Are they servants of Christ? (I am speaking as a madman.) I am a better one: with far greater labours, far more imprisonments, with countless beatings, and often near death.

**24** Five times I received from the Jews the forty lashes less one.

**25** Three times I was beaten with rods. Once I was stoned. Three times I was shipwrecked. A night and a day I was adrift at sea.

**26** On frequent journeys, in danger from rivers, danger from robbers, danger from my own people, danger from the nations, danger in the city, danger in the wilderness, danger at sea, danger from false brothers;

**27** in toil and hardship, through many a sleepless night, in hunger and thirst, often without food, in cold and exposure.

**29** Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is made to fall, and I am not indignant?

**30** If I must boast, I will boast of the things that show my weakness.

**31** The God and Father of the Lord Jesus, he who is blessed for ever, knows that I am not lying.

- **13:2** "My second visit" is rendered as written.
- **13:4** "Weak in him" is the reading of the critical text, and some manuscripts read "with him" (*sun auto*).
- **13:5** "In the faith" translates *en te pistei*, and "fail to meet the test" translates *adokimoi*. See entry G-PISTIS.
- **13:8** "The truth" translates *aletheias*. See entry G-ALETHEIA.
- **13:9** "Your restoration" translates *katartisin*.
- **13:11** "Aim for restoration" translates *katartizesthe*, which may also be read "be restored" or "mend your ways". See entries G-EIRENE and G-AGAPE.
- **13:12** "A holy kiss" is as in 1 Corinthians 16:20.
- **13:13** "The holy ones" translates *hagioi*. See entry G-HAGIOS. In the Greek versification this verse and verse 14 are numbered differently, and the benediction is 13:13 in the Greek.
- **13:14** "The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit" is the three-part benediction, and it is the fullest in the New Testament. "The fellowship of the Holy Spirit" may be read "the sharing in the Holy Spirit" or "the fellowship that the Holy Spirit gives". See entries G-CHARIS, G-AGAPE and G-PNEUMA.
