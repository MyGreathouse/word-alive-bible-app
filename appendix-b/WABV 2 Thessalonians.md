# WABV: 2 Thessalonians

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-EKKLESIA\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Second Thessalonians is the fourteenth book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the ninth of the letters. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It is a letter from Paul, Silvanus and Timothy to the assembly of the Thessalonians, and it follows the first letter closely in its wording and its setting: the believers are suffering (1:4-7), and some are saying that the day of the Lord has already come (2:2), and some are living in idleness (3:6-12). Its authorship is debated, since some scholars hold it to be Paul and some a close follower of Paul, and the order of the two letters has also been questioned, and the WABV text does not decide either. The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text of 2 Thessalonians is drafted with the voice standard (Part 46 of the review package). Where the text of a critical edition is not certain, the draft says so in the footnote and asks the team to confirm the reading. It makes a number of fixed choices that follow from its Greek, and they are written down here so that they are used throughout, and they are the same as the choices in 1 Thessalonians. *Ekklesia* is rendered "assembly" and is tagged G-EKKLESIA, and the team should decide between "assembly" and "church". *Hagios* and *hagiasmos* are rendered "holy" and "being made holy", and are tagged G-HAGIOS. *Parousia* (2:1, 2:8, 2:9) is rendered "coming". *Apokalupsis* (1:7) is rendered "revealing" or "be revealed". *Anomia* (2:3, 2:7) and *anomos* (2:8) are rendered "lawlessness" and "the lawless one". *Pistis* is rendered "faith" and *pisteuo* "believe", and both are tagged G-PISTIS. *Agape* is rendered "love" and is tagged G-AGAPE. *Euangelion* is rendered "good news" and is tagged G-EUANGELION. *Charis* is rendered "grace" and is tagged G-CHARIS. *Eirene* is rendered "peace" and is tagged G-EIRENE. *Doxa* is rendered "glory" and is tagged G-DOXA. *Basileia* is rendered "kingdom" and is tagged G-BASILEIA. *Sozo* is rendered "save" and is tagged G-SOZO. *Pneuma* is rendered "Spirit" or "spirit" and is tagged G-PNEUMA. *Adelphoi* is rendered "brothers", and the footnote records that in many places it may include the sisters, and the team should decide. *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. The headings of the parts are not in the Greek. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Chapters 1 to 3, the faith of the Thessalonians, the day of the Lord and the call to stand firm

### Chapter 1

**1** Paul, Silvanus and Timothy, to the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\]:

**2** grace \[G-CHARIS\] to you and peace \[G-EIRENE\] from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**3** We ought always to give thanks to God for you, brothers, as is right, because your faith \[G-PISTIS\] is growing abundantly, and the love \[G-AGAPE\] of every one of you for one another is increasing.

**4** Therefore we ourselves boast about you in the assemblies \[G-EKKLESIA\] of God for your steadfastness and faith \[G-PISTIS\] in all your persecutions and in the afflictions that you are enduring.

**5** This is evidence of the righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] judgement of God, that you may be considered worthy of the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of God, for which you are also suffering,

**6** since indeed God considers it just to repay with affliction those who afflict you,

**7** and to grant relief to you who are afflicted as well as to us, when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with his mighty angels

**8** in flaming fire, inflicting vengeance on those who do not know God and on those who do not obey the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of our Lord Jesus.

**9** They will suffer the punishment of eternal destruction, away from the presence of the Lord and from the glory \[G-DOXA\] of his might,

**10** when he comes on that day to be glorified in his holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\], and to be marvelled at among all who have believed \[G-PISTIS\], because our testimony to you was believed.

**11** To this end we always pray for you, that our God may make you worthy of his calling and fulfil every good resolve and work of faith \[G-PISTIS\] by his power,

**12** so that the name of our Lord Jesus may be glorified in you, and you in him, according to the grace \[G-CHARIS\] of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.

### Chapter 2

**1** Now concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our being gathered together to him, we ask you, brothers,

**2** not to be quickly shaken in mind or alarmed, either by a spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] or a spoken word, or a letter seeming to be from us, to the effect that the day of the Lord has come.

**3** Let no one deceive you in any way. For that day will not come unless the rebellion comes first, and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction,

**4** who opposes and exalts himself against every so-called god or object of worship, so that he takes his seat in the temple of God, proclaiming himself to be God.

**5** Do you not remember that when I was still with you I told you these things?

**6** And you know what is restraining him now so that he may be revealed in his time.

**7** For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work. Only he who now restrains it will do so until he is out of the way.

**8** And then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus will kill with the breath of his mouth and bring to nothing by the appearance of his coming.

**9** The coming of the lawless one is by the activity of Satan with all power and false signs and wonders,

**10** and with all wicked deception for those who are perishing, because they refused to love \[G-AGAPE\] the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] and so be saved \[G-SOZO\].

**11** Therefore God sends them a strong delusion, so that they may believe \[G-PISTIS\] what is false,

**12** so that all may be condemned who did not believe \[G-PISTIS\] the truth but had pleasure in unrighteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

**13** But we ought always to give thanks to God for you, brothers loved \[G-AGAPE\] by the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning to be saved \[G-SOZO\], through being made holy \[G-HAGIOS\] by the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] and faith \[G-PISTIS\] in the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\].

**14** To this he called you through our good news \[G-EUANGELION\], so that you may obtain the glory \[G-DOXA\] of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**15** So then, brothers, stand firm and hold to the traditions that you were taught by us, either by our spoken word or by our letter.

**16** Now may our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God our Father, who loved \[G-AGAPE\] us and gave us eternal comfort and good hope through grace \[G-CHARIS\],

**17** comfort your hearts and establish them in every good work and word.

### Chapter 3

**1** Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may speed ahead and be honoured, as happened among you,

**2** and that we may be delivered from wicked and evil people. For not all have faith \[G-PISTIS\].

**3** But the Lord is faithful. He will establish you and guard you against the evil one.

**4** And we have confidence in the Lord about you, that you are doing and will do the things that we command.

**5** May the Lord direct your hearts to the love \[G-AGAPE\] of God and to the steadfastness of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\].

**6** Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you keep away from any brother who is walking in idleness and not in accord with the tradition that you received from us.

**7** For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we were not idle when we were with you,

**8** nor did we eat anyone's bread without paying for it, but with toil and labour we worked night and day, that we might not be a burden to any of you.

**9** It was not because we do not have that right, but to give you in ourselves an example to imitate.

**10** For even when we were with you, we would give you this command: If anyone is not willing to work, let him not eat.

**11** For we hear that some among you walk in idleness, not busy at work, but busybodies.

**12** Now such persons we command and encourage in the Lord Jesus Christ to do their work quietly and to earn their own living.

**13** As for you, brothers, do not grow weary in doing good.

**14** If anyone does not obey what we say in this letter, take note of that person, and have nothing to do with him, that he may be ashamed.

**15** Do not regard him as an enemy, but warn him as a brother.

**16** Now may the Lord of peace \[G-EIRENE\] himself give you peace at all times in every way. The Lord be with you all.

**17** I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. This is the sign of genuineness in every letter of mine. It is how I write.

**18** The grace \[G-CHARIS\] of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all.

*Review note: 2 Thessalonians 1 to 3 were drafted in WABV wording, from the Greek, using the fixed choices set out in the introduction to this book, and with the WABV voice standard (Part 46 of the review package) applied. They are the greeting (1:1-2), the thanksgiving for the growing faith and love of the Thessalonians (1:3-4), the righteous judgement of God and the revealing of the Lord Jesus (1:5-10), the prayer (1:11-12), the day of the Lord and the warning against being shaken (2:1-2), the rebellion and the man of lawlessness (2:3-12), the thanksgiving for their choice and the call to stand firm (2:13-17), the request for prayer (3:1-5), the command to keep away from the idle (3:6-15) and the closing (3:16-18). The numbering is the usual numbering. No new G- marker is used, and the markers are G-EKKLESIA (1:1-4), G-CHRISTOS (1:1-3:5), G-CHARIS (1:2, 1:12, 2:16, 3:18), G-EIRENE (1:2, 3:16), G-PISTIS (1:3-3:2), G-AGAPE (1:3, 2:10-16, 3:5), G-DIKAIOSUNE (1:5, 2:12), G-BASILEIA (1:5), G-EUANGELION (1:8, 2:14), G-DOXA (1:9, 2:14), G-HAGIOS (1:10, 2:13), G-PNEUMA (2:2, 2:13), G-ALETHEIA (2:10-13) and G-SOZO (2:10, 2:13). Two readings are drafted with some uncertainty and the team should confirm them against the critical edition in use: at 2:3 the draft has "the man of lawlessness", and some manuscripts read "the man of sin", and at 2:13 the draft has "from the beginning", and some manuscripts read "as firstfruits". In 1:7 "revealed" translates *apokalupsei*, and "with his mighty angels in flaming fire" is rendered as written. In 1:8-9 "inflicting vengeance" and "eternal destruction, away from the presence of the Lord" are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. In 2:1-12 the passage on the day of the Lord, the rebellion, the man of lawlessness and the one who restrains is rendered as written, and the footnote records that it is one of the most debated passages in the New Testament, and that the WABV text does not decide who or what is meant by "the one who restrains" or "what is restraining him" (2:6-7), or by "the temple of God" (2:4). In 2:3 "the rebellion" translates *he apostasia*, which may also be read "the apostasy" or "the falling away". In 2:3 "the son of destruction" is rendered as written. In 2:6-7 "what is restraining him" and "he who now restrains it" translate *to katechon* and *ho katechon*, a neuter and a masculine form. In 2:7 "the mystery of lawlessness" is rendered as written. In 2:8 "the Lord Jesus will kill with the breath of his mouth" is the reading of the critical text (*anelei*), and some manuscripts read *analoseis*, "consume", and the allusion is to Isaiah 11:4, and the footnote records the matter. In 2:9 "false signs and wonders" is rendered as written. In 2:11 "God sends them a strong delusion" is rendered as written, with no comment on how it is read. In 2:13 "brothers loved by the Lord" is the reading of the critical text, and some manuscripts read "by God", and "through being made holy by the Spirit" translates *en hagiasmo pneumatos*. In 2:15 "the traditions that you were taught by us" is rendered as written. In 2:16 "who loved us and gave us eternal comfort" is rendered as written. In 3:1 "may speed ahead and be honoured" is rendered as written. In 3:2 "not all have faith" is rendered as written, and "faith" translates *pistis*. In 3:3 "the evil one" may be read "evil", and the footnote records the matter. In 3:5 "the steadfastness of Christ" may be read "the steadfastness that Christ gives" or "waiting for Christ", and the footnote records the matter. In 3:6 "the tradition that you received" is the reading of the critical text (*parelabosan*, the third person), and some manuscripts read the second person (*parelabete*), and the footnote records the matter. In 3:6-12 "walking in idleness" translates *ataktos peripatountos*, and may also be read "walking in disorder", and "busybodies" translates *periergazomenous*. In 3:10 "if anyone is not willing to work, let him not eat" is rendered as written. In 3:14 "take note of that person" is rendered as written. In 3:17 "with my own hand" is as in 1 Corinthians 16:21, Galatians 6:11 and Colossians 4:18, and "this is the sign of genuineness in every letter of mine" is rendered as written. In 3:18 "with you all" is the reading of the critical text, and some manuscripts add "Amen". In 1:3, 2:1, 2:13, 2:15, 3:1, 3:6, 3:13 "brothers" translates *adelphoi*, and the team should decide whether it is to include the sisters. There are no departures from the critical Greek text in chapters 1 to 3. The Greek is hard in some places, including 1:5-10, 2:1-12, 2:13 and 3:6-12. The footnotes record the main differences and the uncertain terms. The words on the revealing of the Lord Jesus from heaven (1:7-10), the day of the Lord and the rebellion (2:1-4), the one who restrains (2:6-7), the lawless one and the breath of his mouth (2:8), the strong delusion (2:11), the traditions (2:15) and "if anyone is not willing to work, let him not eat" (3:10) are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. The section needs a check against the Greek by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked. With this the book of 2 Thessalonians is complete in first draft.*
