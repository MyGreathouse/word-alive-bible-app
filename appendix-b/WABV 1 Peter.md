# WABV: 1 Peter

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-LYTRON\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. First Peter is the twenty-first book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the second of the seven general letters. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It is a letter from Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the elect exiles of the Dispersion in five provinces of Asia Minor (1:1), written "through Silvanus" (5:12) from "Babylon" (5:13), which most readers take to mean Rome. It is a letter to believers who are suffering, and it speaks of a living hope, of holiness, of Christ who suffered and is the example of those who suffer, and of the people of God as a holy priesthood and a chosen race, and its Greek is good and smooth and is full of the language of the Old Testament in the Septuagint. Who wrote it is debated: the letter names Peter, and some scholars hold that it is by Peter with the help of Silvanus and some that it was written in his name, and the WABV text does not decide. Some passages are among the hardest in the New Testament, and they are rendered as written, with the Greek and the main alternatives in the footnotes: the household code (2:18-3:7), the spirits in prison and baptism (3:18-22) and the gospel that was preached to the dead (4:6). The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text of 1 Peter is drafted with the voice standard (Part 46 of the review package). Where the text of a critical edition is not certain, the draft says so in the footnote and asks the team to confirm the reading. It makes a number of fixed choices that follow from its Greek, and they are written down here so that they are used throughout. *Parepidemos* and *paroikos* (1:1, 2:11) are rendered "exile" and "sojourner". *Hagios* and *hagiasmos* (1:2, 1:15-16, 2:5, 2:9, 3:5) are rendered "holy" and "making holy", and are tagged G-HAGIOS, and "sanctification" is not used. *Lutroo* (1:18) is rendered "ransom" and is tagged G-LYTRON. *Anagennao* (1:3, 1:23) is rendered "cause to be born again" and "be born again", and it has no marker. *Pistis* is rendered "faith" and *pisteuo* "believe", and both are tagged G-PISTIS. *Agape* is rendered "love" and is tagged G-AGAPE, and *philadelphia* (1:22, 3:8) "brotherly love" and is not tagged. *Charis* is rendered "grace" and is tagged G-CHARIS. *Eleos* is rendered "mercy" and is tagged G-ELEOS. *Eirene* is rendered "peace" and is tagged G-EIRENE. *Doxa* is rendered "glory" and is tagged G-DOXA. *Sozo* and *soteria* are rendered "save" and "salvation" and are tagged G-SOZO. *Zoe* is rendered "life" and is tagged G-ZOE. *Psyche* is rendered "soul" and is tagged G-PSYCHE. *Pneuma* is rendered "Spirit" or "spirit" and is tagged G-PNEUMA. *Euangelion* and *euangelizo* are rendered "good news" and "preach the good news" and are tagged G-EUANGELION. *Dikaiosune* is rendered "righteousness" and is tagged G-DIKAIOSUNE. *Aletheia* is rendered "truth" and is tagged G-ALETHEIA. *Makarios* is rendered "blessed" and is tagged G-MAKARIOS. *Oiketai* (2:18) is rendered "household servants". *Presbuteros* (5:1) is rendered "elder" and *episkopeo* (5:2) "exercise oversight". *Anthropos* is rendered "person" or "people". *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Chapters 1 to 5, the living hope, the holy people and suffering for doing good

### Chapter 1

**1** Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], to the elect exiles of the Dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithynia,

**2** chosen according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, in the making holy \[G-HAGIOS\] by the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], for obedience to Jesus Christ and for sprinkling with his blood: grace \[G-CHARIS\] and peace \[G-EIRENE\] be multiplied to you.

**3** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. According to his great mercy \[G-ELEOS\] he has caused us to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,

**4** to an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept in heaven for you,

**5** who by the power of God are being guarded through faith \[G-PISTIS\] for a salvation \[G-SOZO\] ready to be revealed in the last time.

**6** In this you rejoice, though now for a little while, if need be, you have been grieved by various trials,

**7** so that the tested genuineness of your faith \[G-PISTIS\], more precious than gold that perishes though it is tested by fire, may be found to result in praise and glory \[G-DOXA\] and honour at the revealing of Jesus Christ.

**8** Though you have not seen him, you love \[G-AGAPE\] him. Though you do not now see him, you believe \[G-PISTIS\] in him and rejoice with joy that is inexpressible and filled with glory \[G-DOXA\],

**9** obtaining the outcome of your faith \[G-PISTIS\], the salvation \[G-SOZO\] of your souls \[G-PSYCHE\].

**10** Concerning this salvation, the prophets who prophesied about the grace \[G-CHARIS\] that was to be yours searched and enquired carefully,

**11** enquiring what person or time the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of Christ in them was indicating when he predicted the sufferings of Christ and the glories that would follow.

**12** It was revealed to them that they were serving not themselves but you, in the things that have now been announced to you through those who preached the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] to you by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven, things into which angels long to look.

**13** Therefore, preparing your minds for action, and being sober-minded, set your hope fully on the grace \[G-CHARIS\] that will be brought to you at the revealing of Jesus Christ.

**14** As obedient children, do not be conformed to the passions of your former ignorance,

**15** but as he who called you is holy \[G-HAGIOS\], you also be holy in all your conduct,

**16** since it is written, "You shall be holy, for I am holy."

**17** And if you call on him as Father who judges impartially according to each one's deeds, conduct yourselves with fear throughout the time of your exile,

**18** knowing that you were ransomed \[G-LYTRON\] from the futile ways inherited from your fathers, not with perishable things such as silver or gold,

**19** but with the precious blood of Christ, like that of a lamb without blemish or spot.

**20** He was foreknown before the foundation of the world but was made manifest in the last times for your sake,

**21** who through him are believers \[G-PISTIS\] in God, who raised him from the dead and gave him glory \[G-DOXA\], so that your faith and hope are in God.

**22** Having purified your souls \[G-PSYCHE\] by your obedience to the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] for a sincere brotherly love, love \[G-AGAPE\] one another earnestly from a pure heart,

**23** since you have been born again, not of perishable seed but of imperishable, through the living and abiding word of God.

**24** For "All flesh is like grass, and all its glory \[G-DOXA\] like the flower of grass. The grass withers, and the flower falls,

**25** but the word of the Lord remains for ever." And this word is the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] that was preached to you.

### Chapter 2

**1** So put away all malice and all deceit and hypocrisy and envy and all slander.

**2** Like newborn infants, long for the pure spiritual milk, that by it you may grow up into salvation \[G-SOZO\],

**3** if indeed you have tasted that the Lord is good.

**4** As you come to him, a living stone rejected by people but in the sight of God chosen and precious,

**5** you yourselves like living stones are being built up as a spiritual house, to be a holy \[G-HAGIOS\] priesthood, to offer spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.

**6** For it stands in scripture: "Look, I am laying in Zion a stone, a cornerstone chosen and precious, and whoever believes \[G-PISTIS\] in him will not be put to shame."

**7** So the honour is for you who believe, but for those who do not believe, "The stone that the builders rejected has become the cornerstone,"

**8** and "A stone of stumbling, and a rock of offence." They stumble because they disobey the word, as they were destined to do.

**9** But you are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy \[G-HAGIOS\] nation, a people for his own possession, that you may proclaim the excellencies of him who called you out of darkness into his marvellous light.

**10** Once you were not a people, but now you are God's people. Once you had not received mercy \[G-ELEOS\], but now you have received mercy.

**11** Beloved \[G-AGAPE\], I urge you as sojourners and exiles to abstain from the passions of the flesh, which wage war against your soul \[G-PSYCHE\].

**12** Keep your conduct among the nations honourable, so that when they speak against you as evildoers, they may see your good deeds and glorify God on the day of visitation.

**13** Be subject for the Lord's sake to every human institution, whether it be to the emperor as supreme,

**14** or to governors as sent by him to punish those who do evil and to praise those who do good.

**15** For this is the will of God, that by doing good you should put to silence the ignorance of foolish people.

**16** Live as free people, not using your freedom as a cover-up for evil, but living as servants of God.

**17** Honour everyone. Love the brotherhood. Fear God. Honour the emperor.

**18** Household servants, be subject to your masters with all respect, not only to the good and gentle but also to the unjust.

**19** For this is a gracious thing \[G-CHARIS\], when, mindful of God, one endures sorrows while suffering unjustly.

**20** For what credit is it if, when you sin and are beaten for it, you endure? But if when you do good and suffer for it you endure, this is a gracious thing \[G-CHARIS\] in the sight of God.

**21** For to this you were called, because Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] also suffered for you, leaving you an example, so that you might follow in his steps.

**22** He committed no sin, neither was deceit found in his mouth.

**23** When he was reviled, he did not revile in return. When he suffered, he did not threaten, but continued entrusting himself to him who judges justly.

**24** He himself bore our sins in his body on the tree, that we might die to sin and live to righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\]. By his wounds you have been healed.

**25** For you were straying like sheep, but have now returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls \[G-PSYCHE\].

### Chapter 3

**1** Likewise, wives, be subject to your own husbands, so that even if some do not obey the word, they may be won without a word by the conduct of their wives,

**2** when they see your respectful and pure conduct.

**3** Do not let your adorning be external, the braiding of hair and the putting on of gold jewellery, or the clothing you wear,

**4** but let your adorning be the hidden person of the heart with the imperishable beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], which in God's sight is very precious.

**5** For this is how the holy \[G-HAGIOS\] women who hoped in God used to adorn themselves, by submitting to their own husbands,

**6** as Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord. And you are her children, if you do good and do not fear anything that is frightening.

**7** Likewise, husbands, live with your wives in an understanding way, showing honour to the woman as the weaker vessel, since they are heirs with you of the grace \[G-CHARIS\] of life \[G-ZOE\], so that your prayers may not be hindered.

**8** Finally, all of you, have unity of mind, sympathy, brotherly love, a tender heart, and a humble mind.

**9** Do not repay evil for evil or reviling for reviling, but on the contrary, bless, for to this you were called, that you may obtain a blessing.

**10** For "Whoever desires to love life \[G-ZOE\] and see good days, let him keep his tongue from evil and his lips from speaking deceit.

**11** Let him turn away from evil and do good. Let him seek peace \[G-EIRENE\] and pursue it.

**12** For the eyes of the Lord are on the righteous, and his ears are open to their prayer. But the face of the Lord is against those who do evil."

**13** Now who is there to harm you if you are zealous for what is good?

**14** But even if you should suffer for righteousness' \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] sake, you will be blessed \[G-MAKARIOS\]. Have no fear of them, nor be troubled,

**15** but set Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] apart as Lord in your hearts, always being prepared to make a defence to anyone who asks you for a reason for the hope that is in you. Yet do it with gentleness and respect,

**16** having a good conscience, so that, when you are slandered, those who revile your good conduct in Christ may be put to shame.

**17** For it is better to suffer for doing good, if that should be God's will, than for doing evil.

**18** For Christ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, that he might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh but made alive in the spirit \[G-PNEUMA\],

**19** in which he went and proclaimed to the spirits in prison,

**20** because they formerly did not obey, when God's patience waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was being prepared, in which a few, that is, eight persons, were brought safely through water.

**21** Baptism, which corresponds to this, now saves \[G-SOZO\] you, not as a removal of dirt from the body but as an appeal to God for a good conscience, through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,

**22** who has gone into heaven and is at the right hand of God, with angels, authorities, and powers having been subjected to him.

### Chapter 4

**1** Since therefore Christ suffered in the flesh, arm yourselves with the same way of thinking, for whoever has suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin,

**2** so as to live for the rest of the time in the flesh no longer for human passions but for the will of God.

**3** For the time already past suffices for doing what the nations want to do, living in sensuality, passions, drunkenness, orgies, drinking parties, and lawless idolatry.

**4** With respect to this they are surprised when you do not join them in the same flood of debauchery, and they malign you,

**5** but they will give account to him who is ready to judge the living and the dead.

**6** For this is why the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] was preached even to those who are dead, that though judged in the flesh the way people are, they might live in the spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] the way God does.

**7** The end of all things is at hand. Therefore be self-controlled and sober-minded for the sake of your prayers.

**8** Above all, keep loving \[G-AGAPE\] one another earnestly, since love covers a multitude of sins.

**9** Show hospitality to one another without grumbling.

**10** As each has received a gift \[G-CHARIS\], use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God's varied grace \[G-CHARIS\].

**11** Whoever speaks, as one who speaks the oracles of God. Whoever serves, as one who serves by the strength that God supplies, in order that in everything God may be glorified through Jesus Christ. To him belong glory \[G-DOXA\] and dominion for ever and ever. Amen.

**12** Beloved, do not be surprised at the fiery trial when it comes upon you to test you, as though something strange were happening to you.

**13** But rejoice insofar as you share Christ's sufferings, that you may also rejoice and be glad when his glory \[G-DOXA\] is revealed.

**14** If you are insulted for the name of Christ, you are blessed \[G-MAKARIOS\], because the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of glory and of God rests upon you.

**15** But let none of you suffer as a murderer or a thief or an evildoer or as a meddler.

**16** Yet if anyone suffers as a Christian, let him not be ashamed, but let him glorify God in that name.

**17** For it is time for judgement to begin at the household of God. And if it begins with us, what will be the outcome for those who do not obey the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of God?

**18** And "If the righteous is scarcely saved \[G-SOZO\], what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?"

**19** Therefore let those who suffer according to God's will entrust their souls \[G-PSYCHE\] to a faithful Creator while doing good.

### Chapter 5

**1** So I exhort the elders among you, as a fellow elder and a witness of the sufferings of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] as well as a partaker in the glory \[G-DOXA\] that is going to be revealed:

**2** shepherd the flock of God that is among you, exercising oversight, not under compulsion, but willingly, as God would have you, not for shameful gain, but eagerly,

**3** not domineering over those in your charge, but being examples to the flock.

**4** And when the chief Shepherd appears, you will receive the unfading crown of glory \[G-DOXA\].

**5** Likewise, you who are younger, be subject to the elders. Clothe yourselves, all of you, with humility toward one another, for "God opposes the proud but gives grace \[G-CHARIS\] to the humble."

**6** Humble yourselves, therefore, under the mighty hand of God so that at the proper time he may exalt you,

**7** casting all your anxieties on him, because he cares for you.

**8** Be sober-minded, be watchful. Your adversary the devil prowls around like a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour.

**9** Resist him, firm in your faith \[G-PISTIS\], knowing that the same kinds of suffering are being experienced by your brotherhood throughout the world.

**10** And after you have suffered a little while, the God of all grace \[G-CHARIS\], who has called you to his eternal glory \[G-DOXA\] in Christ, will himself restore, confirm, strengthen, and establish you.

**11** To him be the dominion for ever and ever. Amen.

**12** By Silvanus, a faithful brother as I regard him, I have written briefly to you, exhorting and declaring that this is the true grace \[G-CHARIS\] of God. Stand firm in it.

**13** She who is at Babylon, who is likewise chosen, sends you greetings, and so does Mark, my son.

**14** Greet one another with the kiss of love \[G-AGAPE\]. Peace \[G-EIRENE\] to all of you who are in Christ.

*Review note: 1 Peter 1 to 5 were drafted in WABV wording, from the Greek, using the fixed choices set out in the introduction to this book, and with the WABV voice standard (Part 46 of the review package) applied. They are the greeting (1:1-2), the living hope and the inheritance (1:3-9), the prophets and the angels (1:10-12), the call to holiness and the ransom by the blood of Christ (1:13-21), the new birth and the word that remains (1:22-25), the living stone and the holy people (2:1-10), conduct among the nations and submission to authorities (2:11-17), household servants and the example of Christ (2:18-25), wives and husbands (3:1-7), the call to unity, blessing and doing good (3:8-17), Christ who suffered once, the spirits in prison and baptism (3:18-22), living for the will of God (4:1-6), love, hospitality and gifts (4:7-11), the fiery trial (4:12-19), the elders and the younger (5:1-5), humility and the adversary (5:6-11) and the closing (5:12-14). The numbering is the usual numbering. No new G- marker is used. The main points are these, and the footnotes carry the Greek and the sources: the household code (2:18-3:7) is rendered as written, with no comment, and "household servants" translates *oiketai*, and the footnote records that the passage presupposes the institution of slavery and does not address it; at 3:15 "set Christ apart as Lord in your hearts" translates *kurion de ton Christon hagiasate*, and the verb is tagged G-HAGIOS in the sense of making holy, and "sanctify" is not used; at 3:18-22 the passage on the spirits in prison and baptism is rendered as written, with no comment, and it is one of the hardest in the New Testament, and the WABV text does not decide who the spirits are, when Christ proclaimed to them, or what baptism "saves" means; at 4:6 "the good news was preached even to those who are dead" is rendered as written, and the WABV text does not decide who the dead are; at 1:22 and 3:8 "brotherly love" translates *philadelphia* and is not tagged G-AGAPE; at 5:13 "she who is at Babylon" is rendered as written, and "Babylon" is taken by most readers to mean Rome. Several readings are drafted with some uncertainty and are to be confirmed against the critical edition in use: 1:8 (*idontes* or *eidotes*), 1:22 ("pure" heart), 2:21 ("for you" or "for us"), 3:15 ("Christ" or "God"), 3:18 ("suffered" or "died", "you" or "us"), 4:1 (the Byzantine "for us"), 4:14 (the Byzantine addition), 5:2 (the Byzantine "exercising oversight") and 5:10 ("Christ" or "Christ Jesus"). There are no departures from the critical Greek text in chapters 1 to 5. The Greek is hard in some places, including 1:11, 3:19-21, 4:6 and 5:12. The words on the living hope (1:3-5), the ransom (1:18-19), the living stone (2:4-8), a chosen race, a royal priesthood (2:9), the example of Christ (2:21-25), the spirits in prison (3:19-20), baptism (3:21), love that covers a multitude of sins (4:8), the fiery trial (4:12-14) and the roaring lion (5:8) are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. The section needs a check against the Greek by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked. With this the book of 1 Peter is complete in first draft.*

- **3:20** "Eight persons were brought safely through water" is rendered as written, and see Genesis 6 to 8.
- **3:21** "Baptism, which corresponds to this, now saves you" is rendered as written, and "an appeal to God for a good conscience" translates *suneideseos agathes eperotema eis theon*, and *eperotema* may also be read "a pledge" or "a response", and the footnote records the matter.
- **3:22** "Angels, authorities, and powers having been subjected to him" is rendered as written.
- **4:1** "Christ suffered in the flesh" is the reading that the draft follows, and the Byzantine tradition adds "for us", and "has ceased from sin" is rendered as written, and the Greek is hard.
- **4:6** "The good news was preached even to those who are dead" is rendered as written, with no comment on how it is read, and the footnote records that it may refer to those who heard the good news and have since died, or to those who died before Christ, and the WABV text does not decide. See entries G-EUANGELION and G-PNEUMA.
- **4:8** "Love covers a multitude of sins" is from Proverbs 10:12. See entry G-AGAPE.
- **4:10** "God's varied grace" translates *poikiles charitos theou*. See entry G-CHARIS.
- **4:11** "As one who speaks the oracles of God" is rendered as written. See entry G-DOXA.
- **4:12** "The fiery trial" is rendered as written.
- **4:14** "The Spirit of glory and of God rests upon you" is the reading of the critical text, and the Byzantine tradition adds "on their part he is blasphemed, but on your part he is glorified". See entries G-MAKARIOS and G-PNEUMA.
- **4:16** "A Christian" translates *christianos*, and it is used in the New Testament only here and at Acts 11:26 and 26:28.
- **4:17** "Judgement to begin at the household of God" is rendered as written. See entry G-EUANGELION.
- **4:18** The words are from Proverbs 11:31 as in the Septuagint. See entry G-SOZO.
- **4:19** "To a faithful Creator" is rendered as written. See entry G-PSYCHE.
- **5:1** "A fellow elder" translates *sumpresbuteros*, and "a partaker in the glory that is going to be revealed" is rendered as written. See entries G-CHRISTOS and G-DOXA.
- **5:2** "Exercising oversight" is the reading that the draft follows (*episkopountes*), and some manuscripts omit it, and "as God would have you" translates *kata theon*, and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **5:4** "The chief Shepherd" is rendered as written, and "the unfading crown of glory" is rendered as written.
- **5:5** The words are from Proverbs 3:34 as in the Septuagint. See entry G-CHARIS.
- **5:8** "Your adversary the devil prowls around like a roaring lion" is rendered as written.
- **5:9** "Firm in your faith" is rendered as written. See entry G-PISTIS.
- **5:10** "The God of all grace, who has called you to his eternal glory in Christ" is the reading that the draft follows, and some manuscripts read "in Christ Jesus", and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **5:12** "By Silvanus" may mean that he wrote the letter, carried it or both, and "the true grace of God" is rendered as written.
- **5:13** "She who is at Babylon" is rendered as written, and "Babylon" is taken by most readers to mean Rome, and "Mark, my son" is rendered as written.
- **5:14** "The kiss of love" is as in Romans 16:16. See entries G-AGAPE and G-EIRENE.
