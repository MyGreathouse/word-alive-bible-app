# WABV: Luke

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-PNEUMA\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Luke is the third book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. The book is a gospel, the longest of the four, and it is the first part of a work in two parts, with the book of Acts, which is addressed, like this book, to Theophilus (1:3; Acts 1:1). Its Greek is more formal than Mark's, with a periodic sentence as a preface (1:1-4), long sentences in the narrative, and several songs: the songs of Mary (1:46-55), Zechariah (1:68-79), the angels (2:14) and Simeon (2:29-32). It shares much with Mark and with Matthew, and has much that is only in Luke, among it the parables of the good Samaritan, the lost coin, the two sons, the dishonest manager, and the rich man and Lazarus, and the story of Zacchaeus. The book is anonymous in the Greek, and the name "Luke" comes from the title and from early tradition. The WABV text does not decide on the questions of authorship, date or sources, and the footnotes record only the language. The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. Some verses that the Byzantine tradition has are not in the critical Greek text, and the verse numbers are kept, with a footnote. The words "Son of Man", "brothers" and "Christ" are open decisions, and they are rendered literally in the draft and flagged in the footnotes for the team. The book falls into eight parts in the WABV text: the births and the beginning (chapters 1 to 3), the testing and the sermon on the plain (4 to 6), the mighty acts and the road begun (7 to 9), the seventy-two and the teaching on the way (10 to 12), the parables of the lost and the call to repent (13 to 16), the way to Jerusalem (17 to 19), the teaching in the temple and the supper (20 to 22), and the death, the resurrection and the ascension (23 and 24). The headings of the parts are not in the Greek. The Gospel voice sheet (Part 40 of the review package) applies. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System, and the new ones are listed in the review note of the part where each first appears. The names of God and of Jesus are given as the Greek has them: "God" for *theos*, "the Lord" for *kyrios*, "Jesus" and "Christ". The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Part One: Chapters 1 to 3, the births and the beginning

### Chapter 1

**1** Since many have taken in hand to draw up an account of the events that have been fulfilled among us,

**2** just as those who from the beginning were eyewitnesses and servants of the word handed them down to us,

**3** it seemed good to me also, having followed everything closely from the start, to write it out for you in order, most excellent Theophilus,

**4** so that you may know the certainty of the things you have been taught.

**5** In the days of Herod, king of Judea, there was a priest named Zechariah, of the division of Abijah, and his wife was one of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.

**6** Both of them were righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] before God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and requirements of the Lord.

**7** But they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and both were advanced in years.

**8** Now it happened that while he was serving as priest before God in the order of his division,

**9** according to the custom of the priesthood he was chosen by lot to enter the temple of the Lord and burn incense.

**10** And the whole crowd of the people was praying outside at the hour of the incense.

**11** An angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing on the right side of the altar of incense.

**12** When Zechariah saw him he was troubled, and fear fell on him.

**13** But the angel said to him, "Do not be afraid, Zechariah, for your prayer has been heard, and your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you will name him John.

**14** You will have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth,

**15** for he will be great before the Lord. He will drink no wine or strong drink, and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] even from his mother's womb.

**16** And he will turn many of the sons of Israel to the Lord their God.

**17** And he will go before him in the spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of fathers to children and the disobedient to the wisdom of the righteous, to make ready for the Lord a people prepared."

**18** Zechariah said to the angel, "How will I know this? For I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in years."

**19** The angel answered him, "I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God, and I was sent to speak to you and to announce this good news \[G-EUANGELION\] to you.

**20** And look, you will be silent and unable to speak until the day these things happen, because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled in their time."

**21** The people were waiting for Zechariah, and they were wondering at his delay in the temple.

**22** When he came out he could not speak to them, and they realised that he had seen a vision in the temple. He kept making signs to them and remained mute.

**23** When his days of service were completed, he went away to his home.

**24** After these days his wife Elizabeth conceived, and she hid herself for five months, saying,

**25** "This is what the Lord has done for me in the days when he looked on me to take away my disgrace among people."

**26** In the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a town in Galilee named Nazareth,

**27** to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David, and the virgin's name was Mary.

**28** He came to her and said, "Greetings, favoured one \[G-CHARIS\], the Lord is with you."

**29** But she was greatly troubled at the saying and wondered what kind of greeting this might be.

**30** The angel said to her, "Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favour \[G-CHARIS\] with God.

**31** And look, you will conceive in your womb and bear a son, and you will name him Jesus.

**32** He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High, and the Lord God will give him the throne of his father David,

**33** and he will reign over the house of Jacob for ever, and of his kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] there will be no end."

**34** Mary said to the angel, "How will this be, since I do not know a man?"

**35** The angel answered her, "The Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] will come on you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore the child to be born will be called holy \[G-HAGIOS\], the Son of God.

**36** And look, your relative Elizabeth has also conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month for her who was called barren.

**37** For nothing will be impossible with God."

**38** Mary said, "Look, I am the slave of the Lord. Let it be to me according to your word." And the angel left her.

**39** In those days Mary got up and went with haste into the hill country, to a town of Judah,

**40** and she entered the house of Zechariah and greeted Elizabeth.

**41** When Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the baby leapt in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\],

**42** and she cried out with a loud voice, "Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb.

**43** And why has this happened to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?

**44** For look, when the sound of your greeting reached my ears, the baby in my womb leapt for joy.

**45** And blessed \[G-MAKARIOS\] is she who believed that there would be a fulfilment of what was spoken to her from the Lord."

**46** And Mary said, "My soul \[G-PSYCHE\] makes the Lord great,

**47** and my spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] exults in God my saviour,

**48** because he has looked on the lowliness of his slave. For look, from now on all generations will count me blessed \[G-MAKARIOS\],

**49** because the Mighty One has done great things for me, and holy \[G-HAGIOS\] is his name,

**50** and his mercy \[G-ELEOS\] reaches from generation to generation to those who fear him.

**51** He has done a mighty deed with his arm. He has scattered those who are proud in the thinking of their hearts.

**52** He has pulled rulers down from thrones and lifted up the lowly.

**53** The hungry he has filled with good things, and the rich he has sent away empty.

**54** He has taken the side of Israel his servant, remembering mercy \[G-ELEOS\],

**55** just as he spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and his seed for ever."

**56** Mary stayed with her about three months and then returned to her home.

**57** Now the time came for Elizabeth to give birth, and she bore a son.

**58** Her neighbours and relatives heard that the Lord had shown great mercy \[G-ELEOS\] to her, and they rejoiced with her.

**59** On the eighth day they came to circumcise the child, and they were going to name him Zechariah after his father,

**60** but his mother answered, "No. He is to be called John."

**61** They said to her, "None of your relatives has this name."

**62** And they made signs to his father, asking what he wanted him to be called.

**63** He asked for a writing tablet and wrote, "His name is John." And they were all amazed.

**64** Immediately his mouth was opened and his tongue loosed, and he spoke, blessing God.

**65** Fear came on all their neighbours, and all these things were talked about through the whole hill country of Judea,

**66** and all who heard them stored them up in their hearts, saying, "What then will this child be?" For the hand of the Lord was with him.

**67** His father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] and prophesied, saying,

**68** "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, because he has visited his people and brought them redemption \[G-LYTRON\],

**69** and has raised up for us a horn of salvation \[G-SOZO\] in the house of David his servant,

**70** as he spoke through the mouth of his holy \[G-HAGIOS\] prophets from the beginning of the age,

**71** salvation \[G-SOZO\] from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us,

**72** to deal mercifully \[G-ELEOS\] with our fathers and to remember his holy \[G-HAGIOS\] covenant \[G-DIATHEKE\],

**73** the oath he swore to Abraham our father,

**74** to grant us, rescued from the hand of enemies, to serve him without fear,

**75** in holiness and righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] before him all our days.

**76** And you, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High, for you will go ahead of the Lord to make ready his roads,

**77** to give his people knowledge of salvation \[G-SOZO\] through the forgiveness of their sins,

**78** through the deep compassion \[G-ELEOS\] of our God, by which the sunrise from on high will visit us,

**79** to shine on those sitting in darkness and the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the road of peace \[G-EIRENE\]."

**80** The child grew and became strong in spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], and he was in the wilderness until the day of his public appearance to Israel.

### Chapter 2

**1** In those days a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that the whole inhabited world should be registered.

**2** This was the first registration, when Quirinius was governing Syria.

**3** And all went to be registered, each to his own town.

**4** Joseph also went up from Galilee, from the town of Nazareth, to Judea, to the city of David called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David,

**5** to be registered with Mary, who was betrothed to him, and was pregnant.

**6** While they were there, the days were completed for her to give birth,

**7** and she gave birth to her firstborn son and wrapped him in strips of cloth and laid him in a feeding trough, because there was no room for them in the guest room.

**8** In that region there were shepherds living out in the fields and keeping watch over their flock by night.

**9** An angel of the Lord stood before them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were terrified.

**10** The angel said to them, "Do not be afraid, for look, I bring you good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of great joy that will be for all the people.

**11** For today in the city of David a saviour has been born for you, who is Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] the Lord.

**12** And this will be the sign for you: you will find a baby wrapped in strips of cloth and lying in a feeding trough."

**13** Suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army, praising God and saying,

**14** "Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace \[G-EIRENE\] among people of his favour."

**15** When the angels went away from them into heaven, the shepherds said to one another, "Let us go over to Bethlehem and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has made known to us."

**16** They went with haste and found Mary and Joseph and the baby lying in the feeding trough.

**17** When they saw it, they made known the saying that had been told them about this child.

**18** And all who heard it were amazed at what the shepherds told them.

**19** But Mary treasured up all these things, pondering them in her heart.

**20** The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all they had heard and seen, as it had been told them.

**21** When eight days were completed for his circumcision, he was named Jesus, the name given by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.

**22** When the days of their purification were completed according to the Law \[G-NOMOS\] of Moses, they brought him up to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord

**23** (as it is written in the Law of the Lord, "Every male who opens the womb will be called holy \[G-HAGIOS\] to the Lord"),

**24** and to offer a sacrifice according to what is said in the Law of the Lord, "a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons."

**25** Now there was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon, and this man was righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] and devout, waiting for the consolation of Israel, and the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] was on him.

**26** It had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] that he would not see death before he had seen the Lord's Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\].

**27** He came in the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] into the temple, and when the parents brought in the child Jesus, to do for him what was customary under the Law,

**28** he took him in his arms and blessed God and said,

**29** "Now, Master, you are releasing your slave in peace \[G-EIRENE\], according to your word,

**30** for my eyes have seen your salvation \[G-SOZO\],

**31** which you have prepared in the presence of all peoples,

**32** a light for revelation to the nations and glory for your people Israel."

**33** His father and mother were amazed at what was said about him.

**34** Simeon blessed them and said to Mary his mother, "Look, this child is set for the falling and rising of many in Israel, and for a sign that is opposed

**35** (and a sword will pierce your own soul \[G-PSYCHE\] too), so that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed."

**36** There was also a prophet, Anna, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher. She was advanced in years, having lived with her husband seven years from her virginity,

**37** and then as a widow until she was eighty-four. She did not leave the temple, worshipping with fasting and prayer night and day.

**38** Coming up at that very hour, she gave thanks to God and spoke about the child to all who were waiting for the redemption \[G-LYTRON\] of Jerusalem.

**39** When they had finished everything according to the Law of the Lord, they returned to Galilee, to their own town of Nazareth.

**40** The child grew and became strong, filled with wisdom, and the favour \[G-CHARIS\] of God was on him.

**41** His parents went to Jerusalem every year at the feast of the Passover.

**42** And when he was twelve years old, they went up according to the custom of the feast.

**43** When the days were completed and they were returning, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem. His parents did not know it,

**44** but supposing him to be in the company of travellers, they went a day's journey, and then began to look for him among their relatives and acquaintances,

**45** and when they did not find him, they returned to Jerusalem, looking for him.

**46** After three days they found him in the temple, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions.

**47** All who heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.

**48** When they saw him they were astonished, and his mother said to him, "Child, why have you treated us like this? Look, your father and I have been searching for you in great distress."

**49** He said to them, "Why were you searching for me? Did you not know that I must be in my Father's house?"

**50** But they did not understand the saying that he spoke to them.

**51** He went down with them and came to Nazareth, and he was obedient to them. His mother treasured up all these things in her heart.

**52** And Jesus increased in wisdom and in stature and in favour \[G-CHARIS\] with God and people.

### Chapter 3

**1** In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, when Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea, and Herod was ruler of Galilee, and his brother Philip ruler of the region of Ituraea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias ruler of Abilene,

**2** in the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas, the word of God came to John the son of Zechariah in the wilderness.

**3** He went into all the region around the Jordan, announcing a baptism of repentance \[G-METANOIA\] for the forgiveness of sins,

**4** as it is written in the book of the words of Isaiah the prophet: "A voice shouting in the wilderness: Prepare the way of the Lord, make his paths straight.

**5** Every valley will be filled, and every mountain and hill will be made low, and the crooked will become straight, and the rough ways smooth,

**6** and all flesh will see the salvation \[G-SOZO\] of God."

**7** He said to the crowds who came out to be baptised by him, "You brood of vipers, who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?

**8** Produce fruits worthy of repentance \[G-METANOIA\]. And do not begin to say to yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father.' For I tell you that God can raise up children for Abraham from these stones.

**9** Already the axe is laid at the root of the trees. Every tree that does not produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire."

**10** The crowds asked him, "What then should we do?"

**11** He answered them, "Whoever has two tunics must share with the one who has none, and whoever has food must do the same."

**12** Tax collectors also came to be baptised, and they said to him, "Teacher, what should we do?"

**13** He said to them, "Collect no more than you have been ordered to."

**14** Soldiers also asked him, "And we, what should we do?" He said to them, "Do not extort money from anyone by threats or false accusation, and be content with your wages."

**15** The people were in expectation, and all were wondering in their hearts about John, whether he might be the Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\],

**16** John answered them all, "I baptise you with water, but one who is stronger than I is coming, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy to untie. He will baptise you with the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] and fire.

**17** His winnowing fork is in his hand, to clear his threshing floor and to gather the wheat into his barn, but the chaff he will burn with unquenchable fire."

**18** With many other exhortations he announced the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] to the people.

**19** But Herod the ruler, who had been rebuked by him about Herodias, his brother's wife, and about all the evil things that Herod had done,

**20** added this to them all: he locked John up in prison.

**21** When all the people were baptised, and Jesus also had been baptised and was praying, the heaven was opened,

**22** and the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] descended on him in bodily form like a dove, and a voice came from heaven: "You are my Son, the beloved \[G-AGAPE\]. In you I am well pleased."

**23** Jesus himself, when he began, was about thirty years old, being the son (as was supposed) of Joseph, the son of Heli,

**24** the son of Matthat, the son of Levi, the son of Melchi, the son of Jannai, the son of Joseph,

**25** the son of Mattathias, the son of Amos, the son of Nahum, the son of Esli, the son of Naggai,

**26** the son of Maath, the son of Mattathias, the son of Semein, the son of Josech, the son of Joda,

**27** the son of Joanan, the son of Rhesa, the son of Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, the son of Neri,

**28** the son of Melchi, the son of Addi, the son of Cosam, the son of Elmadam, the son of Er,

**29** the son of Joshua, the son of Eliezer, the son of Jorim, the son of Matthat, the son of Levi,

**30** the son of Simeon, the son of Judah, the son of Joseph, the son of Jonam, the son of Eliakim,

**31** the son of Melea, the son of Menna, the son of Mattatha, the son of Nathan, the son of David,

**32** the son of Jesse, the son of Obed, the son of Boaz, the son of Sala, the son of Nahshon,

**33** the son of Amminadab, the son of Admin, the son of Arni, the son of Hezron, the son of Perez, the son of Judah,

**34** the son of Jacob, the son of Isaac, the son of Abraham, the son of Terah, the son of Nahor,

**35** the son of Serug, the son of Reu, the son of Peleg, the son of Eber, the son of Shelah,

**36** the son of Cainan, the son of Arphaxad, the son of Shem, the son of Noah, the son of Lamech,

**37** the son of Methuselah, the son of Enoch, the son of Jared, the son of Mahalaleel, the son of Cainan,

**38** the son of Enos, the son of Seth, the son of Adam, the son of God.

They are the preface (1:1-4), the birth of John foretold to Zechariah (1:5-25), the annunciation to Mary (1:26-38), the visit to Elizabeth and the song of Mary (1:39-56), the birth of John and the song of Zechariah (1:57-80), the birth of Jesus and the shepherds (2:1-20), the presentation, with Simeon and Anna (2:21-40), the boy in the temple (2:41-52), the ministry of John the baptiser (3:1-20), the baptism of Jesus (3:21-22) and the genealogy (3:23-38). In 2:14 "among people of his favour" translates *en anthropois eudokias*, a genitive, and the Byzantine tradition has the nominative, "good will among people", and the footnote records the matter. In 2:22 "their purification" is the reading of the critical text, and some manuscripts read "her purification". In 2:33 "his father and mother" is the reading of the critical text, and some manuscripts read "Joseph and his mother". In 3:22 some manuscripts, in the Western tradition, read "today I have begotten you", from Psalm 2:7, in place of "in you I am well pleased", and the footnote records the matter. In 1:3 "most excellent Theophilus" is the Greek of the text, and the WABV text does not decide who he was. In 1:27 "a virgin" translates *parthenon*, as in Matthew 1:23. In 3:23-38 the genealogy goes up from Joseph to Adam, and it differs from the genealogy in Matthew 1:2-16, and the footnote records the matter. The names in the genealogy are given as in the WABV text of the Old Testament, and the Greek spellings are in the footnote. The word "sons" in 1:16 is literal, and in 3:23-38 "the son of" is literal. There are no departures from the critical Greek text in chapters 1 to 3. The Greek is hard in some places, including 1:1-4, 1:34 (*andra ou ginosko*), 2:2 (*prote hegemoneuontos*), 2:7 (*kataluma*), 2:14, 2:49 (*en tois tou patros mou*), 3:23 and 3:36. The footnotes record the main differences and the uncertain terms. The words on the conception of Jesus (1:34-35), the sign of the shepherds (2:12), the sword that will pierce Mary's soul (2:35) and "I must be in my Father's house" (2:49) are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. The section needs a check against the Greek by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked.*

## Part Two: Chapters 4 to 6, the testing, Nazareth and the sermon on the plain

### Chapter 4

**1** Jesus, full of the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], returned from the Jordan and was led by the Spirit in the wilderness

**2** for forty days, being tested by the devil. He ate nothing in those days, and when they were completed he was hungry.

**3** The devil said to him, "If you are the Son of God, tell this stone to become bread."

**4** Jesus answered him, "It is written: 'A person will not live on bread alone.'"

**5** Leading him up, he showed him all the kingdoms \[G-BASILEIA\] of the inhabited world in a moment of time,

**6** and the devil said to him, "To you I will give all this authority and their glory, for it has been handed over to me, and I give it to whomever I wish.

**7** If you, then, will bow down before me, it will all be yours."

**8** Jesus answered him, "It is written: 'You will bow down to the Lord your God, and him alone you will serve.'"

**9** He led him to Jerusalem and set him on the highest point of the temple and said to him, "If you are the Son of God, throw yourself down from here,

**10** for it is written: 'He will command his angels concerning you to guard you,'

**11** and 'On their hands they will lift you up, so that you do not strike your foot against a stone.'"

**12** Jesus answered him, "It is said: 'You will not put the Lord your God to the test.'"

**13** When the devil had finished every test, he left him until an opportune time.

**14** Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] to Galilee, and a report about him spread through the whole surrounding region.

**15** He taught in their synagogues, and was praised by everyone.

**16** He came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up, and as was his custom he went into the synagogue on the Sabbath \[G-SABBATON\] day, and stood up to read.

**17** The scroll of the prophet Isaiah was handed to him. He unrolled the scroll and found the place where it was written:

**18** "The Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of the Lord is on me, because he has anointed me to announce good news \[G-EUANGELION\] to the poor. He has sent me to proclaim release to the captives and recovery of sight to the blind, to send the oppressed away free,

**19** to proclaim the year of the Lord's favour."

**20** He rolled up the scroll, gave it back to the attendant and sat down. The eyes of everyone in the synagogue were fixed on him.

**21** He began to say to them, "Today this scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing."

**22** All spoke well of him and were amazed at the words of grace \[G-CHARIS\] that came out of his mouth, and they said, "Is this not Joseph's son?"

**23** He said to them, "Surely you will quote this proverb to me: 'Doctor, heal yourself.' 'Do here in your home town what we heard was done in Capernaum.'"

**24** He said, "Amen, I tell you, no prophet is accepted in his home town.

**25** But in truth I tell you, there were many widows in Israel in the days of Elijah, when the heaven was shut for three years and six months, and a great famine came over all the land,

**26** and Elijah was sent to none of them, but only to a widow at Zarephath in the land of Sidon.

**27** And there were many with skin disease in Israel in the time of the prophet Elisha, and none of them was cleansed, but only Naaman the Syrian."

**28** All in the synagogue were filled with rage when they heard this.

**29** They got up and drove him out of the town and led him to the brow of the hill on which their town was built, to throw him down the cliff,

**30** but he passed through the middle of them and went on his way.

**31** He went down to Capernaum, a town in Galilee, and he was teaching them on the Sabbath \[G-SABBATON\].

**32** They were astonished at his teaching, because his word had authority.

## Part Three: Chapters 7 to 9, the mighty acts and the road begun

### Chapter 7

**1** When he had finished all his words in the hearing of the people, he went into Capernaum.

**2** A centurion's slave, who was valued highly by him, was sick and at the point of death.

**3** When he heard about Jesus, he sent elders of the Jews to him, asking him to come and save \[G-SOZO\] his slave.

**6** Jesus went with them. When he was not far from the house, the centurion sent friends, saying to him, "Lord, do not trouble yourself, for I am not worthy to have you come under my roof.

**10** When those who had been sent returned to the house, they found the slave in good health.

**11** Soon afterwards he went to a town called Nain, and his disciples and a large crowd went with him.

**12** As he approached the gate of the town, look, a dead man was being carried out, the only son of his mother, and she was a widow, and a large crowd from the town was with her.

**13** When the Lord saw her, he was moved with compassion \[G-SPLANCHNIZOMAI\] for her and said to her, "Do not weep."

**15** The dead man sat up and began to speak, and Jesus gave him to his mother.

**16** Fear seized them all, and they glorified God, saying, "A great prophet has risen among us," and "God has visited his people."

**26** But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I tell you, and more than a prophet.

**29** (When all the people and the tax collectors heard this, they declared God righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\], having been baptised with the baptism of John,

**31** "To what then will I compare the people of this generation, and what are they like?

**33** For John the baptiser has come eating no bread and drinking no wine, and you say, 'He has a demon.'

**34** The Son of Man \[G-HUIOS-ANTHROPOU\] has come eating and drinking, and you say, 'Look, a glutton and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners.'

**35** Yet wisdom is justified by all her children."
