# WABV: 1 Corinthians

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-EKKLESIA\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. First Corinthians is the seventh book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the second of the letters. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It is a letter from Paul and Sosthenes to the assembly of God in Corinth, written from Ephesus (16:8) in about AD 54 to 55, and it answers reports that came from the household of Chloe (1:11) and questions that the Corinthians had sent in a letter (7:1). It is not one argument but a series of matters, taken one after another, and the WABV text keeps the signals that Paul gives for each ("now concerning", 7:1, 7:25, 8:1, 12:1, 16:1, 16:12). The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text of 1 Corinthians is drafted with the voice standard (Part 46 of the review package), and makes a number of fixed choices that follow from its Greek, and they are written down here so that they are used throughout. *Ekklesia* is rendered "assembly" and is tagged G-EKKLESIA, and the team should decide between "assembly" and "church". *Hagioi* is rendered "holy ones" and is tagged G-HAGIOS, and *hegiasmenoi* "made holy". *Logos tou staurou* (1:18) is rendered "the message of the cross". *Sophia* is rendered "wisdom", and *moria* "foolishness". *Kauchaomai* is rendered "boast". *Musterion* is rendered "mystery". *Psychikos* (2:14, 15:44-46) is rendered "natural", and the footnote records "unspiritual" and "soulish". *Pneumatikos* is rendered "spiritual", and *sarkinos* and *sarkikos* "of the flesh". *Oikonomos* is rendered "steward". *Charisma* is rendered "gift" and is tagged G-CHARIS. *Dikaioo* is rendered "declare righteous", as in Romans. *Pistis* is rendered "faith" and *pisteuo* "believe", and both are tagged G-PISTIS. *Agape* is rendered "love" and is tagged G-AGAPE. *Adelphoi* is rendered "brothers", and the footnote records that in many places it may include the sisters, and the team should decide. *Ioudaios* is rendered "Jew", and *ethne* "the nations". *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. The book falls into five parts in the WABV text: the divisions, the message of the cross, the wisdom of God and the servants of Christ (chapters 1 to 4), discipline, lawsuits, the body and marriage (5 to 7), food offered to idols, the rights of an apostle, the warnings, the head coverings and the Lord's supper (8 to 11), the gifts, the body of Christ, love, and tongues and prophecy (12 to 14), and the resurrection, the collection and the greetings (15 and 16). The headings of the parts are not in the Greek. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System, and the new ones are listed in the review note of the part where each first appears. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Part One: Chapters 1 to 4, the divisions, the message of the cross and the servants of Christ

### Chapter 1

**1** Paul, called to be an apostle of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] Jesus by the will of God, and Sosthenes our brother,

**2** to the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] of God in Corinth, to those who have been made holy \[G-HAGIOS\] in Christ Jesus, called to be holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\], together with all who in every place call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, their Lord and ours:

**3** grace \[G-CHARIS\] to you and peace \[G-EIRENE\] from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**4** I thank my God always for you, because of the grace \[G-CHARIS\] of God that was given to you in Christ Jesus,

**5** because in everything you were enriched in him, in all speech and all knowledge,

**6** since the testimony about Christ was made firm among you,

**7** so that you lack no gift \[G-CHARIS\] as you wait for our Lord Jesus Christ to be revealed.

**8** He will also keep you firm to the end, free of any charge on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**9** God is faithful, and through him you were called into partnership with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

**10** I urge you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ: all of you say the same thing, and let there be no splits among you, but be knit together in the same mind and the same judgement.

**11** For some of Chloe's people have told me about you, my brothers, that there are quarrels among you.

**12** I mean this: each one of you says, "I belong to Paul," or "I belong to Apollos," or "I belong to Cephas," or "I belong to Christ."

**13** Has Christ been divided up? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you baptised into the name of Paul?

**14** I thank God that I baptised none of you except Crispus and Gaius,

**15** so that no one can say that you were baptised into my name.

**16** (I did baptise the household of Stephanas as well. Beyond that, I do not know whether I baptised anyone else.)

**17** For Christ did not send me to baptise but to announce the good news \[G-EUANGELION\], and not in clever speech, so that the cross of Christ would not be emptied of its meaning.

**18** For the message of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved \[G-SOZO\] it is the power of God.

**19** For it is written: "I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and the intelligence of the intelligent I will set aside."

**20** Where is the wise person? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Has God not made the wisdom of the world foolish?

**21** For since, in the wisdom of God, the world through its wisdom did not know God, God chose to save \[G-SOZO\] those who believe \[G-PISTIS\] through the foolishness of the message.

**22** Jews demand signs and Greeks look for wisdom,

**23** but we proclaim Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] crucified: to Jews a stumbling block and to the nations foolishness,

**24** but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God and the wisdom of God.

**25** For the foolishness of God is wiser than people, and the weakness of God is stronger than people.

**26** Look at your own calling, brothers: not many of you were wise by human standards, not many were powerful, not many were well born.

**27** But God chose the foolish things of the world to shame the wise, and God chose the weak things of the world to shame the strong,

**28** and God chose the lowly things of the world and the despised, the things that are not, to bring to nothing the things that are,

**29** so that no human being could boast in the presence of God.

**30** It is from him that you are in Christ Jesus, who became for us wisdom from God, and righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] and holiness \[G-HAGIOS\] and redemption \[G-LYTRON\],

**31** so that, as it is written: "Let the one who boasts boast in the Lord."

### Chapter 2

**1** When I came to you, brothers, I did not come announcing the testimony of God with lofty speech or wisdom.

**2** For I decided to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ, and him crucified.

**3** I came to you in weakness and fear and much trembling,

**4** and my message and my proclamation were not in persuasive words of wisdom but in a showing of the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] and of power,

**5** so that your faith \[G-PISTIS\] would rest not on human wisdom but on the power of God.

**6** Yet among the mature we do speak wisdom, but not the wisdom of this age or of the rulers of this age, who are being brought to nothing.

**7** No, we speak God's wisdom in a mystery, the hidden wisdom that God marked out before the ages for our glory \[G-DOXA\].

**8** None of the rulers of this age knew it, for if they had known, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory \[G-DOXA\].

**9** But, as it is written: "What no eye has seen and no ear has heard, and what has not come up in the human heart, what God has prepared for those who love \[G-AGAPE\] him."

**10** God revealed it to us through the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\]. For the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God.

**11** For who among people knows what belongs to a person except the person's own spirit within? So also no one has known what belongs to God except the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of God.

**12** We have received not the spirit of the world but the Spirit that is from God, so that we may know the things freely given to us by God.

**13** We also speak of these things not in words taught by human wisdom but in words taught by the Spirit, explaining spiritual things with spiritual words.

**14** The natural person does not receive the things of the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of God, for to him they are foolishness, and he cannot know them, because they are examined in a spiritual way.

**15** The spiritual person examines everything, but is himself examined by no one.

**16** "For who has known the mind of the Lord, so as to instruct him?" But we have the mind of Christ.

### Chapter 3

**1** But I, brothers, could not speak to you as spiritual people but as people of the flesh, as infants in Christ.

**2** I gave you milk to drink, not solid food, for you were not ready for it. And you are not ready even now,

**3** for you are still of the flesh. For where there is jealousy and quarrelling among you, are you not of the flesh and living in an ordinary human way?

**4** For when one says, "I belong to Paul," and another, "I belong to Apollos," are you not ordinary human beings?

**5** What then is Apollos? What is Paul? Servants through whom you came to believe \[G-PISTIS\], as the Lord gave to each.

**6** I planted, Apollos watered, but God made it grow.

**7** So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but God who makes it grow.

**8** The one who plants and the one who waters are one, and each will receive his own reward for his own labour.

**9** For we are God's fellow workers. You are God's field, God's building.

**10** According to the grace \[G-CHARIS\] of God given to me, like a skilled master builder I laid a foundation, and someone else is building on it. Let each take care how he builds.

**11** For no one can lay any foundation other than the one that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.

**12** Now if anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay or straw,

**13** the work of each one will become visible, for the Day will make it plain, because it is revealed in fire, and the fire will test what kind of work each one has done.

**14** If the work that someone has built on the foundation stays, he will receive a reward.

**15** If someone's work is burned up, he will suffer loss, though he himself will be saved \[G-SOZO\], but only as through fire.

**16** Do you not know that you are God's temple and that the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of God lives in you?

**17** If anyone destroys God's temple, God will destroy him. For God's temple is holy \[G-HAGIOS\], and you are that temple.

**18** Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you thinks he is wise in this age, let him become a fool, so that he may become wise.

**19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it is written: "He catches the wise in their craftiness,"

**20** and again: "The Lord knows the reasonings of the wise, that they are empty."

**21** So let no one boast in people. For all things are yours,

**22** whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life \[G-ZOE\] or death or the present or the future, all are yours,

**23** and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.

### Chapter 4

**1** This is how a person should think of us: as servants of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] and stewards of the mysteries of God.

**2** In this matter, moreover, what is required of stewards is that each be found faithful.

**3** But for me it is a very small thing that I should be examined by you or by any human court. I do not even examine myself.

**4** For I am not aware of anything against myself, but I am not for that reason declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\]. The one who examines me is the Lord.

**5** So do not pass judgement before the time, until the Lord comes, who will bring to light the things hidden in the dark and will make plain the purposes of the hearts. Then each one will receive his praise from God.

**6** I have applied all this to myself and Apollos for your sake, brothers, so that you may learn from us what "Not beyond what is written" means, so that none of you will be puffed up in favour of one person against another.

**7** For who sees any difference in you? What do you have that you did not receive? And if you received it, why do you boast as though you had not received it?

**8** Already you have all you want. Already you have become rich. Without us you have become kings. And I wish you had become kings, so that we could reign with you.

**9** For I think that God has put us apostles on display last of all, like people sentenced to death, because we have become a spectacle to the world, to angels and to people.

**10** We are fools for the sake of Christ, but you are wise in Christ. We are weak, but you are strong. You are honoured, but we are dishonoured.

**11** To this very hour we go hungry and thirsty, we are poorly clothed and beaten and homeless,

**12** and we work hard, labouring with our own hands. When we are abused, we bless. When we are persecuted, we put up with it.

**13** When we are slandered, we answer gently. We have become like the rubbish of the world, the scrapings of everything, and so we are still.

**14** I am not writing this to shame you, but to warn you as my loved \[G-AGAPE\] children.

**15** For though you have countless guides in Christ, you do not have many fathers. For in Christ Jesus I became your father through the good news \[G-EUANGELION\].

**16** So I urge you, be imitators of me.

**17** That is why I have sent Timothy to you, my loved \[G-AGAPE\] and faithful child in the Lord. He will remind you of my ways in Christ, as I teach them everywhere in every assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\].

**18** Some have become puffed up, as though I were not coming to you.

**19** But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wills, and I will find out not the talk of these puffed-up people but their power.

**20** For the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of God is not a matter of talk but of power.

**21** What do you want? Am I to come to you with a rod, or with love \[G-AGAPE\] and a spirit of gentleness?

## Part Two: Chapters 5 to 7, discipline, lawsuits, the body and marriage

### Chapter 5

**1** It is actually reported that there is sexual immorality among you, of a kind that is not found even among the nations: a man has his father's wife.

**2** And you are puffed up. Should you not rather have mourned, so that the one who did this would be removed from among you?

**3** For I, absent in body but present in spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], have already passed judgement, as though I were present, on the one who did this,

**4** in the name of our Lord Jesus, when you are gathered together, and my spirit with you, with the power of our Lord Jesus,

**5** to hand such a person over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved \[G-SOZO\] on the day of the Lord.

**6** Your boasting is not good. Do you not know that a little yeast works through the whole lump?

**7** Clear out the old yeast, so that you may be a new lump, as you are unleavened. For our Passover lamb, Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], has been sacrificed.

**8** So let us keep the festival, not with the old yeast, nor with the yeast of badness and evil, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth \[G-ALETHEIA\].

**9** I wrote to you in my letter not to associate with the sexually immoral,

**10** not at all meaning the sexually immoral of this world, or the greedy and swindlers, or idolaters, since then you would need to leave the world.

**11** But now I am writing to you not to associate with anyone who bears the name of brother if he is sexually immoral or greedy or an idolater or abusive or a drunkard or a swindler. Do not even eat with such a person.

**12** For what is it to me to judge outsiders? Is it not those inside that you are to judge?

**13** God judges those outside. "Remove the evil person from among you."

### Chapter 6

**1** When one of you has a dispute with another, does he dare to go to law before the unrighteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] and not before the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\]?

**2** Or do you not know that the holy ones will judge the world? And if the world is to be judged by you, are you unfit to judge the smallest matters?

**3** Do you not know that we will judge angels? How much more the matters of this life.

**4** So if you have disputes about matters of this life, do you appoint as judges those who have no standing in the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\]?

**5** I say this to shame you. Is there no one among you wise enough to settle a dispute between brothers,

**6** but brother goes to law against brother, and before unbelievers?

**7** To have lawsuits at all against one another is already a defeat for you. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be cheated?

**8** But you yourselves wrong and cheat, and you do it to brothers.

**9** Or do you not know that the unrighteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] will not inherit the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of God? Do not be deceived: neither the sexually immoral, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor the soft, nor men who lie with men,

**10** nor thieves, nor the greedy, nor drunkards, nor the abusive, nor swindlers will inherit the kingdom of God.

**11** And some of you were like this. But you were washed, you were made holy \[G-HAGIOS\], you were declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and in the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of our God.

**12** "All things are permitted for me," but not all things are helpful. "All things are permitted for me," but I will not be mastered by anything.

**13** "Food is for the stomach and the stomach is for food," but God will do away with both of them. The body is not for sexual immorality but for the Lord, and the Lord is for the body.

**14** And God raised the Lord and will also raise us up by his power.

**15** Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Am I then to take the members of Christ and make them members of a prostitute? Certainly not.

**16** Or do you not know that the one who joins himself to a prostitute is one body with her? For it says, "The two will become one flesh."

**17** But the one who joins himself to the Lord is one spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] with him.

**18** Flee sexual immorality. Every other sin that a person commits is outside the body, but the sexually immoral person sins against his own body.

**19** Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] within you, whom you have from God? You are not your own,

**20** for you were bought with a price. So glorify \[G-DOXA\] God in your body.

### Chapter 7

**1** Now concerning the matters about which you wrote: "It is good for a man not to touch a woman."

**2** But because of sexual immorality, let each man have his own wife and each woman her own husband.

**3** The husband should give to his wife what is owed her, and likewise the wife to her husband.

**4** The wife does not have authority over her own body, but the husband does. Likewise the husband does not have authority over his own body, but the wife does.

**5** Do not deprive one another, except perhaps by agreement for a limited time, so that you may devote yourselves to prayer, and then come together again, so that Satan does not tempt you because of your lack of self-control.

**6** But I say this as a concession, not as a command.

**7** I wish that all were as I myself am. But each has his own gift \[G-CHARIS\] from God, one of one kind and one of another.

**8** To the unmarried and the widows I say that it is good for them to remain as I am.

**9** But if they cannot control themselves, they should marry. For it is better to marry than to burn.

**10** To the married I give this command (not I, but the Lord): the wife should not separate from her husband

**11** (but if she does separate, she should remain unmarried or be reconciled to her husband), and the husband should not divorce his wife.

**12** To the rest I say (I, not the Lord): if any brother has a wife who is an unbeliever, and she consents to live with him, he should not divorce her.

**13** And if any woman has a husband who is an unbeliever, and he consents to live with her, she should not divorce him.

**14** For the unbelieving husband is made holy \[G-HAGIOS\] through his wife, and the unbelieving wife is made holy through the brother. Otherwise your children would be unclean, but as it is they are holy.

**15** But if the unbeliever separates, let it be so. The brother or sister is not bound in such cases. God has called you to peace \[G-EIRENE\].

**16** For how do you know, wife, whether you will save \[G-SOZO\] your husband? Or how do you know, husband, whether you will save your wife?

**17** Only, let each person live the life that the Lord has assigned to him, and in which God has called him. This is my rule in all the assemblies \[G-EKKLESIA\].

**18** Was anyone called when circumcised? Let him not seek to remove the marks of it. Was anyone called when uncircumcised? Let him not be circumcised.

**19** Circumcision is nothing and uncircumcision is nothing, but keeping the commandments of God is what matters.

**20** Let each person remain in the calling in which he was called.

**21** Were you called as a slave? Do not let it trouble you. But if you can gain your freedom, make use of it instead.

**22** For the one who was called in the Lord as a slave is the Lord's freed person. Likewise the one who was called as a free person is Christ's \[G-CHRISTOS\] slave.

**23** You were bought with a price. Do not become slaves of people.

**24** In whatever condition each was called, brothers, there let him remain with God.

**25** Now concerning virgins I have no command from the Lord, but I give a judgement as one who by the Lord's mercy \[G-ELEOS\] is trustworthy.

**26** I think that, in view of the present distress, it is good for a person to remain as he is.

**27** Are you bound to a wife? Do not seek to be free. Are you free from a wife? Do not seek a wife.

**28** But if you do marry, you have not sinned, and if a virgin marries, she has not sinned. Yet those who marry will have trouble in this life, and I would spare you that.

**29** This is what I mean, brothers: the time is short. From now on, let those who have wives live as though they had none,

**30** and those who weep as though they were not weeping, and those who rejoice as though they were not rejoicing, and those who buy as though they had no possessions,

## Part Three: Chapters 8 to 11, food offered to idols, the apostle, the warnings, the head coverings and the Lord's supper

### Chapter 8

**1** Now concerning food offered to idols: we know that "all of us have knowledge." Knowledge puffs up, but love \[G-AGAPE\] builds up.

**2** If anyone thinks that he knows something, he does not yet know as he ought to know.

**3** But if anyone loves \[G-AGAPE\] God, this person is known by him.

**4** So, concerning the eating of food offered to idols, we know that "an idol is nothing in the world" and that "there is no God but one."

**5** For even if there are so-called gods, whether in heaven or on earth (as indeed there are many "gods" and many "lords"),

**6** yet for us there is one God, the Father, from whom are all things and for whom we exist, and one Lord, Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], through whom are all things and through whom we exist.

**7** But not everyone has this knowledge. Some, through being accustomed until now to the idol, eat the food as food offered to an idol, and their conscience, being weak, is defiled.

**8** Food will not bring us near to God. We are no worse off if we do not eat, and no better off if we do.

**9** But take care that this right of yours does not somehow become a stumbling block to the weak.

**10** For if someone sees you, who have knowledge, reclining at table in an idol's temple, will his conscience, if he is weak, not be built up to eat food offered to idols?

**11** And so by your knowledge this weak person is destroyed, the brother for whose sake Christ died.

**12** In sinning against your brothers in this way and wounding their weak conscience, you sin against Christ.

**13** Therefore, if food makes my brother stumble, I will never eat meat again, so that I do not make my brother stumble.

### Chapter 9

**1** Am I not free? Am I not an apostle? Have I not seen Jesus our Lord? Are you not my work in the Lord?

**2** If to others I am not an apostle, at least I am to you, for you are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord.

**3** This is my defence to those who examine me.

**4** Do we not have the right to eat and drink?

**5** Do we not have the right to take along a believing wife, as do the other apostles and the brothers of the Lord and Cephas?

**6** Or is it only Barnabas and I who have no right to stop working for a living?

**7** Who serves as a soldier at his own expense? Who plants a vineyard and does not eat its fruit? Or who tends a flock and does not drink the milk of the flock?

**8** Do I say this on human authority? Does not the law \[G-NOMOS\] say the same?

**9** For it is written in the law of Moses, "You shall not muzzle an ox when it treads out the grain." Is it for oxen that God is concerned?

**10** Does he not speak entirely for our sake? It was written for our sake, because the ploughman should plough in hope and the thresher thresh in hope of a share.

**11** If we have sown spiritual things among you, is it too much if we reap material things from you?

**12** If others share this right over you, do not we still more? Nevertheless, we have not made use of this right, but we endure anything rather than put an obstacle in the way of the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of Christ.

**13** Do you not know that those who are employed in the temple service get their food from the temple, and those who serve at the altar share in the sacrifices of the altar?

**14** In the same way, the Lord commanded that those who proclaim the good news should get their living by the good news.

**15** But I have made no use of any of these rights, nor am I writing this so that it may be done in my case. For I would rather die than have anyone deprive me of my ground for boasting.

**16** For if I proclaim the good news \[G-EUANGELION\], I have nothing to boast about, for an obligation is laid on me. Woe to me if I do not proclaim the good news.

**17** For if I do this willingly, I have a reward, but if not of my own will, I am still entrusted with a stewardship.

**18** What then is my reward? That in my proclaiming I may offer the good news free of charge, not making full use of my right in the good news.

**19** For though I am free from all, I have made myself a slave to all, so that I might win more of them.

**20** To the Jews I became as a Jew, so that I might win Jews. To those under the law \[G-NOMOS\] I became as one under the law (though not being myself under the law) so that I might win those under the law.

**21** To those outside the law I became as one outside the law (not being outside the law of God but under the law of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\]) so that I might win those outside the law.

**22** To the weak I became weak, so that I might win the weak. I have become all things to all people, so that by all means I might save \[G-SOZO\] some.

**23** I do it all for the sake of the good news, so that I may share in its blessings.

**24** Do you not know that in a race all the runners run, but only one receives the prize? Run in such a way that you may win.

**25** Everyone who competes exercises self-control in all things. They do it to receive a perishable wreath, but we an imperishable one.

**26** So I do not run aimlessly. I do not box as one beating the air.

**27** But I discipline my body and keep it under control, so that after proclaiming to others I myself will not be disqualified.

### Chapter 10

**1** For I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that our fathers were all under the cloud, and all passed through the sea,

**2** and all were baptised into Moses in the cloud and in the sea,

**3** and all ate the same spiritual food,

**4** and all drank the same spiritual drink. For they drank from a spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\].

**9** We must not put Christ to the test, as some of them did, and were destroyed by serpents,

**12** So let the one who thinks that he stands take care that he does not fall.

**14** Therefore, my loved ones \[G-AGAPE\], flee from idolatry.

**18** Consider the people of Israel: are not those who eat the sacrifices partners in the altar?

**19** What am I saying then? That food offered to idols is anything, or that an idol is anything?

**22** Or are we to provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than he?

**23** "All things are permitted," but not all things are helpful. "All things are permitted," but not all things build up.

**24** Let no one seek his own good, but the good of the other.

**25** Eat whatever is sold in the meat market without raising any question on the ground of conscience.

**26** For "the earth is the Lord's, and everything in it."

**27** If one of the unbelievers invites you to a meal and you want to go, eat whatever is set before you without raising any question on the ground of conscience.

**28** But if someone says to you, "This has been offered in sacrifice," do not eat it, for the sake of the one who informed you, and for the sake of conscience.

**29** I mean the other person's conscience, not your own. For why should my freedom be judged by another person's conscience?

**30** If I partake with thankfulness, why am I denounced because of that for which I give thanks?

**31** So, whether you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do all to the glory \[G-DOXA\] of God.

**32** Give no offence to Jews or to Greeks or to the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] of God,

**33** just as I try to please everyone in everything I do, not seeking my own advantage, but that of many, that they may be saved \[G-SOZO\].

### Chapter 11

**1** Be imitators of me, as I am of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\].

**2** I praise you because you remember me in everything and hold firm the traditions just as I handed them on to you.

**3** But I want you to know that the head of every man is Christ, the head of a woman is the man, and the head of Christ is God.

**4** Every man who prays or prophesies with his head covered dishonours his head,

**6** For if a woman does not cover her head, she might as well have her hair cut off. But since it is shameful for a woman to have her hair cut off or her head shaved, let her cover her head.

**7** For a man ought not to cover his head, since he is the image and glory \[G-DOXA\] of God, but the woman is the glory of man.

**8** For man was not made from woman, but woman from man.

**9** Neither was man created for the sake of woman, but woman for the sake of man.

**10** That is why a woman ought to have authority on her head, because of the angels.

**11** Nevertheless, in the Lord woman is not independent of man nor man of woman,

**12** for as woman was made from man, so man is now born through woman. And all things are from God.

**13** Judge for yourselves: is it proper for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered?

**14** Does not nature itself teach you that if a man wears long hair it is a disgrace for him,

**15** but if a woman has long hair, it is her glory \[G-DOXA\]? For her hair is given to her for a covering.

**16** If anyone is inclined to be contentious, we have no such practice, nor do the assemblies \[G-EKKLESIA\] of God.

**17** But in giving this instruction I do not praise you, because when you come together it is not for the better but for the worse.

**18** For, in the first place, when you come together as an assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\], I hear that there are divisions among you, and I believe it in part,

**19** for there must be factions among you in order that those who are genuine among you may be recognised.

**20** When you come together, it is not the Lord's supper that you eat.

**21** For in eating, each one goes ahead with his own meal, and one goes hungry and another gets drunk.

**22** Do you not have houses to eat and drink in? Or do you despise the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] of God and humiliate those who have nothing? What shall I say to you? Shall I praise you in this? No, I will not.

**23** For I received from the Lord what I also handed on to you: that the Lord Jesus on the night when he was handed over took bread,

**24** and when he had given thanks, he broke it and said, "This is my body, which is for you. Do this in remembrance of me."

**25** In the same way also the cup, after supper, saying, "This cup is the new covenant \[G-DIATHEKE\] in my blood. Do this, as often as you drink it, in remembrance of me."

**26** For as often as you eat this bread and drink the cup, you proclaim the Lord's death until he comes.

**27** Whoever, therefore, eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an unworthy manner will be guilty concerning the body and blood of the Lord.

**28** Let a person examine himself, and so eat of the bread and drink of the cup.

**29** For anyone who eats and drinks without discerning the body eats and drinks judgement on himself.

**30** That is why many of you are weak and ill, and some have died.

**31** But if we judged ourselves truly, we would not be judged.

**32** But when we are judged by the Lord, we are disciplined so that we may not be condemned along with the world.

**33** So then, my brothers, when you come together to eat, wait for one another.

**34** If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, so that when you come together it will not be for judgement. About the other things I will give directions when I come.

## Part Four: Chapters 12 to 14, the gifts, the body, love, and tongues and prophecy

### Chapter 12

**1** Now concerning spiritual matters, brothers, I do not want you to be uninformed.

**2** You know that when you were of the nations, you were led astray to mute idols, however you were led.

**3** So I make known to you that no one speaking by the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of God says, "Jesus is cursed," and no one can say, "Jesus is Lord," except by the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

**4** Now there are varieties of gifts \[G-CHARIS\], but the same Spirit,

**5** and there are varieties of service, but the same Lord,

**6** and there are varieties of working, but the same God who works all things in all people.

**7** To each is given the showing of the Spirit for the common good.

**8** To one is given through the Spirit a message of wisdom, and to another a message of knowledge according to the same Spirit,

**9** to another faith \[G-PISTIS\] by the same Spirit, to another gifts \[G-CHARIS\] of healing by the one Spirit,

**10** to another the working of powers, to another prophecy, to another the discernment of spirits, to another various kinds of tongues, to another the interpretation of tongues.

**11** All these are worked by one and the same Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], who distributes to each one individually as he wills.

**12** For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, though many, are one body, so it is with Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\].

**13** For in one Spirit we were all baptised into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, slaves or free, and we were all given one Spirit to drink.

**14** For the body is not one member but many.

**15** If the foot says, "Because I am not a hand, I do not belong to the body," that does not make it any less a part of the body.

**16** And if the ear says, "Because I am not an eye, I do not belong to the body," that does not make it any less a part of the body.

**17** If the whole body were an eye, where would the hearing be? If the whole were hearing, where would the sense of smell be?

**18** But as it is, God arranged the members in the body, each one of them, as he chose.

**19** If all were a single member, where would the body be?

**20** As it is, there are many members, yet one body.

**21** The eye cannot say to the hand, "I have no need of you," nor again the head to the feet, "I have no need of you."

**22** On the contrary, the members of the body that seem to be weaker are indispensable,

**23** and those parts of the body that we think less honourable we treat with greater honour, and our unpresentable parts are treated with greater modesty,

**24** which our presentable parts do not need. But God has put the body together, giving greater honour to the part that lacked it,

**25** so that there may be no division in the body, but that the members may have the same care for one another.

**26** If one member suffers, all suffer together. If one member is honoured, all rejoice together.

**27** Now you are the body of Christ and individually members of it.

**28** And God has placed in the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then works of power, then gifts \[G-CHARIS\] of healing, helps, administrations, various kinds of tongues.

**29** Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Do all work powers?

**30** Do all have gifts \[G-CHARIS\] of healing? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret?

**31** But earnestly desire the greater gifts \[G-CHARIS\]. And I will show you a still more excellent way.

### Chapter 13

**1** If I speak in the tongues of people and of angels, but have not love \[G-AGAPE\], I am a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal.

**2** And if I have prophetic powers and understand all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith \[G-PISTIS\], so as to move mountains, but have not love, I am nothing.

**3** If I give away all that I have, and if I hand over my body that I may boast, but have not love, I gain nothing.

**4** Love \[G-AGAPE\] is patient and kind. Love does not envy or boast. It is not arrogant

**5** or rude. It does not insist on its own way. It is not irritable or resentful.

**6** It does not rejoice at wrongdoing, but rejoices with the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\].

**7** Love bears all things, believes \[G-PISTIS\] all things, hopes all things, endures all things.

**8** Love never ends. As for prophecies, they will pass away. As for tongues, they will cease. As for knowledge, it will pass away.

**9** For we know in part and we prophesy in part,

**10** but when the complete comes, the partial will pass away.

**11** When I was a child, I spoke like a child, I thought like a child, I reasoned like a child. When I became an adult, I put away childish things.

**12** For now we see in a mirror dimly, but then face to face. Now I know in part, then I will know fully, even as I have been fully known.

**13** So now faith \[G-PISTIS\], hope and love \[G-AGAPE\] remain, these three. But the greatest of these is love.

### Chapter 14

**1** Pursue love \[G-AGAPE\], and earnestly desire the spiritual gifts, especially that you may prophesy.

**2** For the one who speaks in a tongue speaks not to people but to God, for no one understands him, but he speaks mysteries in the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

**3** But the one who prophesies speaks to people for their upbuilding and encouragement and consolation.

**4** The one who speaks in a tongue builds himself up, but the one who prophesies builds up the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\].

**5** Now I want all of you to speak in tongues, but even more to prophesy. The one who prophesies is greater than the one who speaks in tongues, unless someone interprets, so that the assembly may be built up.

**6** Now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in tongues, how will I benefit you unless I bring you some revelation or knowledge or prophecy or teaching?

**7** If even lifeless instruments, such as the flute or the harp, do not give distinct notes, how will anyone know what is played?

**8** And if the trumpet gives an indistinct sound, who will get ready for battle?

**9** So with yourselves: if with your tongue you utter speech that is not intelligible, how will anyone know what is said? For you will be speaking into the air.

**10** There are doubtless many different kinds of languages in the world, and none is without meaning,

**11** but if I do not know the meaning of the language, I will be a foreigner to the speaker and the speaker a foreigner to me.

**12** So with yourselves, since you are eager for manifestations of the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], strive to excel in building up the assembly.

**13** Therefore, one who speaks in a tongue should pray for the power to interpret.

**14** For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays but my mind is unfruitful.

**15** What am I to do? I will pray with my spirit, but I will pray with my mind also. I will sing with my spirit, but I will sing with my mind also.

**16** Otherwise, if you give thanks with your spirit, how can anyone in the position of an outsider say "Amen" to your thanksgiving when he does not know what you are saying?

**17** For you may be giving thanks well enough, but the other person is not built up.

**18** I thank God that I speak in tongues more than all of you.

**19** Nevertheless, in the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] I would rather speak five words with my mind in order to instruct others, than ten thousand words in a tongue.

**20** Brothers, do not be children in your thinking. Be infants in evil, but in your thinking be mature.

**21** In the Law \[G-NOMOS\] it is written: "By people of strange tongues and by the lips of foreigners will I speak to this people, and even then they will not listen to me, says the Lord."

**22** Thus tongues are a sign not for believers \[G-PISTIS\] but for unbelievers, while prophecy is a sign not for unbelievers but for believers.

**23** If, therefore, the whole assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] comes together and all speak in tongues, and outsiders or unbelievers enter, will they not say that you are out of your minds?

**24** But if all prophesy, and an unbeliever or outsider enters, he is convicted by all, he is called to account by all,

**25** the secrets of his heart are disclosed, and so, falling on his face, he will worship God and declare that God is really among you.

**26** What then, brothers? When you come together, each one has a hymn, a lesson, a revelation, a tongue or an interpretation. Let all things be done for building up.

**27** If any speak in a tongue, let there be only two or at most three, and each in turn, and let someone interpret.

**28** But if there is no one to interpret, let each of them keep silent in the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] and speak to himself and to God.

**29** Let two or three prophets speak, and let the others weigh what is said.

**30** If a revelation is made to another sitting there, let the first be silent.

**31** For you can all prophesy one by one, so that all may learn and all be encouraged,

**32** and the spirits \[G-PNEUMA\] of prophets are subject to prophets.

**33** For God is not a God of confusion but of peace \[G-EIRENE\]. As in all the assemblies \[G-EKKLESIA\] of the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\],

**34** the women should keep silent in the assemblies \[G-EKKLESIA\]. For they are not permitted to speak, but should be in submission, as the law \[G-NOMOS\] also says.

**35** If there is anything they desire to learn, let them ask their husbands at home, for it is shameful for a woman to speak in the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\].

**36** Or was it from you that the word of God came? Or are you the only people it has reached?

**37** If anyone thinks that he is a prophet, or spiritual, he should acknowledge that what I am writing to you is a command of the Lord.

**38** If anyone does not recognise this, he is not recognised.

**39** So, my brothers, earnestly desire to prophesy, and do not forbid speaking in tongues.

**40** But all things should be done decently and in order.

## Part Five: Chapters 15 and 16, the resurrection, the collection and the greetings

### Chapter 15

**1** Now I make known to you, brothers, the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] that I announced to you, which you also received, in which you also stand,

**2** and through which you are being saved \[G-SOZO\], if you hold firmly to the message I announced to you, unless you believed \[G-PISTIS\] for nothing.

**3** For I handed on to you as of first importance what I also received: that Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] died for our sins in accordance with the scriptures,

**4** and that he was buried, and that he was raised on the third day in accordance with the scriptures,

**5** and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the Twelve.

**6** Then he appeared to more than five hundred brothers at one time, most of whom are still alive, though some have fallen asleep.

**7** Then he appeared to James, then to all the apostles.

**8** Last of all, as to one born out of due time, he appeared also to me.

**9** For I am the least of the apostles, unfit to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] of God.

**10** But by the grace \[G-CHARIS\] of God I am what I am, and his grace toward me was not in vain. On the contrary, I worked harder than any of them, though it was not I, but the grace of God that is with me.

**11** So whether it was I or they, this is what we proclaim, and this is what you believed \[G-PISTIS\].

**12** Now if Christ is proclaimed as raised from the dead, how can some of you say that there is no resurrection of the dead?

**13** But if there is no resurrection of the dead, then Christ has not been raised.

**14** And if Christ has not been raised, then our proclamation is empty, and your faith \[G-PISTIS\] is empty too.

**15** We are even found to be misrepresenting God, because we testified of God that he raised Christ, whom he did not raise if it is true that the dead are not raised.

**16** For if the dead are not raised, then Christ has not been raised.

**17** And if Christ has not been raised, your faith \[G-PISTIS\] is futile and you are still in your sins.

**18** Then those also who have fallen asleep in Christ have perished.

**19** If in this life only we have hoped in Christ, we are of all people most to be pitied.

**20** But in fact Christ has been raised from the dead, the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.

**21** For as by a man came death, by a man has come also the resurrection of the dead.

**22** For as in Adam all die, so also in Christ all will be made alive \[G-ZOE\].

**23** But each in his own order: Christ the firstfruits, then at his coming those who belong to Christ.

**24** Then comes the end, when he hands over the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] to God the Father after destroying every ruler and every authority and power.

**25** For he must reign until he has put all his enemies under his feet.

**26** The last enemy to be destroyed is death.

**27** For "God has put all things in subjection under his feet." But when it says, "all things are put in subjection," it is plain that this excludes the one who put all things in subjection under him.

**28** When all things are subjected to him, then the Son himself will also be subjected to the one who put all things in subjection under him, so that God may be all in all.

**29** Otherwise, what do people mean by being baptised on behalf of the dead? If the dead are not raised at all, why are people baptised on their behalf?

**30** Why are we also in danger every hour?

**31** I die every day, I swear it by my pride in you, brothers, which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord.

**32** If, humanly speaking, I fought with wild beasts at Ephesus, what do I gain? If the dead are not raised, "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die."

**33** Do not be deceived: "Bad company ruins good morals."

**34** Come to your right mind, as you ought, and stop sinning. For some have no knowledge of God. I say this to your shame.

**35** But someone will ask, "How are the dead raised? With what kind of body do they come?"

**36** You fool. What you sow does not come to life unless it dies.

**37** And what you sow is not the body that is to be, but a bare kernel, perhaps of wheat or of some other grain.

**38** But God gives it a body as he has chosen, and to each kind of seed its own body.

**39** Not all flesh is the same, but there is one kind for humans, another for animals, another for birds, and another for fish.

**40** There are heavenly bodies and earthly bodies, but the glory \[G-DOXA\] of the heavenly is of one kind and the glory of the earthly is of another.

**41** There is one glory of the sun, and another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars, for star differs from star in glory.

**42** So it is with the resurrection of the dead. What is sown is perishable, what is raised is imperishable.

**43** It is sown in dishonour, it is raised in glory \[G-DOXA\]. It is sown in weakness, it is raised in power.

**44** It is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body. If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual body.

**45** Thus it is written: "The first man Adam became a living being"; the last Adam became a life-giving spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

**46** But it is not the spiritual that is first but the natural, and then the spiritual.

**47** The first man was from the earth, a man of dust. The second man is from heaven.

**48** As was the man of dust, so are those who are of the dust, and as is the man of heaven, so are those who are of heaven.

**49** Just as we have borne the image of the man of dust, let us also bear the image of the man of heaven.

**50** I tell you this, brothers: flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of God, nor does the perishable inherit the imperishable.

**51** Look, I tell you a mystery: we will not all fall asleep, but we will all be changed,

**52** in a moment, in the blink of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised imperishable, and we will be changed.

**53** For this perishable body must put on the imperishable, and this mortal body must put on immortality.

**54** When the perishable puts on the imperishable, and the mortal puts on immortality, then the saying that is written will come true: "Death is swallowed up in victory."

**55** "Where, O death, is your victory? Where, O death, is your sting?"

**56** The sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law \[G-NOMOS\].

**57** But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.

**58** So then, my loved \[G-AGAPE\] brothers, be steadfast, immovable, always excelling in the work of the Lord, knowing that in the Lord your labour is not in vain.

- **16:2** "On the first day of every week" translates *kata mian sabbatou*.
- **16:3** "Your gift" translates *ten charin humon*. See entry G-CHARIS.
- **16:8** "Pentecost" is as in Acts 2:1.
- **16:9** "A wide door for effective work" translates *thura moi aneoigen megale kai energes*.
- **16:11** "Peace" translates *eirene*. See entry G-EIRENE.
- **16:13** "Stand firm in the faith" translates *stekete en te pistei*, and "be brave" translates *andrizesthe*, literally "act like men". See entry G-PISTIS.
- **16:15** "The first converts" translates *aparche*, literally "the firstfruits".
- **16:17** "Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus" are named only here.
- **16:18** "Spirit" translates *pneuma*. See entry G-PNEUMA.
- **16:19** "Prisca" is the Priscilla of Acts 18:2.
- **16:20** "A holy kiss" is rendered as written.
- **16:21** "With my own hand" is the Greek of the text.
- **16:22** "Let him be accursed" translates *anathema esto*, and "Our Lord, come" translates *marana tha*, an Aramaic phrase that may also be read "our Lord has come" (*maran atha*). The WABV text does not decide. See entry G-AGAPE.
- **16:23** "The grace of the Lord Jesus" is the reading of the critical text, and some manuscripts add "Christ".
- **16:24** "Amen" is omitted by some manuscripts.
