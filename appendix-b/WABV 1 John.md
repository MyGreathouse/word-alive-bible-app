# WABV: 1 John

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-AGAPE\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. First John is the twenty-third book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the fourth of the seven general letters. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It names no writer and no readers and has no greeting or farewell, and it is written in a simple Greek, in short sentences that repeat and turn on a few words: light and darkness, truth and lie, life and death, love and hate, to abide, to be born of God, to know. It was written to believers who had been troubled by those who left them (2:18-19) and who denied that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh (4:2-3), and it gives tests of true belief: to walk in the light, to keep the commandments, to love the brothers, and to confess the Son. The writer is not named, and the tradition names John, and the WABV text does not decide. It is related in language to the Gospel of John, and to 2 and 3 John, and the footnote records the matter. The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. The WABV text of 1 John is drafted with the voice standard (Part 46 of the review package). Where the text of a critical edition is not certain, the draft says so in the footnote and asks the team to confirm the reading. It makes a number of fixed choices that follow from its Greek, and they are written down here so that they are used throughout. *Meno* is rendered "abide" and "remain", and the footnote records the choice, and the word is used about twenty-four times. *Gennao ek tou theou* is rendered "be born of God". *Koinonia* (1:3, 1:6-7) is rendered "fellowship". *Hilasmos* (2:2, 4:10) is rendered "the atonement", and has no marker, and is the same family as *hilasterion* at Romans 3:25 and Hebrews 9:5. *Parakletos* (2:1) is rendered "advocate" and is tagged G-PARAKLETOS. *Chrisma* (2:20, 2:27) is rendered "anointing". *Antichristos* (2:18, 2:22, 4:3) is rendered "antichrist". *Agape* is rendered "love" and is tagged G-AGAPE. *Aletheia* is rendered "truth" and is tagged G-ALETHEIA. *Zoe* is rendered "life" and is tagged G-ZOE. *Pistis* and *pisteuo* are rendered "faith" and "believe" and are tagged G-PISTIS. *Dikaiosune* and *dikaios* are rendered "righteousness" and "righteous" and are tagged G-DIKAIOSUNE. *Hagios* is rendered "holy" and is tagged G-HAGIOS. *Pneuma* is rendered "Spirit" or "spirit" and is tagged G-PNEUMA. *Anomia* (3:4) is rendered "lawlessness". *Logos* (1:1, 2:7, 2:14) is rendered "word", and is tagged G-LOGOS at 1:1 only, where it is the word of life, and the footnote records the matter. *Teknia* is rendered "little children" and *tekna* "children". *Adelphoi* is rendered "brothers", and the footnote records that in many places it may include the sisters, and the team should decide. *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Chapters 1 to 5, light, love and the Son of God

### Chapter 1

**1** That which was from the beginning, which we have heard, which we have seen with our eyes, which we looked upon and have touched with our hands, concerning the word \[G-LOGOS\] of life \[G-ZOE\],

**2** the life was made manifest, and we have seen it, and testify to it and proclaim to you the eternal life, which was with the Father and was made manifest to us,

**3** that which we have seen and heard we proclaim also to you, so that you also may have fellowship with us. And our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\].

**4** And we are writing these things so that our joy may be complete.

**5** This is the message we have heard from him and proclaim to you, that God is light, and in him is no darkness at all.

**6** If we say we have fellowship with him while we walk in darkness, we lie and do not practise the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\].

**7** But if we walk in the light, as he is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us from all sin.

**8** If we say we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] is not in us.

**9** If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

**10** If we say we have not sinned, we make him a liar, and his word is not in us.

### Chapter 2

**1** My little children, I am writing these things to you so that you may not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have an advocate \[G-PARAKLETOS\] with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous.

**2** He is the atonement for our sins, and not for ours only but also for the sins of the whole world.

**3** And by this we know that we have come to know him, if we keep his commandments.

**4** Whoever says "I know him" but does not keep his commandments is a liar, and the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] is not in him,

**5** but whoever keeps his word, in him truly the love \[G-AGAPE\] of God is perfected. By this we may know that we are in him:

**6** whoever says he abides in him ought to walk in the same way in which he walked.

**7** Beloved, I am writing you no new commandment, but an old commandment that you had from the beginning. The old commandment is the word that you have heard.

**8** At the same time, it is a new commandment that I am writing to you, which is true in him and in you, because the darkness is passing away and the true light is already shining.

**9** Whoever says he is in the light and hates his brother is still in darkness.

**10** Whoever loves \[G-AGAPE\] his brother abides in the light, and in him there is no cause for stumbling.

**11** But whoever hates his brother is in the darkness and walks in the darkness, and does not know where he is going, because the darkness has blinded his eyes.

**12** I am writing to you, little children, because your sins are forgiven for his name's sake.

**13** I am writing to you, fathers, because you know him who is from the beginning. I am writing to you, young men, because you have overcome the evil one. I write to you, children, because you know the Father.

**14** I write to you, fathers, because you know him who is from the beginning. I write to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God abides in you, and you have overcome the evil one.

**15** Do not love \[G-AGAPE\] the world or the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him.

**16** For all that is in the world, the desires of the flesh and the desires of the eyes and the pride of life, is not from the Father but is from the world.

**17** And the world is passing away along with its desires, but whoever does the will of God abides for ever.

**18** Children, it is the last hour, and as you have heard that antichrist is coming, so now many antichrists have come. Therefore we know that it is the last hour.

**19** They went out from us, but they were not of us. For if they had been of us, they would have remained with us. But they went out, so that it might become plain that they all are not of us.

**20** But you have been anointed by the Holy \[G-HAGIOS\] One, and you all have knowledge.

**21** I write to you, not because you do not know the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\], but because you know it, and because no lie is of the truth.

**22** Who is the liar but the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\]? This is the antichrist, he who denies the Father and the Son.

**23** No one who denies the Son has the Father. Whoever confesses the Son has the Father also.

**24** Let what you heard from the beginning abide in you. If what you heard from the beginning abides in you, then you too will abide in the Son and in the Father.

**25** And this is the promise that he made to us: eternal life \[G-ZOE\].

**26** I write these things to you about those who are trying to deceive you.

**27** But the anointing that you received from him abides in you, and you have no need that anyone should teach you. But as his anointing teaches you about everything, and is true, and is no lie, just as it has taught you, abide in him.

**28** And now, little children, abide in him, so that when he appears we may have confidence and not shrink from him in shame at his coming.

**29** If you know that he is righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\], you may be sure that everyone who practises righteousness has been born of him.

### Chapter 3

**1** Look what kind of love \[G-AGAPE\] the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God. And so we are. The reason why the world does not know us is that it did not know him.

**2** Beloved, we are God's children now, and what we will be has not yet appeared. We know that when he appears we shall be like him, because we shall see him as he is.

**3** And everyone who has this hope in him purifies himself as he is pure.

**4** Everyone who makes a practice of sinning also practises lawlessness. Sin is lawlessness.

**5** You know that he appeared in order to take away sins, and in him there is no sin.

**6** No one who abides in him keeps on sinning. No one who keeps on sinning has either seen him or known him.

**7** Little children, let no one deceive you. Whoever practises righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] is righteous, as he is righteous.

**8** Whoever makes a practice of sinning is of the devil, for the devil has been sinning from the beginning. The reason the Son of God appeared was to destroy the works of the devil.

**9** No one born of God makes a practice of sinning, for God's seed abides in him, and he cannot keep on sinning because he has been born of God.

**10** By this it is evident who are the children of God, and who are the children of the devil: whoever does not practise righteousness is not of God, nor is the one who does not love \[G-AGAPE\] his brother.

**11** For this is the message that you have heard from the beginning, that we should love one another,

**12** and not be like Cain, who was of the evil one and murdered his brother. And why did he murder him? Because his own deeds were evil and his brother's righteous.

**13** Do not be surprised, brothers, that the world hates you.

**14** We know that we have passed out of death into life \[G-ZOE\], because we love the brothers. Whoever does not love abides in death.

**15** Everyone who hates his brother is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life \[G-ZOE\] abiding in him.

**16** By this we know love \[G-AGAPE\], that he laid down his life for us, and we ought to lay down our lives for the brothers.

**17** But if anyone has the world's goods and sees his brother in need, yet closes his heart against him, how does God's love \[G-AGAPE\] abide in him?

**18** Little children, let us not love in word or talk but in deed and in truth \[G-ALETHEIA\].

**19** By this we shall know that we are of the truth and reassure our heart before him,

**20** for whenever our heart condemns us, God is greater than our heart, and he knows everything.

**21** Beloved, if our heart does not condemn us, we have confidence before God,

**22** and whatever we ask we receive from him, because we keep his commandments and do what pleases him.

**23** And this is his commandment, that we believe \[G-PISTIS\] in the name of his Son Jesus Christ and love one another, just as he has commanded us.

**24** Whoever keeps his commandments abides in God, and God in him. And by this we know that he abides in us, by the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] whom he has given us.

### Chapter 4

**1** Beloved, do not believe \[G-PISTIS\] every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God, for many false prophets have gone out into the world.

**2** By this you know the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of God: every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is from God,

**3** and every spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God. This is the spirit of the antichrist, which you have heard is coming and now is in the world already.

**4** Little children, you are from God and have overcome them, for he who is in you is greater than he who is in the world.

**5** They are from the world, therefore they speak from the world, and the world listens to them.

**6** We are from God. Whoever knows God listens to us. Whoever is not from God does not listen to us. By this we know the Spirit of truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] and the spirit of error.

**7** Beloved, let us love \[G-AGAPE\] one another, for love is from God, and whoever loves has been born of God and knows God.

**8** Anyone who does not love does not know God, because God is love.

**9** In this the love \[G-AGAPE\] of God was made manifest among us, that God sent his only Son into the world, so that we might live through him.

**10** In this is love, not that we have loved God but that he loved us and sent his Son to be the atonement for our sins.

**11** Beloved, if God so loved us, we also ought to love one another.

**12** No one has ever seen God. If we love one another, God abides in us and his love is perfected in us.

**13** By this we know that we abide in him and he in us, because he has given us of his Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

**14** And we have seen and testify that the Father has sent his Son as the Saviour of the world.

**15** Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God abides in him, and he in God.

**16** So we have come to know and to believe \[G-PISTIS\] the love \[G-AGAPE\] that God has for us. God is love, and whoever abides in love abides in God, and God abides in him.

**17** By this is love perfected with us, so that we may have confidence for the day of judgement, because as he is so also are we in this world.

**18** There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear. For fear has to do with punishment, and whoever fears has not been perfected in love.

**19** We love because he first loved us.

**20** If anyone says, "I love God," and hates his brother, he is a liar. For he who does not love his brother whom he has seen cannot love God whom he has not seen.

**21** And this commandment we have from him: whoever loves God must also love his brother.

### Chapter 5

**1** Everyone who believes \[G-PISTIS\] that Jesus is the Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] has been born of God, and everyone who loves the Father loves whoever has been born of him.

**2** By this we know that we love the children of God, when we love God and obey his commandments.

**3** For this is the love \[G-AGAPE\] of God, that we keep his commandments. And his commandments are not burdensome.

**4** For everyone who has been born of God overcomes the world. And this is the victory that has overcome the world: our faith \[G-PISTIS\].

**5** Who is it that overcomes the world except the one who believes that Jesus is the Son of God?

**6** This is he who came by water and blood, Jesus Christ. Not by the water only but by the water and the blood. And the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] is the one who testifies, because the Spirit is the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\].

**7** For there are three that testify:

**8** the Spirit and the water and the blood, and these three agree.

**9** If we receive the testimony of people, the testimony of God is greater, for this is the testimony of God that he has borne concerning his Son.

**10** Whoever believes \[G-PISTIS\] in the Son of God has the testimony in himself. Whoever does not believe God has made him a liar, because he has not believed in the testimony that God has borne concerning his Son.

**11** And this is the testimony, that God gave us eternal life \[G-ZOE\], and this life is in his Son.

**12** Whoever has the Son has life. Whoever does not have the Son of God does not have life.

**13** I write these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God that you may know that you have eternal life.

**14** And this is the confidence that we have toward him, that if we ask anything according to his will he hears us.

**15** And if we know that he hears us in whatever we ask, we know that we have the requests that we have asked of him.

**16** If anyone sees his brother committing a sin not leading to death, he shall ask, and God will give him life, to those who commit sins not leading to death. There is sin that leads to death. I do not say that one should pray for that.

**17** All wrongdoing is sin, but there is sin that does not lead to death.

**18** We know that everyone who has been born of God does not keep on sinning, but he who was born of God protects him, and the evil one does not touch him.

**19** We know that we are from God, and the whole world lies in the power of the evil one.

**20** And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us understanding, so that we may know him who is true. And we are in him who is true, in his Son Jesus Christ. He is the true God and eternal life \[G-ZOE\].

**21** Little children, keep yourselves from idols.

*Review note: 1 John 1 to 5 were drafted in WABV wording, from the Greek, using the fixed choices set out in the introduction to this book, and with the WABV voice standard (Part 46 of the review package) applied. They are the word of life that was heard and seen and touched (1:1-4), God is light and the walk in the light (1:5-10), the advocate and the atonement (2:1-2), keeping the commandments and the new commandment (2:3-11), the children, fathers and young men (2:12-14), the world (2:15-17), the antichrists (2:18-23), abiding in what was heard and the anointing (2:24-29), children of God and sin (3:1-10), the love of the brothers (3:11-18), confidence before God (3:19-24), testing the spirits (4:1-6), God is love (4:7-21), faith that overcomes the world (5:1-5), the testimony of the Spirit, the water and the blood (5:6-12), confidence in prayer and the sin that leads to death (5:13-17) and the closing certainties (5:18-21). The numbering is the usual numbering. No new G- marker is used, and the markers are G-LOGOS (1:1), G-ZOE (1:1-5:20), G-CHRISTOS (1:3, 2:22, 5:1), G-ALETHEIA (1:6-5:6), G-DIKAIOSUNE (1:9, 2:29, 3:7), G-PARAKLETOS (2:1), G-AGAPE (2:5-5:3), G-HAGIOS (2:20), G-PISTIS (3:23-5:10), G-PNEUMA (3:24-5:6). The verse 5:7 is the critical text, and the longer form of 5:7-8 of the later manuscripts (the "Johannine comma") is not in the critical text and is held as a note, and the footnote records the matter in full. Several readings are drafted with some uncertainty and are to be confirmed against the critical edition in use: 2:14 (the form of "I write"), 2:20 ("you all have knowledge" or "you know everything"), 2:23 (the end of the verse), 3:1 ("and so we are"), 4:3 ("does not confess Jesus" or "dissolves Jesus"), 4:19 ("him") and 5:18 ("he who was born of God" or "the birth of God"). The main points are these, and the footnotes carry the Greek: *meno* is rendered "abide", and the footnote records that "remain" is the common alternative; *hilasmos* (2:2, 4:10) is rendered "the atonement" and has no marker; 2:1 "advocate" translates *parakleton* and is tagged G-PARAKLETOS; 3:4 "lawlessness" translates *anomia*, and "sin is lawlessness" is rendered as written; 3:9 "cannot keep on sinning" is rendered as written, with no comment, and the footnote records that the present tense is debated; 5:6 "by water and blood" is rendered as written, and the footnote records that it may refer to the baptism and the death of Jesus, or to his birth and death, and the WABV text does not decide; 5:16 "a sin not leading to death" is rendered as written, and the WABV text does not decide what it is; 5:20 "He is the true God and eternal life" is rendered as written, and the footnote records that "he" may refer to the Son or to God. There are no departures from the critical Greek text in chapters 1 to 5. The Greek is hard in some places, including 1:1-2, 2:20-27, 3:4-9, 3:19-20, 5:6-8 and 5:16-18. The words on the word of life (1:1-4), God is light (1:5), the advocate and the atonement (2:1-2), the anointing (2:20-27), children of God (3:1-2), "sin is lawlessness" (3:4), "no one born of God makes a practice of sinning" (3:9), "God is love" (4:8, 4:16), "perfect love casts out fear" (4:18), "we love because he first loved us" (4:19), the testimony of the Spirit, the water and the blood (5:6-8), the sin that leads to death (5:16) and "keep yourselves from idols" (5:21) are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. The section needs a check against the Greek by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked. With this the book of 1 John is complete in first draft.*

## Appendix A: Footnotes

- **1:1** "The word of life" translates *peri tou logou tes zoes*, and "word" is tagged G-LOGOS here, since it is the word of life, and may mean the message or the person of the Son, and the opening is close in language to John 1:1-4. See entry G-ZOE.
- **1:3** "Fellowship" translates *koinonian*, which may also be read "sharing" or "partnership", and it is used also in verses 6 and 7. See entry G-CHRISTOS.
- **1:5** "God is light, and in him is no darkness at all" is rendered as written.
- **1:6** "Practise the truth" is rendered as written. See entry G-ALETHEIA.
- **1:7** "The blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us from all sin" is rendered as written.
- **1:9** "He is faithful and righteous to forgive us our sins" is rendered as written. See entry G-DIKAIOSUNE.
- **2:1** "Advocate" translates *parakleton*, and is tagged G-PARAKLETOS, and the same word is used of the Spirit in the Gospel of John (14:16, 14:26, 15:26, 16:7). "Jesus Christ the righteous" is rendered as written.
- **2:2** "The atonement" translates *hilasmos*, and has no marker, and is of the same family as *hilasterion* (Romans 3:25, Hebrews 9:5), and may be read "the expiation" or "the propitiation", and the same word is used at 4:10.
- **2:5** "The love of God is perfected" may be read of the love that God has or the love that we have for God. See entry G-AGAPE.
- **2:6** "Abides" translates *menein*, and is rendered "abide" throughout, and "remain" is the common alternative, and the word is used about twenty-four times in the letter.
- **2:7** "An old commandment ... the word that you have heard" is rendered as written.
- **2:8** "A new commandment" is as in John 13:34.
- **2:12** The addresses to the little children, fathers and young men in verses 12 to 14 are rendered as written, and the Greek has a change from "I am writing" (*grapho*) to "I wrote" (*egrapsa*), and some manuscripts read the present in verse 14, and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **2:15** "Do not love the world" is rendered as written.
- **2:16** "The desires of the flesh and the desires of the eyes and the pride of life" is rendered as written, and "life" here translates *bios*, and it is not tagged G-ZOE, because the Greek word is not *zoe*.
- **2:18** "The last hour" and "antichrist" are rendered as written, and "antichrist" translates *antichristos*, and it is used only in this letter and in 2 John 7.
- **2:19** "They went out from us, but they were not of us" is rendered as written.
- **2:20** "You have been anointed by the Holy One, and you all have knowledge" is the reading that the draft follows (*kai oidate panta*, "and you know all things" in some manuscripts), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use. "Anointed" translates *chrisma*, and "the Holy One" may mean the Father or Christ. See entry G-HAGIOS.
- **2:22** "Who is the liar but the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ?" is rendered as written. See entry G-CHRISTOS.
- **2:23** "Whoever confesses the Son has the Father also" is rendered as written, and some manuscripts omit the second half of the verse, and the critical text includes it.
- **2:27** "The anointing that you received from him abides in you" is rendered as written.
- **2:28** "At his coming" translates *en te parousia autou*, and is rendered "coming", as in the Thessalonian letters.
- **2:29** "Everyone who practises righteousness has been born of him" is rendered as written. See entry G-DIKAIOSUNE.
- **3:1** "Look what kind of love" translates *idete potapen agapen*, and "and so we are" is the reading that the draft follows, and some manuscripts omit it, and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use. See entry G-AGAPE.
- **3:2** "We shall be like him, because we shall see him as he is" is rendered as written.
- **3:4** "Sin is lawlessness" translates *he hamartia estin he anomia*, and "lawlessness" is *anomia*.
- **3:6** "No one who abides in him keeps on sinning" and "he cannot keep on sinning" (verse 9) translate present tenses, and the matter of the present tense is debated, and the WABV text does not decide.
- **3:8** "The reason the Son of God appeared was to destroy the works of the devil" is rendered as written.
- **3:9** "God's seed abides in him" is rendered as written.
- **3:12** "Cain, who was of the evil one and murdered his brother" is rendered as written.
- **3:14** "We have passed out of death into life" is rendered as written. See entry G-ZOE.
- **3:16** "He laid down his life for us" is rendered as written.
- **3:17** "The world's goods" translates *ton bion tou kosmou*, and is not tagged G-ZOE, because the Greek word is *bios*.
- **3:19** "By this we shall know that we are of the truth" is rendered as written, and the Greek of verses 19 and 20 is hard, and may be read in more than one way. See entry G-ALETHEIA.
- **3:20** "God is greater than our heart" is rendered as written.
- **3:23** "That we believe in the name of his Son Jesus Christ and love one another" is rendered as written. See entry G-PISTIS.
- **3:24** "By the Spirit whom he has given us" is rendered as written. See entry G-PNEUMA.
- **4:1** "Test the spirits" is rendered as written.
- **4:2** "Every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is from God" is rendered as written, and the Greek may be read "confesses Jesus Christ as having come in the flesh".
- **4:3** "Every spirit that does not confess Jesus" is the reading of the critical text, and some manuscripts read "that does not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh", and a form of the verse ("dissolves Jesus", *luei*) was known to the early church, and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **4:6** "The Spirit of truth and the spirit of error" is rendered as written.
- **4:8** "God is love" is rendered as written, and the same is said at verse 16.
- **4:9** "His only Son" translates *ton huion autou ton monogene*, and is as in John 3:16.
- **4:10** "The atonement for our sins" translates *hilasmon peri ton hamartion hemon*, and is as at 2:2.
- **4:14** "The Saviour of the world" is as in John 4:42.
- **4:17** "As he is so also are we in this world" is rendered as written.
- **4:18** "There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear" is rendered as written.
- **4:19** "We love because he first loved us" is the reading that the draft follows, and some manuscripts read "we love him because he first loved us" (*auton*), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **5:1** "Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ has been born of God" is rendered as written. See entries G-PISTIS and G-CHRISTOS.
- **5:3** "His commandments are not burdensome" is rendered as written.
- **5:6** "He who came by water and blood" is rendered as written, and "water" and "blood" may refer to the baptism and the death of Jesus, or to his birth and death, and the WABV text does not decide. See entry G-PNEUMA.
- **5:7** The critical text has "For there are three that testify: the Spirit and the water and the blood, and these three agree" (verses 7 and 8). The Latin tradition and the later Greek manuscripts have a longer text (the "Johannine comma"), which adds after "three that testify" the words "in heaven, the Father, the Word and the Holy Spirit, and these three are one. And there are three that testify on earth", and it is not in the oldest Greek manuscripts, and it is not part of the WABV text, and is held here as a note.
- **5:9** "The testimony of God is greater" is rendered as written.
- **5:11** "God gave us eternal life, and this life is in his Son" is rendered as written. See entry G-ZOE.
- **5:16** "A sin not leading to death" is rendered as written, and the WABV text does not decide what it is.
- **5:18** "He who was born of God protects him" is the reading that the draft follows (*ho gennetheis ek tou theou*), and some manuscripts read "the birth of God" (*he gennesis*), and the team should confirm the reading against the critical edition in use.
- **5:19** "The whole world lies in the power of the evil one" is rendered as written.
- **5:20** "He is the true God and eternal life" is rendered as written, and "he" may refer to the Son or to God. See entry G-ZOE.
- **5:21** "Keep yourselves from idols" is rendered as written.
