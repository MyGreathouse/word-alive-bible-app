# WABV: Hosea

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[H-RUACH\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Hosea is the first of the twelve books of the Minor Prophets in the Protestant order that the WABV text follows. In the Hebrew Bible the twelve form one scroll, the Book of the Twelve, and the WABV text gives each as its own book. Hosea is in Hebrew, and mostly in poetry, with prose in chapters 1 and 3 and in parts of chapter 2. The Hebrew text of Hosea is among the hardest in the prophets, with many obscure forms and some lines that cannot be read with certainty, and the Greek differs from it in many places. The WABV text follows the Hebrew text, and the footnotes record the other readings and the uncertain terms where they affect the draft. The chapter and verse numbering is the English numbering. English 1:10-11 is Hebrew 2:1-2, so English 2:1-23 is Hebrew 2:3-25. English 11:12 is Hebrew 12:1, so English 12:1-14 is Hebrew 12:2-15. English 13:16 is Hebrew 14:1, so English 14:1-9 is Hebrew 14:2-10. The book falls into four parts in the WABV text: Hosea's marriage and the three children, and the woman bought back (chapters 1 to 3), the case of the LORD against Israel (4 to 7), the sin of the calf and the vine (8 to 10) and the love of the LORD for his child and the call to return (11 to 14). The headings of the parts are not in the Hebrew. The book is dated in 1:1 by the kings of Judah and of Israel, and the footnotes give the dates in the modern count. The WABV text does not decide on questions of whether Hosea's marriage was in a vision, in fact or in a story, and the footnotes record only the language. The names of God are given as in the earlier books: "the LORD" for YHWH, "Lord" for Adonai, and "the Lord GOD" for Adonai YHWH. The Hebrew names Ephraim and Israel, which the book uses of the northern kingdom, are given as written, and the footnotes mark the places where they mean the whole people.*

## Part One: Chapters 1 to 3, Hosea's marriage and the woman bought

### Chapter 1

**1** The word of the LORD that came to Hosea son of Beeri in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.

**2** When the LORD first spoke through Hosea, the LORD said to Hosea, "Go, take for yourself a wife of whoredom and children of whoredom, for the land commits great whoredom by turning away from the LORD."

**3** So he went and took Gomer daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.

**4** And the LORD said to him, "Call his name Jezreel, for in a little while I will punish the house of Jehu for the blood of Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of the house of Israel.

**5** On that day I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel."

**6** She conceived again and bore a daughter. And the LORD said to him, "Call her name Lo-ruhamah, Not Pitied, for I will no longer have compassion on the house of Israel, so as to forgive them.

**7** But I will have compassion on the house of Judah, and I will save them by the LORD their God. I will not save them by bow or by sword or by war, by horses or by horsemen."

**8** When she had weaned Lo-ruhamah, she conceived and bore a son.

**9** And the LORD said, "Call his name Lo-ammi, Not My People, for you are not my people, and I am not your God."

**10** Yet the number of the people of Israel will be like the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured or counted. And in the place where it was said to them, "You are not my people," it will be said to them, "Children of the living God."

**11** The people of Judah and the people of Israel will be gathered together, and they will appoint for themselves one head, and they will go up from the land, for great will be the day of Jezreel.

### Chapter 2

**1** Say to your brothers, "My people," and to your sisters, "Pitied."

**2** Plead with your mother, plead, for she is not my wife, and I am not her husband, that she put away her whoring from her face and her adultery from between her breasts,

**3** or I will strip her naked and set her as on the day she was born, and make her like a wilderness, and turn her into a parched land, and kill her with thirst.

**4** On her children also I will have no compassion, because they are children of whoredom.

**5** For their mother has played the whore. She who conceived them has acted shamefully. For she said, "I will go after my lovers, who give me my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, my oil and my drink."

**6** Therefore, see, I will hedge up her way with thorns, and I will build a wall against her, so that she cannot find her paths.

**7** She will pursue her lovers but not overtake them, and she will seek them but not find them. Then she will say, "I will go and return \[H-SHUV\] to my first husband, for it was better for me then than now."

**8** She did not know that it was I who gave her the grain, the wine and the oil, and who lavished on her silver and gold, which they used for Baal.

**9** Therefore I will take back \[H-SHUV\] my grain in its time and my wine in its season, and I will take away my wool and my flax, which were to cover her nakedness.

**10** Now I will uncover her shame in the sight of her lovers, and no one will rescue her out of my hand.

**11** And I will put an end to all her joy, her feasts, her new moons, her Sabbaths, and all her appointed festivals.

**12** And I will lay waste her vines and her fig trees, of which she said, "These are my wages, which my lovers have given me." I will make them a forest, and the wild animals will devour them.

**13** And I will punish her for the feast days of the Baals, when she burned offerings to them and adorned herself with her ring and her jewellery, and went after her lovers and forgot me," declares the LORD.

**14** "Therefore, see, I will allure her and bring her into the wilderness and speak tenderly to her.

**15** And from there I will give her her vineyards, and make the Valley of Achor a door of hope. And there she will respond as in the days of her youth, as in the day when she came up out of the land of Egypt.

**16** And in that day," declares the LORD, "you will call me "My husband," and you will no longer call me "My Baal."

**17** For I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth, and they will be remembered by name no more.

**18** And I will make for them a covenant \[H-BERIT\] on that day with the wild animals, the birds of the heavens and the creeping things of the ground. And I will break the bow, the sword and war out of the land, and I will make you lie down in safety.

**19** And I will betroth you to me for ever. I will betroth you to me in righteousness \[H-TSEDAQAH\] and in justice, in faithful love \[H-HESED\] and in mercy.

**20** I will betroth you to me in faithfulness, and you will know the LORD.

**21** And in that day I will answer," declares the LORD, "I will answer the heavens, and they will answer the earth,

**22** and the earth will answer the grain, the wine and the oil, and they will answer Jezreel.

**23** And I will sow her for myself in the land. And I will have compassion on Lo-ruhamah, and I will say to Lo-ammi, "You are my people," and he will say, "My God.""

### Chapter 3

**1** And the LORD said to me, "Go again, love \[H-AHAV\] a woman who is loved \[H-AHAV\] by another and is an adulteress, just as the LORD loves \[H-AHAV\] the people of Israel, though they turn to other gods and love \[H-AHAV\] cakes of raisins."

**2** So I bought her for fifteen shekels of silver and a homer and a lethech of barley.

**3** And I said to her, "You are to stay with me for many days. You must not play the whore or belong to another man. And I will be the same toward you."

**4** For the people of Israel will remain many days without king or prince, without sacrifice or pillar, without ephod or household gods.

**5** Afterward the people of Israel will return \[H-SHUV\] and seek the LORD their God and David their king, and they will come in awe to the LORD and to his goodness in the latter days.

## Part Three: Chapters 8 to 10, the calf of Samaria and the vine

### Chapter 8

**1** Put the trumpet to your lips. Like a vulture over the house of the LORD, because they have broken my covenant \[H-BERIT\] and rebelled against my teaching \[H-TORAH\].

**2** To me they cry, "My God, we, Israel, know you."

**3** Israel has rejected what is good. The enemy will pursue him.

**4** They made kings, but not through me. They set up princes, but without my knowledge. With their silver and gold they made idols for themselves, that they might be cut off.

**5** I have rejected your calf, Samaria. My anger burns against them. How long will they be incapable of innocence?

**6** For it is from Israel. A craftsman made it, and it is not God. The calf of Samaria will be broken to pieces.

**7** For they sow the wind \[H-RUACH\], and they reap the whirlwind. The standing grain has no head, and it will yield no flour. If it were to yield, foreigners would swallow it.

**8** Israel is swallowed up. Now they are among the nations like a vessel no one wants.

**9** For they have gone up to Assyria, a wild donkey wandering alone. Ephraim has hired lovers.

**10** Though they hire among the nations, I will now gather them. And they will soon writhe under the burden of the king of princes.

**11** Because Ephraim multiplied altars for sin, they have become altars for sinning.

**12** Though I wrote for him the many things of my teaching \[H-TORAH\], they are regarded as a strange thing.

**13** As for my sacrificial gifts, they sacrifice flesh and eat it, but the LORD does not accept them. Now he will remember their iniquity and punish their sins. They will return \[H-SHUV\] to Egypt.

**14** For Israel has forgotten his Maker and built palaces, and Judah has multiplied fortified cities. So I will send a fire on his cities, and it will devour his strongholds.

### Chapter 9

**2** Threshing floor and winepress will not feed them, and the new wine will fail them.

**14** Give them, LORD. What will you give? Give them a miscarrying womb and dry breasts.

**15** Every evil of theirs is in Gilgal. There I came to hate them. Because of the wickedness of their deeds I will drive them out of my house. I will love \[H-AHAV\] them no more. All their princes are rebels.

**16** Ephraim is struck down. Their root is dried up. They will bear no fruit. Even if they give birth, I will put their beloved children to death.

## Part Four: Chapters 11 to 14, the love of the LORD and the call to return

- **12:11** "Worthless" translates *shav*, and "stone heaps" translates *gallim*, a word-play on *Gilgal*.
- **12:12** "Aram" translates *Aram*. See Genesis 29:18-20.
- **12:13** "A prophet" translates *navi*, and refers to Moses.
- **12:14** "Bitter provocation" translates *hikh'is tamrurim*, "his bloodguilt" translates *damav*, "repay" translates *yashiv*, and "his disgrace" translates *cherpato*. See entry H-SHUV.
- **13:1** "Trembling" translates *retet*, which is rare and hard, and "died" translates *va-yamot*.
- **13:2** "A cast metal image" translates *massekhah*. "Let the people who sacrifice kiss the calves" translates *zovchei adam agalim yishaqun*, which may also be read "those who sacrifice human beings kiss calves". It is rendered as written, with no comment.
- **13:3** "Chaff" translates *motz*, and "a window" translates *arubbah*.
- **13:4** "Saviour" translates *moshia*.
- **13:5** "I knew you" translates *yeda'tikha*, in which the Greek has "I fed you", and "drought" translates *tal'ubot*.
- **13:7** "I will lurk" translates *ashur*, which is a word-play on *Ashur* (Assyria).
- **13:8** "The covering of their heart" translates *se'gor libbam*, which is hard.
- **13:9** "I will destroy you" translates *shichatkha*, and the Greek has "I have destroyed you, Israel, and who will help you?".
- **13:10** "Where now" translates *ehi*, which may also be read "I will be" ("I will be your king").
- **13:14** "Shall I ransom" translates *efdem*, from *padah*, "shall I redeem" translates *ega'alem*, from *ga'al*, and "Sheol" translates *She'ol*. See entries H-PADAH, H-GAAL and H-SHEOL. The Hebrew can be read as questions, as in this text, or as statements of promise ("I will ransom them"), and "where are your plagues?" translates *ehi devareikha*, which may also be read "I will be your plagues". The footnote does not decide, and the team should confirm the reading. See 1 Corinthians 15:55. "Compassion is hidden from my eyes" translates *nocham yissater me-einai*. See entry H-NACHAM.
- **13:15** "An east wind" translates *ruach qadim*, and "a wind of the LORD" translates *ruach YHWH*. See entry H-RUACH. "Flourishes" translates *yaphri*.
- **13:16** The verse is Hebrew 14:1. "Their pregnant women ripped open" translates *harotav yevuqqa'u*, and it is rendered as written, with no comment.
- **14:1** The verse is Hebrew 14:2. "Return" translates *shuvah*. See entry H-SHUV.
- **14:2** "Take words with you" translates *qechu imakhem devarim*, and "return" translates *shuvu*. See entry H-SHUV. "We will offer the bulls of our lips" follows the Hebrew (*u-neshallemah parim sefateinu*). The Greek and the Syriac read "the fruit of our lips" (*peri sefateinu*), and Hebrews 13:15 follows that reading.
- **14:3** "In you the orphan finds compassion" translates *asher-becha yerucham yatom*.
- **14:4** "Their turning away" translates *meshuvatam*, "I will love them freely" translates *ohavem nedavah*, and "has turned" translates *shav*. See entries H-SHUV and H-AHAV.
- **14:5** "The dew" translates *tal*, and "the lily" translates *shoshannah*.
- **14:6** "His shoots" translates *yonqotav*, and "his fragrance" translates *reicho*.
- **14:7** "They will return and live in his shade" translates *yashuvu yoshevei be-tsilo*. See entry H-SHUV. The words may also be read "those who live in his shade will return". "Their fame" translates *zikhro*.
- **14:8** "An evergreen cypress" translates *berosh ra'anan*, and "it is I who answer and look after you" translates *ani anitiv va-ashurennu*, in which the Greek has "I humbled him".
- **14:9** "The righteous" translates *tsaddiqim*. See entry H-TSEDAQAH.
