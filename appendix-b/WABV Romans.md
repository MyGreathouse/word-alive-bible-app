# WABV: Romans

*Word Alive Bible Version, draft 1. Words in square brackets, such as \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\], point to entries in the Biblical Concepts and Language System. Footnotes and centre references are in the appendices at the end. Romans is the sixth book of the New Testament in the order that the WABV text follows, and the first of the letters. It is written in Greek, and the WABV text is drafted from a critical Greek text, with the Byzantine tradition noted in the footnotes where it differs, as the Charter requires. It is a letter from Paul to the believers in Rome, written (16:1, 16:23) from Corinth, probably about AD 56 to 57, and it is the longest and most argued of his letters. Its sentences are long and its arguments are carried by a small number of weighty words, used again and again and often close together: *dikaiosune* (righteousness), *pistis* (faith), *charis* (grace), *nomos* (law), *hamartia* (sin), *sarx* (flesh), *pneuma* (Spirit), *zoe* (life) and *thanatos* (death). The chapter and verse numbering is the usual numbering. Some verses and words are not in the critical Greek text (16:24 is the only verse), and the matter of the ending, and of the doxology of 16:25-27, which stands in different places in the manuscripts, is recorded in the footnotes. The WABV text of Romans makes a number of fixed choices that follow from its Greek, and they are written down here so that they are used throughout. *Dikaiosune* is rendered "righteousness" and is tagged G-DIKAIOSUNE. The verb *dikaioo* is rendered "declare righteous" (and in the passive "be declared righteous"), because in Paul the word is a verdict, and it is tagged G-DIKAIOSUNE, and the team should decide between "declare righteous" and "justify". *Pistis* is rendered "faith" and *pisteuo* "believe", and both are tagged G-PISTIS. The phrase *pistis Christou* (3:22, 3:26, and in Galatians) is rendered "faith in Christ" in the text, and the footnote records "the faithfulness of Christ", and the team should decide. *Charis* is rendered "grace" and is tagged G-CHARIS. *Nomos* is rendered "law" and is tagged G-NOMOS, and the footnote records the places where it may mean the Law of Moses and the places where it may mean a principle. *Hamartia* is rendered "sin", and it is not tagged. *Sarx* is rendered "flesh", and its senses (the body, the human being, the human nature as opposed to the Spirit) are recorded in the footnotes. *Pneuma* is rendered "Spirit" or "spirit" and is tagged G-PNEUMA. *Hilasterion* (3:25) is rendered "place of atonement", and the footnote records "mercy seat" and "means of atonement". *Apolutrosis* is rendered "redemption" and is tagged G-LYTRON. *Katallage* is rendered "reconciliation". *Logizomai* is rendered "reckon". *Huiothesia* is rendered "adoption as sons". *Doulos* is rendered "slave". *Hagioi* is rendered "holy ones" and is tagged G-HAGIOS. *Ethne* is rendered "the nations". *Ioudaios* is rendered "Jew", as in Luke and Acts. *Adelphoi* is rendered "brothers", and the footnote records that in many places the Greek may include the sisters, and the team should decide. *Christos* is rendered "Christ" and is tagged G-CHRISTOS. *Agape* is rendered "love" and is tagged G-AGAPE. *Euangelion* is rendered "good news" and is tagged G-EUANGELION. The book falls into five parts in the WABV text: the greeting, the good news, the wrath of God and righteousness through faith (chapters 1 to 3), Abraham, peace with God, Adam and Christ and the slaves of righteousness (4 to 6), the law and sin, life in the Spirit and Israel (7 to 9), faith and confession, the olive tree and the living sacrifice (10 to 12), and the authorities, the weak and the strong, the plans of Paul and the greetings (13 to 16). The headings of the parts are not in the Greek. The markers beginning with G- (for Greek words) are defined in the Biblical Concepts and Language System, and the new ones are listed in the review note of the part where each first appears. The wording of the WABV text is drafted from the Greek and is not taken from any other English translation.*

## Part One: Chapters 1 to 3, the good news, the wrath and righteousness through faith

### Chapter 1

**1** Paul, a slave of Christ Jesus, called as an apostle, set apart for the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of God,

**2** which he promised beforehand through his prophets in holy writings,

**3** concerning his Son, who came from the seed of David according to the flesh,

**4** who was marked out as Son of God in power according to the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of holiness \[G-HAGIOS\], by the resurrection of the dead, Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] our Lord,

**5** through whom we received grace \[G-CHARIS\] and apostleship, to bring about the obedience of faith \[G-PISTIS\] among all the nations for the sake of his name,

**6** among whom you also are called to belong to Jesus Christ,

**7** to all those in Rome who are loved \[G-AGAPE\] by God, called to be holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\]: grace \[G-CHARIS\] to you and peace \[G-EIRENE\] from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**8** First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, because your faith \[G-PISTIS\] is announced throughout the whole world.

**9** For God, whom I serve with my spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] in the good news of his Son, is my witness how constantly I mention you

**10** in my prayers, always asking that somehow, now at last, by God's will, I may succeed in coming to you.

**11** For I long to see you, so that I may share some spiritual gift with you to strengthen you,

**12** that is, that we may be encouraged together by each other's faith \[G-PISTIS\], yours and mine.

**13** I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that I often planned to come to you (and have been prevented until now), so that I might have some fruit among you also, as among the rest of the nations.

**14** I am in debt to Greeks and to non-Greeks, to the wise and to the foolish.

**15** So, for my part, I am eager to announce the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] to you in Rome also.

**16** For I am not ashamed of the good news \[G-EUANGELION\], because it is the power of God for salvation \[G-SOZO\] to everyone who believes \[G-PISTIS\], to the Jew first and also to the Greek.

**17** For in it the righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] of God is revealed, from faith to faith, as it is written: "The righteous one will live by faith \[G-PISTIS\]."

**18** For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all godlessness and injustice of people who hold down the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] by injustice,

**19** because what can be known of God is plain among them, for God has made it plain to them.

**20** For since the creation of the world his invisible qualities, his eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly seen, being understood through what has been made. So they have no excuse,

**21** because, although they knew God, they did not honour him as God or give thanks, but their thinking became empty and their senseless hearts were darkened.

**22** Claiming to be wise, they became fools,

**23** and they exchanged the glory \[G-DOXA\] of the immortal God for the likeness of an image of mortal human beings and birds and four-footed animals and reptiles.

**24** Therefore God gave them up in the desires of their hearts to impurity, to the dishonouring of their bodies among themselves,

**25** because they exchanged the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] of God for the lie and worshipped and served what was created instead of the Creator, who is blessed for ever. Amen.

**26** For this reason God gave them up to shameful passions. Their women exchanged natural relations for what is against nature,

**27** and in the same way the men also left natural relations with women and burned in their desire for one another, men committing shameful acts with men and receiving in themselves the penalty that their error deserved.

**28** And since they did not think it worth while to keep God in their knowledge, God gave them up to a worthless mind, to do what is not fitting.

**29** They are filled with every kind of injustice, wickedness, greed and badness; full of envy, murder, strife, deceit and malice; whisperers,

**30** slanderers, haters of God, insolent, proud, boastful, inventors of evil, disobedient to parents,

**31** senseless, faithless, without natural affection, without mercy.

**32** Though they know the righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] decree of God, that those who practise such things deserve death, they not only do them but also applaud those who practise them.

### Chapter 2

**1** So you have no excuse, whoever you are, when you judge. For in judging another you condemn yourself, because you, the one who judges, practise the same things.

**2** We know that the judgement of God on those who practise such things is according to truth \[G-ALETHEIA\].

**3** Do you suppose, you who judge those who practise such things and yet do them yourself, that you will escape the judgement of God?

**4** Or do you despise the riches of his kindness and restraint and patience, not knowing that God's kindness is meant to lead you to repentance \[G-METANOIA\]?

**5** But because of your hard and unrepentant heart you are storing up wrath for yourself on the day of wrath, when the righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] judgement of God will be revealed.

**6** He will repay each person according to his deeds:

**7** eternal life \[G-ZOE\] to those who by patient endurance in doing good seek glory \[G-DOXA\] and honour and immortality,

**8** but wrath and fury to those who are self-seeking and who do not obey the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] but obey injustice.

**9** There will be distress and anguish for every human being who does evil, for the Jew first and also the Greek,

**10** but glory and honour and peace \[G-EIRENE\] for everyone who does good, for the Jew first and also the Greek.

**11** For with God there is no favouritism.

**12** For all who have sinned without the law will also perish without the law, and all who have sinned under the law will be judged through the law.

**13** For it is not the hearers of the law \[G-NOMOS\] who are righteous before God, but the doers of the law who will be declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

**14** For when nations that do not have the law do by nature what the law requires, these, though they do not have the law, are a law to themselves.

**15** They show that the work of the law is written on their hearts, while their conscience bears witness and their thoughts among themselves accuse or even defend them,

**16** on the day when, according to my good news \[G-EUANGELION\], God judges the secrets of people through Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] Jesus.

**17** But if you call yourself a Jew and rely on the law \[G-NOMOS\] and boast in God

**18** and know his will and approve what is excellent, being instructed from the law,

**19** and are confident that you are a guide for the blind, a light for those in darkness,

**20** an educator of the foolish, a teacher of children, having in the law the form of knowledge and of the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\],

**21** you then who teach another, do you not teach yourself? You who preach against stealing, do you steal?

**22** You who say that one must not commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who loathe idols, do you rob temples?

**23** You who boast in the law, do you dishonour God by breaking the law?

**24** For, as it is written, "The name of God is blasphemed among the nations because of you."

**25** For circumcision is of value if you practise the law, but if you are a lawbreaker, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.

**26** So if the uncircumcised keeps the righteous requirements \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] of the law, will his uncircumcision not be counted as circumcision?

**27** And the one who is physically uncircumcised but fulfils the law will judge you, who with the written code and circumcision are a lawbreaker.

**28** For the true Jew is not the one who is so on the outside, nor is circumcision what is outward in the flesh.

**29** But the true Jew is the one who is so on the inside, and circumcision is of the heart, in the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], not by the written code. His praise comes not from people but from God.

### Chapter 3

**1** What then is the advantage of the Jew? Or what is the value of circumcision?

**2** Great in every way. First of all, they were entrusted with the oracles of God.

**3** What if some were unfaithful? Will their unfaithfulness cancel the faithfulness of God?

**4** Certainly not. Let God be true, though every person is a liar, as it is written: "So that you may be declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] in your words and prevail when you are judged."

**5** But if our injustice shows up the righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] of God, what shall we say? That God is unjust to bring his wrath? (I am speaking as people do.)

**6** Certainly not. For how then would God judge the world?

**7** But if through my lie the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] of God abounds to his glory \[G-DOXA\], why am I still condemned as a sinner?

**8** And why not say, as some slanderously claim that we say, "Let us do evil so that good may come"? Their condemnation is deserved.

**9** What then? Are we better off? Not at all. For we have already charged that all, both Jews and Greeks, are under sin,

**10** as it is written: "There is no one righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\], not even one,

**11** there is no one who understands, there is no one who seeks God.

**12** All have turned aside, together they have become useless, there is no one who does good, not even one."

**13** "Their throat is an open grave, with their tongues they deceive." "The poison of asps is under their lips."

**14** "Their mouth is full of cursing and bitterness."

**15** "Their feet are quick to shed blood,

**16** ruin and misery are in their paths,

**17** and the way of peace \[G-EIRENE\] they have not known."

**18** "There is no fear of God before their eyes."

**19** Now we know that whatever the law \[G-NOMOS\] says, it says to those who are under the law, so that every mouth may be shut and the whole world may be answerable to God.

**20** For by works of the law no flesh will be declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] in his sight, since through the law comes knowledge of sin.

**21** But now the righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] of God has been made known apart from the law, though the Law and the Prophets bear witness to it,

**22** the righteousness of God through faith \[G-PISTIS\] in Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] for all who believe \[G-PISTIS\]. For there is no distinction,

**23** for all have sinned and fall short of the glory \[G-DOXA\] of God,

**24** and are declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] freely by his grace \[G-CHARIS\], through the redemption \[G-LYTRON\] that is in Christ Jesus,

**25** whom God put forward as a place of atonement, through faith \[G-PISTIS\], by his blood, to show his righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\], because in the restraint of God he had passed over the sins done before,

**26** to show his righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] at the present time, so that he might be righteous and the one who declares righteous the one who has faith \[G-PISTIS\] in Jesus.

**27** Where then is boasting? It is shut out. By what kind of law? Of works? No, but by the law \[G-NOMOS\] of faith \[G-PISTIS\].

**28** For we reckon that a person is declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by faith \[G-PISTIS\], apart from works of the law.

**29** Or is God the God of the Jews only? Is he not also the God of the nations? Yes, of the nations also,

**30** since God is one, who will declare righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] the circumcised by faith \[G-PISTIS\] and the uncircumcised through that same faith.

**31** Do we then cancel the law \[G-NOMOS\] through faith \[G-PISTIS\]? Certainly not. On the contrary, we uphold the law.

*Review note: Romans 1 to 3 were drafted in WABV wording, from the Greek, using the fixed choices set out in the introduction to this book. They are the greeting and the statement of the good news (1:1-7), the thanksgiving and the plans of Paul (1:8-15), the theme of the letter, the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith (1:16-17), the wrath of God against the godlessness of the nations (1:18-32), the judgement of God, which is without favouritism, and the Jew who has the law (2:1-29), the faithfulness of God and the charge that all are under sin (3:1-20), the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ, with the redemption and the place of atonement (3:21-26) and the exclusion of boasting and the upholding of the law (3:27-31). The numbering is the usual numbering. No new G- marker is used in this part, and the markers are G-EUANGELION (1:1, 1:15-16, 2:16), G-PNEUMA (1:4, 1:9, 2:29), G-HAGIOS (1:4, 1:7), G-CHRISTOS (1:4, 2:16, 3:22-24), G-CHARIS (1:5, 1:7, 3:24), G-PISTIS (1:5-17, 3:22-31), G-AGAPE (1:7), G-EIRENE (1:7, 2:10, 3:17), G-SOZO (1:16), G-DIKAIOSUNE (1:17, 1:32, 2:5-26, 3:4-30), G-ALETHEIA (1:18, 1:25, 2:2-20, 3:7), G-DOXA (1:23, 2:7, 3:7, 3:23), G-METANOIA (2:4), G-ZOE (2:7), G-NOMOS (2:13-3:31) and G-LYTRON (3:24). Verses 1:3-4 are rendered as written, with the Greek of 1:4 (*horisthentos*, "marked out"), which may also be read "appointed" or "declared", and the footnote records the matter. In 1:5 "the obedience of faith" may be read "the obedience that comes from faith" or "the obedience that is faith", and the footnote records the matter. In 1:7 "in Rome" is omitted by a few manuscripts, and the footnote records the matter. In 1:16 "to the Jew first and also to the Greek" is the Greek of the text, and some manuscripts add "of Christ" after "the good news", and the footnote records that the critical text omits it. In 1:17 "from faith to faith" is rendered as written, and the quotation is Habakkuk 2:4, and the footnote records that it may be read "the righteous one will live by faith" or "the one who is righteous by faith will live". In 1:18-32 the words on the wrath of God and on the exchange of truth are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. In 1:26-27 the verses are rendered as written, with no comment. In 1:29 some manuscripts add "sexual immorality" and "fornication" to the list, and the footnote records the matter. In 2:13 "the hearers of the law" and "the doers of the law" are rendered as written, and "will be declared righteous" translates *dikaiothesontai*. In 2:14 "nations" translates *ethne* without the article, and the footnote records that it may be read "Gentiles". In 2:15 "their thoughts among themselves accuse or even defend them" is the Greek of the text. In 2:16 "according to my good news" is the Greek of the text. In 2:17 "if you call yourself a Jew" is the reading of the critical text (*ei de su Ioudaios eponomazei*), and the Byzantine tradition has *ide su Ioudaios eponomazei*, "see, you call yourself a Jew", and the footnote records the matter. In 2:24 the quotation is Isaiah 52:5. In 2:26 "the righteous requirements" translates *ta dikaiomata*. In 2:29 "in the Spirit" and "by the written code" are rendered as written, and the footnote records the contrast. In 3:2 "the oracles of God" translates *ta logia tou theou*. In 3:3 "unfaithful" and "unfaithfulness" translate *epistesan* and *apistia*, and "the faithfulness of God" translates *ten pistin tou theou*, and the word is not tagged G-PISTIS in 3:3, because the sense is faithfulness, and the footnote records the matter. In 3:4 the quotation is Psalm 51:4 as in the Greek. In 3:10-18 the quotations are from Psalm 14:1-3 and 53:1-3, Psalm 5:9, Psalm 140:3, Psalm 10:7, Isaiah 59:7-8 and Psalm 36:1, and the footnote records the matter. In 3:20 "no flesh" translates *pasa sarx*, and the quotation echoes Psalm 143:2, and "works of the law" is rendered as written, and the footnote records the matter. In 3:21 "the Law and the Prophets" is the Greek of the text. In 3:22 "faith in Jesus Christ" translates *dia pisteos Iesou Christou*, which may also be read "through the faithfulness of Jesus Christ", and in 3:26 "the one who has faith in Jesus" translates *ton ek pisteos Iesou*, which may also be read "the one who lives from the faithfulness of Jesus", and the team should decide between the two readings, and the footnote records the matter. In 3:24 "are declared righteous" translates *dikaioumenoi*, and "redemption" translates *apolutroseos*, and the footnote records the link with G-LYTRON. In 3:25 "a place of atonement" translates *hilasterion*, which may also be read "mercy seat" or "means of atonement", and "through faith, by his blood" is the Greek of the text, and "in the restraint of God" translates *en te anoche tou theou*. In 3:25-26 "passed over" translates *paresin*, and the footnote records that it differs from *aphesis*, "forgiveness". In 3:27 "the law of faith" is rendered as written, and the footnote records that *nomos* here may mean a principle. In 3:28 "we reckon" is the reading of the critical text (*logizometha gar*), and the Byzantine tradition has *oun*, "therefore", and the footnote records the matter. In 3:30 "since God is one" is the reading of the critical text (*eiper heis ho theos*), and some manuscripts read *epeiper*, and the footnote records the matter. In 1:13, 7:1 and elsewhere "brothers" translates *adelphoi*, and the team should decide whether it is to include the sisters. The word *Ioudaios* is rendered "Jew" in 1:16, 2:9-29, 3:1-29, and the team should decide the rendering. There are no departures from the critical Greek text in chapters 1 to 3. The Greek is hard in some places, including 1:4-5, 1:17, 2:14-16, 2:26-29, 3:2-8, 3:22-26 and 3:28-31. The footnotes record the main differences and the uncertain terms. The words on the wrath of God (1:18-32), the righteous decree of God (1:32), the judgement of God according to deeds (2:6-11), the hearers and the doers of the law (2:13), the oracles of God and the faithfulness of God (3:2-4), the quotations from the psalms and Isaiah (3:10-18), the righteousness of God (3:21-26), the redemption and the place of atonement (3:24-25) and the upholding of the law (3:31) are rendered as written, with no comment on how they are read. The section needs a check against the Greek by a scholar and an originality check before it is locked.*

## Part Two: Chapters 4 to 6, Abraham, peace with God, Adam and Christ, and the slaves of righteousness

### Chapter 4

**1** What then shall we say that Abraham, our forefather according to the flesh, has found?

**2** For if Abraham was declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by works, he has something to boast about, but not before God.

**3** For what does the scripture say? "Abraham believed \[G-PISTIS\] God, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\]."

**4** Now to the one who works, his wages are not reckoned as a gift but as something owed.

**5** But to the one who does not work but believes \[G-PISTIS\] in the one who declares the ungodly righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\], his faith is reckoned as righteousness.

**6** So David also speaks of the blessing \[G-MAKARIOS\] of the person to whom God reckons righteousness apart from works:

**7** "Blessed are those whose lawless acts have been forgiven and whose sins have been covered.

**8** Blessed is the one against whom the Lord will never reckon sin."

**9** Is this blessing, then, only for the circumcised, or also for the uncircumcised? For we say, "Faith \[G-PISTIS\] was reckoned to Abraham as righteousness."

**10** How then was it reckoned? When he was circumcised, or before? Not after, but before.

**11** He received the sign of circumcision as a seal of the righteousness that he had by faith while he was still uncircumcised, so that he might be the father of all who believe \[G-PISTIS\] without being circumcised, so that righteousness might be reckoned to them also,

**12** and the father of the circumcised, who are not only circumcised but also follow in the footsteps of the faith that our father Abraham had before he was circumcised.

**13** For the promise to Abraham or to his seed, that he would inherit the world, did not come through the law \[G-NOMOS\] but through the righteousness of faith \[G-PISTIS\].

**14** For if those who live by the law are the heirs, faith is emptied and the promise is made void.

**15** For the law brings wrath, but where there is no law there is no transgression.

**16** For this reason it is from faith, so that it may be by grace \[G-CHARIS\], so that the promise may be secure for all his seed, not only for those of the law but also for those who share the faith of Abraham, who is the father of us all,

**17** as it is written: "I have made you the father of many nations." He is our father in the sight of God, in whom he believed, who gives life to the dead and calls things that do not exist into being.

**18** Against hope he believed in hope, so that he would become the father of many nations, as it had been said: "So will your seed be."

**19** Without weakening in faith \[G-PISTIS\] he considered his own body, which was as good as dead (since he was about a hundred years old), and the deadness of Sarah's womb.

**20** He did not waver in unbelief concerning the promise of God, but grew strong in faith, giving glory \[G-DOXA\] to God,

**21** fully convinced that what God had promised he was also able to do.

**22** That is why it was also reckoned to him as righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

**23** But it was not written for his sake alone that it was reckoned to him,

**24** but for ours also, to whom it is going to be reckoned, to us who believe \[G-PISTIS\] in the one who raised Jesus our Lord from the dead,

**25** who was handed over because of our trespasses and raised because of our being declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

### Chapter 5

**1** So, having been declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by faith \[G-PISTIS\], we have peace \[G-EIRENE\] with God through our Lord Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\],

**2** through whom we have also gained access by faith to this grace \[G-CHARIS\] in which we stand, and we boast in the hope of the glory \[G-DOXA\] of God.

**3** And not only that, but we also boast in our afflictions, knowing that affliction produces endurance,

**4** and endurance produces tested character, and tested character produces hope,

**5** and hope does not put us to shame, because the love \[G-AGAPE\] of God has been poured out in our hearts through the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] who was given to us.

**6** For while we were still weak, at the right time Christ died for the ungodly.

**7** For scarcely will anyone die for a righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] person, though for a good person perhaps someone might dare to die.

**8** But God demonstrates his own love \[G-AGAPE\] for us in that while we were still sinners Christ died for us.

**9** Since we have now been declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] by his blood, how much more will we be saved \[G-SOZO\] through him from the wrath.

**10** For if, while we were enemies, we were reconciled to God through the death of his Son, how much more, having been reconciled, will we be saved \[G-SOZO\] by his life \[G-ZOE\].

**11** And not only that, but we also boast in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received the reconciliation.

**12** So then, just as sin came into the world through one man, and death through sin, and so death spread to all people, because all sinned

**13** (for sin was in the world before the law, but sin is not charged to anyone's account when there is no law,

**14** yet death reigned from Adam until Moses, even over those who had not sinned in the likeness of Adam's transgression, who is a type of the one who was to come).

**15** But the gift is not like the trespass. For if the many died through the trespass of the one, much more the grace \[G-CHARIS\] of God and the gift by the grace of the one man Jesus Christ have overflowed to the many.

**16** And the gift is not like what came through the one who sinned. For the judgement that followed one trespass brought condemnation, but the free gift that followed many trespasses brought a declaration of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

**17** For if, by the trespass of the one, death reigned through the one, much more will those who receive the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] reign in life \[G-ZOE\] through the one, Jesus Christ.

**18** So then, as one trespass led to condemnation for all people, so one act of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] led to the declaration of life \[G-ZOE\] for all people.

**19** For as through the disobedience of the one man the many were made sinners, so through the obedience of the one the many will be made righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

**20** Now the law \[G-NOMOS\] came in alongside so that the trespass would increase, but where sin increased, grace \[G-CHARIS\] overflowed all the more,

### Chapter 6

**7** For the one who has died has been acquitted \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] of sin.

**8** Now if we died with Christ, we believe \[G-PISTIS\] that we will also live with him.

**9** We know that Christ, raised from the dead, will never die again. Death no longer has mastery over him.

**10** For the death he died, he died to sin once for all, but the life he lives, he lives to God.

**11** So you also must reckon yourselves dead to sin and alive to God in Christ Jesus.

**12** So do not let sin reign in your mortal body to make you obey its desires.

**13** And do not keep presenting your members to sin as weapons of injustice, but present yourselves to God as those who have come alive from the dead \[G-ZOE\], and your members to God as weapons of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

**14** For sin will not have mastery over you, since you are not under law \[G-NOMOS\] but under grace \[G-CHARIS\].

**15** What then? Are we to sin because we are not under law but under grace? Certainly not.

**17** But thanks be to God that you, who were slaves of sin, became obedient from the heart to the pattern of teaching to which you were handed over,

**18** and, set free from sin, you became slaves of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

**19** I am speaking in human terms because of the weakness of your flesh. For just as you once presented your members as slaves to impurity and to lawlessness leading to more lawlessness, so now present your members as slaves to righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] leading to holiness \[G-HAGIOS\].

**20** For when you were slaves of sin, you were free in regard to righteousness.

**21** So what fruit were you getting at that time from the things of which you are now ashamed? For the end of those things is death.

**22** But now that you have been set free from sin and have become slaves of God, the fruit you get leads to holiness \[G-HAGIOS\], and the end is eternal life \[G-ZOE\].

**23** For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift \[G-CHARIS\] of God is eternal life \[G-ZOE\] in Christ Jesus our Lord.

## Part Three: Chapters 7 to 9, the law and sin, life in the Spirit and Israel

### Chapter 7

**1** Or do you not know, brothers (for I am speaking to people who know the law \[G-NOMOS\]), that the law has authority over a person only as long as he lives?

**2** For a married woman is bound by law to her husband while he lives, but if her husband dies, she is released from the law of the husband.

**3** So then, if she becomes another man's while her husband is alive, she will be called an adulteress. But if her husband dies, she is free from the law, so that she is not an adulteress if she becomes another man's.

**4** So, my brothers, you also were made dead to the law \[G-NOMOS\] through the body of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], so that you might belong to another, to the one who was raised from the dead, in order that we might bear fruit for God.

**5** For when we were in the flesh, the sinful passions, aroused through the law, were at work in our members to bear fruit for death.

**6** But now we have been released from the law, having died to what held us down, so that we serve in the newness of the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] and not in the oldness of the written code.

**7** What shall we say then? Is the law sin? Certainly not. Yet I would not have known sin except through the law. For I would not have known what coveting is if the law had not said, "You shall not covet."

**8** But sin, taking its opportunity through the commandment, produced in me every kind of coveting. For apart from the law sin is dead.

**9** I was once alive apart from the law, but when the commandment came, sin came to life, and I died.

**10** The commandment that was meant for life \[G-ZOE\] turned out to be for death for me.

**11** For sin, taking its opportunity through the commandment, deceived me and through it killed me.

**12** So the law is holy \[G-HAGIOS\], and the commandment is holy and righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] and good.

**13** Did what is good, then, become death to me? Certainly not. It was sin, so that it might be shown up as sin, bringing about death for me through what is good, so that through the commandment sin might become exceedingly sinful.

**14** For we know that the law is spiritual \[G-PNEUMA\], but I am fleshly, sold under sin.

**15** For what I do, I do not understand. For I do not do what I want, but I do what I hate.

**16** But if I do what I do not want, I agree that the law is good.

**17** So now it is no longer I who do it, but sin that dwells in me.

**18** For I know that nothing good dwells in me, that is, in my flesh. For the wanting is present with me, but the doing of the good is not.

**19** For I do not do the good that I want, but the evil that I do not want, this I keep doing.

**20** But if I do what I do not want, it is no longer I who do it, but sin that dwells in me.

**21** So I find this law at work: when I want to do good, evil is right there with me.

**22** For I delight in the law of God in my inner person,

**23** but I see another law in my members, at war with the law of my mind and making me a prisoner of the law of sin that is in my members.

**24** Wretched person that I am. Who will rescue me from this body of death?

**25** Thanks be to God, through Jesus Christ our Lord. So then, I myself with my mind serve the law of God, but with my flesh the law of sin.

### Chapter 8

**1** So now there is no condemning verdict against those who are in Christ Jesus.

**2** For the law of the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of life \[G-ZOE\] in Christ Jesus has set you free from the law of sin and of death.

**3** For what the law was powerless to do, weakened as it was by the flesh, God did: by sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh and as an offering for sin, he condemned sin in the flesh,

**4** so that the righteous requirement \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] of the law \[G-NOMOS\] might be fulfilled in us, who walk not according to the flesh but according to the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

**5** For those who are according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh, but those who are according to the Spirit, on the things of the Spirit.

**6** For the mind of the flesh is death, but the mind of the Spirit is life \[G-ZOE\] and peace \[G-EIRENE\].

**7** For the mind of the flesh is hostile to God, for it does not submit to the law of God, and indeed it cannot.

**8** Those who are in the flesh cannot please God.

**9** But you are not in the flesh but in the Spirit, if indeed the Spirit of God dwells in you. And if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], this person does not belong to him.

**10** But if Christ is in you, although the body is dead because of sin, the Spirit is life because of righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

**11** And if the Spirit of the one who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you, the one who raised Christ from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through his Spirit who dwells in you.

**12** So then, brothers, we are in debt, not to the flesh, to live according to the flesh.

**13** For if you live according to the flesh, you are going to die, but if by the Spirit you put to death the practices of the body, you will live.

**14** For all who are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God.

**15** For you did not receive a spirit of slavery leading to fear again, but you received the Spirit of adoption as sons, in whom we cry, "Abba, Father."

**16** The Spirit himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God,

**17** and if children, also heirs: heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ, if indeed we suffer with him so that we may also be glorified \[G-DOXA\] with him.

**18** For I reckon that the sufferings of the present time are not worth comparing with the glory \[G-DOXA\] that is going to be revealed to us.

**19** For the creation waits with eager expectation for the revealing of the sons of God.

**20** For the creation was subjected to futility, not willingly, but through the one who subjected it, in hope

**21** that the creation itself will also be set free from its slavery to decay into the freedom of the glory of the children of God.

**22** For we know that the whole creation has been groaning together and suffering birth pains together until now.

**23** And not only that, but we ourselves, who have the firstfruits of the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], we also groan within ourselves, waiting eagerly for adoption as sons, the redemption \[G-LYTRON\] of our body.

**24** For in hope we were saved \[G-SOZO\]. But hope that is seen is not hope. For who hopes for what he sees?

**25** But if we hope for what we do not see, we wait for it with endurance.

**26** In the same way the Spirit also helps us in our weakness. For we do not know what to pray for as we should, but the Spirit himself pleads for us with groanings too deep for words.

**27** And the one who searches hearts knows what the mind of the Spirit is, because he pleads for the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\] in accordance with God.

**28** And we know that for those who love \[G-AGAPE\] God all things work together for good, for those who are called according to his purpose.

**29** For those whom he knew beforehand he also marked out beforehand to be shaped like the image of his Son, so that he might be the firstborn among many brothers.

**30** And those whom he marked out beforehand, these he also called. And those whom he called, these he also declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\]. And those whom he declared righteous, these he also glorified \[G-DOXA\].

**31** What then shall we say to these things? If God is for us, who is against us?

**32** He who did not spare his own Son but handed him over for us all, how will he not also give us all things with him, freely?

**33** Who will bring a charge against God's chosen ones? God is the one who declares righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\].

**34** Who is the one who condemns? Christ Jesus is the one who died, and even more, who was raised, who is at the right hand of God, who also pleads for us.

**35** Who will separate us from the love \[G-AGAPE\] of Christ? Affliction, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or danger, or sword?

**36** As it is written: "For your sake we are being put to death all day long. We were counted as sheep for slaughter."

**37** But in all these things we are more than conquerors through the one who loved \[G-AGAPE\] us.

**38** For I am convinced that neither death nor life, nor angels nor rulers, nor things present nor things to come, nor powers,

**39** nor height nor depth, nor any other created thing, will be able to separate us from the love \[G-AGAPE\] of God in Christ Jesus our Lord.

### Chapter 9

**1** I am telling the truth in Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], I am not lying, my conscience bearing witness with me in the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\],

**2** that I have great sorrow and unceasing pain in my heart.

**3** For I could wish to be accursed myself, cut off from Christ for the sake of my brothers, my kinsmen according to the flesh.

**4** They are Israelites, and to them belong the adoption as sons, the glory \[G-DOXA\], the covenants \[G-DIATHEKE\], the giving of the law \[G-NOMOS\], the worship and the promises.

**6** But it is not as though the word of God has failed. For not all who are from Israel are Israel,

**9** For this is the word of promise: "At this time I will come, and Sarah will have a son."

**10** And not only that, but Rebecca also, who conceived by one man, our father Isaac,

**11** for when they were not yet born and had done nothing good or bad, so that God's purpose of choice might stand, not from works but from the one who calls,

**12** it was said to her, "The older will serve the younger."

**13** As it is written: "Jacob I loved \[G-AGAPE\], but Esau I hated."

**14** What shall we say then? Is there injustice with God? Certainly not.

**15** For he says to Moses, "I will have mercy \[G-ELEOS\] on whom I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I have compassion."

**16** So then it does not depend on the one who wants or the one who runs, but on God who has mercy \[G-ELEOS\].

**17** For the scripture says to Pharaoh, "For this very purpose I raised you up, so that I might show my power in you, and so that my name might be proclaimed in all the earth."

**18** So then he has mercy on whom he wants, and he hardens whom he wants.

**19** You will say to me then, "Why does he still find fault? For who has resisted his will?"

**20** But who are you, a human being, to talk back to God? Will what is moulded say to the one who moulded it, "Why did you make me like this?"

**21** Does the potter not have authority over the clay, to make out of the same lump one vessel for honour and another for dishonour?

**22** What if God, wanting to show his wrath and to make his power known, put up with great patience with vessels of wrath that were made ready for destruction,

**23** so that he might make known the riches of his glory \[G-DOXA\] on vessels of mercy \[G-ELEOS\], which he prepared beforehand for glory,

**24** that is, us, whom he also called, not only from the Jews but also from the nations?

**25** As he also says in Hosea: "I will call those who were not my people 'my people,' and the one who was not loved \[G-AGAPE\] 'loved.'"

**26** "And in the very place where it was said to them, 'You are not my people,' there they will be called 'sons of the living God.'"

**27** And Isaiah cries out about Israel: "Though the number of the sons of Israel is like the sand of the sea, only a remnant will be saved \[G-SOZO\].

**28** For the Lord will carry out his word on the earth, finishing it and cutting it short."

**29** And as Isaiah said before: "If the Lord of hosts had not left us seed, we would have become like Sodom and been made like Gomorrah."

**30** What shall we say then? That the nations, who were not pursuing righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\], took hold of righteousness, the righteousness that is from faith \[G-PISTIS\],

**31** but Israel, pursuing a law of righteousness, did not reach that law.

**32** Why? Because it was not from faith but as if from works. They stumbled over the stone of stumbling,

**33** as it is written: "Look, I am laying in Zion a stone of stumbling and a rock of offence, and the one who believes \[G-PISTIS\] in him will not be put to shame."

## Part Four: Chapters 10 to 12, faith and confession, the olive tree and the living sacrifice

### Chapter 10

**1** Brothers, my heart's desire and my prayer to God for them is that they be saved \[G-SOZO\].

**2** I bear witness that they have a zeal for God, but not a zeal that is informed.

**3** Not knowing the righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] that comes from God, and trying to set up their own, they did not submit to the righteousness of God.

**4** For Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] is the goal of the law \[G-NOMOS\], bringing righteousness to everyone who believes \[G-PISTIS\].

**5** For Moses writes of the righteousness that comes from the law: "The person who does these things will live by them."

**6** But the righteousness that comes from faith \[G-PISTIS\] speaks like this: "Do not say in your heart, 'Who will go up into heaven?' (that is, to bring Christ down),

**7** or 'Who will go down into the abyss?' (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead)."

**8** But what does it say? "The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart" (that is, the word of faith that we announce):

**9** if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe \[G-PISTIS\] in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved \[G-SOZO\].

**10** For with the heart a person believes and so is declared righteous \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\], and with the mouth a person confesses and so is saved \[G-SOZO\].

**11** For the scripture says, "No one who believes \[G-PISTIS\] in him will be put to shame."

**12** For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, because the same Lord is Lord of all, rich toward all who call on him.

**13** For "everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved \[G-SOZO\]."

**14** But how will they call on one in whom they have not believed? And how will they believe in one of whom they have not heard? And how will they hear without someone to proclaim him?

**15** And how will they proclaim unless they are sent? As it is written: "How beautiful are the feet of those who announce good news \[G-EUANGELION\]."

**16** But not all obeyed the good news. For Isaiah says, "Lord, who has believed \[G-PISTIS\] what he heard from us?"

**17** So faith \[G-PISTIS\] comes from hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ.

**18** But I ask, did they not hear? They certainly did: "Their voice has gone out to all the earth, and their words to the ends of the inhabited world."

**19** But I ask, did Israel not know? First Moses says: "I will make you jealous of those who are not a nation. With a senseless nation I will make you angry."

**20** Then Isaiah is bold enough to say: "I was found by those who were not looking for me. I appeared to those who were not asking about me."

**21** But of Israel he says: "All day long I held out my hands to a disobedient and contrary people."

### Chapter 11

**1** I ask then, has God pushed his people away? Certainly not. For I too am an Israelite, a descendant of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.

**2** God has not pushed away his people, whom he knew beforehand. Or do you not know what the scripture says about Elijah, how he pleads with God against Israel:

**3** "Lord, they have killed your prophets, they have torn down your altars, and I alone am left, and they are looking for my life \[G-PSYCHE\]."

**4** But what does the divine answer say to him? "I have kept for myself seven thousand men who have not bent the knee to Baal."

**5** So too at the present time there is a remnant, chosen by grace \[G-CHARIS\].

**6** But if it is by grace, it is no longer from works, since otherwise grace would no longer be grace.

**7** What then? What Israel is looking for, it did not obtain, but the chosen did obtain it. The rest were hardened,

**8** as it is written: "God gave them a spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of stupor, eyes that would not see and ears that would not hear, down to this very day."

**9** And David says: "Let their table become a snare and a trap, a stumbling block and a payback for them.

**10** Let their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see, and keep their backs bent for ever."

**11** I ask then, did they stumble so as to fall? Certainly not. But through their trespass salvation \[G-SOZO\] has come to the nations, to make Israel jealous.

**12** Now if their trespass means riches for the world, and their failure riches for the nations, how much more will their full number mean.

**13** Now I am speaking to you who are of the nations. Inasmuch as I am an apostle to the nations, I honour my ministry

**14** in the hope of making my own people jealous and so saving some of them.

**15** For if their rejection means the reconciliation of the world, what will their acceptance mean but life from the dead?

**16** If the first portion of dough is holy \[G-HAGIOS\], so is the whole lump, and if the root is holy, so are the branches.

**17** But if some of the branches were broken off, and you, a wild olive, were grafted in among them and became a sharer in the rich root of the olive tree,

**18** do not boast over the branches. If you do boast, remember that you do not support the root, but the root supports you.

**19** You will say, "Branches were broken off so that I could be grafted in."

**20** True. They were broken off because of unbelief, but you stand by faith \[G-PISTIS\]. Do not be arrogant, but be afraid.

**21** For if God did not spare the natural branches, he will not spare you either.

**22** Look then at the kindness and the severity of God: severity toward those who fell, but kindness toward you, as long as you stay in his kindness. Otherwise you too will be cut off.

**23** And they also, if they do not stay in unbelief, will be grafted in, for God is able to graft them in again.

**24** For if you were cut from what is by nature a wild olive and grafted, against nature, into a cultivated olive, how much more will these, the natural branches, be grafted into their own olive tree.

**25** For I do not want you to be unaware of this mystery, brothers, so that you do not think too well of yourselves: a hardening has come over part of Israel, until the full number of the nations has come in,

**26** and in this way all Israel will be saved \[G-SOZO\], as it is written: "The Deliverer will come out of Zion, he will turn godlessness away from Jacob,

**27** and this will be my covenant \[G-DIATHEKE\] with them, when I take away their sins."

**28** As regards the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] they are enemies for your sake, but as regards election they are loved \[G-AGAPE\] for the sake of the fathers.

**29** For the gifts and the calling of God cannot be taken back.

**30** For just as you were once disobedient to God but have now received mercy \[G-ELEOS\] through their disobedience,

**31** so they too have now been disobedient, so that through the mercy shown to you they also may now receive mercy.

**32** For God has locked up all in disobedience, so that he may have mercy \[G-ELEOS\] on all.

**33** Oh, the depth of the riches and the wisdom and the knowledge of God. How unsearchable are his judgements and how untraceable his ways.

**34** "For who has known the mind of the Lord, or who has been his counsellor?"

**35** "Or who has first given to him, so that it must be paid back?"

**36** For from him and through him and to him are all things. To him be the glory \[G-DOXA\] for ever. Amen.

### Chapter 12

**1** So I urge you, brothers, by the compassions of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy \[G-HAGIOS\] and pleasing to God, which is your worship, offered with understanding.

**2** Do not be shaped by this age, but be changed by the renewing of your mind, so that you can test and approve what the will of God is, what is good and pleasing and complete.

**3** For through the grace \[G-CHARIS\] given to me I say to everyone among you not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think, but to think with sober judgement, each according to the measure of faith \[G-PISTIS\] that God has dealt out.

**4** For just as in one body we have many members, and the members do not all have the same function,

**5** so we, though many, are one body in Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], and individually members of one another.

**6** Having gifts that differ according to the grace \[G-CHARIS\] given to us, let us use them: if prophecy, in proportion to faith \[G-PISTIS\];

**7** if service, in serving; the one who teaches, in teaching;

**8** the one who encourages, in encouragement; the one who shares, with generosity; the one who leads, with diligence; the one who shows mercy \[G-ELEOS\], with cheerfulness.

**9** Let love \[G-AGAPE\] be genuine. Hate what is evil, hold fast to what is good.

**10** Be devoted to one another in family love. In showing honour, take the lead in giving it to one another.

**11** Do not be slow in zeal. Be on fire in the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\]. Serve the Lord.

**12** Rejoice in hope, be patient in affliction, be constant in prayer.

**13** Share with the needs of the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\], and pursue hospitality.

**14** Bless those who persecute you. Bless, and do not curse.

**15** Rejoice with those who rejoice, weep with those who weep.

**16** Live in harmony with one another. Do not set your mind on high things, but be drawn to lowly ones. Do not think too well of yourselves.

**17** Repay no one evil for evil, but plan to do what is honourable in the sight of everyone.

**18** If it is possible, as far as it depends on you, live at peace \[G-EIRENE\] with everyone.

**19** Do not take revenge yourselves, loved ones \[G-AGAPE\], but leave room for the wrath of God, for it is written: "Vengeance belongs to me, I will repay, says the Lord."

**20** "But if your enemy is hungry, feed him, and if he is thirsty, give him a drink. For by doing this you will heap burning coals on his head."

**21** Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.

## Part Five: Chapters 13 to 16, the authorities, the weak and the strong, the plans and the greetings

### Chapter 13

**1** Everyone must submit to the governing authorities. For there is no authority except from God, and those that exist have been put in place by God.

**2** So the one who resists the authority opposes what God has ordered, and those who oppose it will bring judgement on themselves.

**3** For rulers are not a terror to good conduct but to bad. Do you want to have no fear of the authority? Do what is good, and you will have praise from it,

**4** for it is God's servant for your good. But if you do wrong, be afraid, for it does not carry the sword for nothing. It is God's servant, an avenger who brings wrath on the one who does wrong.

**5** So it is necessary to submit, not only because of wrath but also because of conscience.

**6** This is also why you pay taxes, for the authorities are God's ministers, attending to this very thing.

**7** Give everyone what is owed: taxes to the one owed taxes, revenue to the one owed revenue, respect to the one owed respect, honour to the one owed honour.

**8** Owe no one anything, except to love \[G-AGAPE\] one another, for the one who loves another has fulfilled the law \[G-NOMOS\].

**9** For the commandments, "You shall not commit adultery, you shall not murder, you shall not steal, you shall not covet," and any other commandment, are summed up in this word: "You shall love \[G-AGAPE\] your neighbour as yourself."

**10** Love does no wrong to a neighbour. So love is the fulfilling of the law.

**11** And do this, knowing the time: it is already the hour for you to wake from sleep, for salvation \[G-SOZO\] is nearer to us now than when we first believed \[G-PISTIS\].

**12** The night is far gone, the day is near. So let us put aside the works of darkness and put on the weapons of light.

**13** Let us walk properly, as in the day, not in revelry and drunkenness, not in sexual excess and indecency, not in strife and jealousy.

**14** Instead, put on the Lord Jesus Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\], and make no provision for the flesh, to satisfy its desires.

### Chapter 14

**1** Welcome the one who is weak in faith \[G-PISTIS\], but not to argue over opinions.

**2** One person believes that he may eat anything, while the weak person eats only vegetables.

**3** The one who eats must not look down on the one who does not eat, and the one who does not eat must not pass judgement on the one who eats, for God has welcomed him.

**4** Who are you to pass judgement on someone else's servant? It is before his own master that he stands or falls. And he will stand, for the Lord is able to make him stand.

**5** One person judges one day to be better than another, while another judges all days to be alike. Let each be fully convinced in his own mind.

**6** The one who observes the day observes it for the Lord. The one who eats, eats for the Lord, since he gives thanks to God, while the one who does not eat, abstains for the Lord and gives thanks to God.

**7** For none of us lives to himself, and none of us dies to himself.

**8** For if we live, we live to the Lord, and if we die, we die to the Lord. So whether we live or whether we die, we are the Lord's.

**9** For to this end Christ died and lived again, so that he might be Lord of both the dead and the living.

**10** But you, why do you pass judgement on your brother? Or you, why do you look down on your brother? For we will all stand before the judgement seat of God.

**11** For it is written: "As I live, says the Lord, every knee will bow to me, and every tongue will give praise to God."

**12** So then, each of us will give an account of himself to God.

**13** So let us no longer pass judgement on one another, but decide this instead: never to put a stumbling block or a trap in the way of a brother.

**14** I know and am convinced in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean in itself, but it is unclean for anyone who thinks it unclean.

**15** For if your brother is distressed by what you eat, you are no longer walking in love \[G-AGAPE\]. Do not by your food destroy the one for whom Christ died.

**16** So do not let what is good for you be spoken of as evil.

**17** For the kingdom \[G-BASILEIA\] of God is not eating and drinking but righteousness \[G-DIKAIOSUNE\] and peace \[G-EIRENE\] and joy in the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

**18** Whoever serves Christ in this way is pleasing to God and approved by people.

**19** So let us pursue what makes for peace and for building one another up.

**20** Do not, for the sake of food, tear down the work of God. Everything is clean, but it is wrong for a person to make another stumble by what he eats.

**21** It is good not to eat meat or drink wine or do anything that makes your brother stumble.

**22** The faith \[G-PISTIS\] that you have, have as your own before God. Blessed \[G-MAKARIOS\] is the one who has no reason to condemn himself in what he approves.

**23** But the one who has doubts is condemned if he eats, because the eating is not from faith \[G-PISTIS\]. For whatever is not from faith is sin.

### Chapter 15

**1** We who are strong have a duty to bear with the weaknesses of those who are not strong, and not to please ourselves.

**2** Let each of us please his neighbour for his good, to build him up.

**3** For Christ \[G-CHRISTOS\] also did not please himself, but as it is written: "The insults of those who insulted you fell on me."

**4** For whatever was written in former days was written for our instruction, so that through endurance and through the encouragement of the scriptures we might have hope.

**5** May the God of endurance and encouragement give you to live in harmony with one another, in accord with Christ Jesus,

**6** so that together you may with one voice glorify \[G-DOXA\] the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**7** So welcome one another, as Christ has welcomed you, for the glory \[G-DOXA\] of God.

**8** For I tell you that Christ became a servant of the circumcised for the sake of the truth \[G-ALETHEIA\] of God, to confirm the promises given to the fathers,

**9** and so that the nations might glorify God for his mercy \[G-ELEOS\]. As it is written: "For this I will praise you among the nations, and sing to your name."

**10** And again it says: "Rejoice, you nations, with his people."

**11** And again: "Praise the Lord, all you nations, and let all the peoples praise him."

**12** And again Isaiah says: "The root of Jesse will come, the one who rises to rule the nations, and in him the nations will hope."

**13** May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace \[G-EIRENE\] in believing \[G-PISTIS\], so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

**14** I myself am convinced about you, my brothers, that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, and able to instruct one another.

**15** But on some points I have written to you more boldly, as a reminder, because of the grace \[G-CHARIS\] given to me by God

**16** to be a minister of Christ Jesus to the nations in the priestly service of the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of God, so that the offering of the nations may be acceptable, made holy \[G-HAGIOS\] by the Holy Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\].

**17** In Christ Jesus, then, I have reason to be proud of my work for God.

**18** For I will not dare to speak of anything except what Christ has accomplished through me to bring the nations to obedience, by word and deed,

**19** by the power of signs and wonders, by the power of the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\] of God, so that from Jerusalem and around as far as Illyricum I have fully preached the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] of Christ.

**20** And so I made it my ambition to preach the good news \[G-EUANGELION\] not where Christ has already been named, so that I would not build on someone else's foundation,

**21** but as it is written: "Those who have never been told of him will see, and those who have never heard will understand."

**22** This is why I have so often been prevented from coming to you.

**23** But now, with no more room for work in these regions, and with a longing of many years to come to you,

**24** I hope to see you in passing as I go to Spain, and to be helped on my journey there by you, once I have first enjoyed your company for a while.

**25** At present, though, I am going to Jerusalem to serve the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\].

**26** For Macedonia and Achaia have been glad to make a contribution for the poor among the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\] at Jerusalem.

**27** They were glad to do it, and indeed they owe it to them. For if the nations have shared in their spiritual blessings, they owe it to them also to serve them in material blessings.

**28** So when I have completed this and have delivered to them what has been raised, I will leave for Spain by way of you.

**29** I know that when I come to you I will come in the fullness of the blessing of Christ.

**30** I urge you, brothers, by our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love \[G-AGAPE\] of the Spirit \[G-PNEUMA\], to strive together with me in your prayers to God on my behalf,

**31** that I may be rescued from those in Judea who are disobedient, and that my service for Jerusalem may be acceptable to the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\],

**32** so that by God's will I may come to you with joy and be refreshed together with you.

**33** The God of peace \[G-EIRENE\] be with you all. Amen.

### Chapter 16

**1** I commend to you our sister Phoebe, a servant of the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] at Cenchreae,

**2** so that you may welcome her in the Lord in a way worthy of the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\], and help her in whatever matter she may need from you, for she herself has been a benefactor of many and of myself as well.

**3** Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus,

**4** who risked their own necks for my life \[G-PSYCHE\], to whom not only I give thanks but also all the assemblies \[G-EKKLESIA\] of the nations.

**5** Greet also the assembly \[G-EKKLESIA\] that meets in their house. Greet my beloved \[G-AGAPE\] Epaenetus, who was the first convert to Christ in Asia.

**6** Greet Mary, who has worked hard for you.

**7** Greet Andronicus and Junia, my kinsmen and my fellow prisoners. They are well known among the apostles, and they were in Christ before me.

**8** Greet Ampliatus, my beloved \[G-AGAPE\] in the Lord.

**9** Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and my beloved Stachys.

**10** Greet Apelles, who is approved in Christ. Greet those who belong to the household of Aristobulus.

**11** Greet my kinsman Herodion. Greet those in the household of Narcissus who are in the Lord.

**12** Greet those workers in the Lord, Tryphaena and Tryphosa. Greet the beloved Persis, who has worked hard in the Lord.

**13** Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother, who has been a mother to me as well.

**14** Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas and the brothers who are with them.

**15** Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas and all the holy ones \[G-HAGIOS\] who are with them.

**16** Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the assemblies \[G-EKKLESIA\] of Christ greet you.

**17** I urge you, brothers, to watch out for those who cause divisions and obstacles contrary to the teaching that you have learned. Turn away from them.

- **13:9** The words are from Exodus 20:13-17 and Leviticus 19:18.

- **15:9** "Mercy" translates *eleous*. See entry G-ELEOS. The quotations in verses 9 to 12 are from Psalm 18:49, Deuteronomy 32:43, Psalm 117:1 and Isaiah 11:10 as in the Greek.

- **15:21** The words are from Isaiah 52:15.
- **15:24** "Spain" translates *Spanian*.

- **15:30** "The love of the Spirit" translates *agapes tou pneumatos*. See entry G-AGAPE.
- **15:31** "Disobedient" translates *apeithounton*, which may also be read "unbelieving".
- **15:33** "Peace" translates *eirenes*. See entry G-EIRENE. Papyrus 46 places the doxology of 16:25-27 after this verse.
- **16:1** "A servant" translates *diakonon*, which may also be read "deacon" or "minister". "The assembly" translates *ekklesias*. See entry G-EKKLESIA.

- **16:17** "To watch out for those who cause divisions" translates *skopein tous tas dichostasias poiountas*.

- **16:20** "The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet" is rendered as written. "The grace of our Lord Jesus be with you" is the reading of the critical text, and some manuscripts add "Christ" and "Amen". See entries G-EIRENE and G-CHARIS.

- **16:23** "The city treasurer" translates *oikonomos tes poleos*.
- **16:24** The verse is not in the critical Greek text. The Byzantine tradition has "The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen".
- **16:25** The doxology of verses 25 to 27 stands at the end of the letter in the critical text. It stands after 14:23 in many Byzantine manuscripts, and after 15:33 in Papyrus 46, and a few Western manuscripts omit it. "My good news" translates *to euangelion mou*, and "the revelation of the mystery" is rendered as written. See entries G-EUANGELION and G-CHRISTOS.
- **16:26** "The obedience of faith" is as in 1:5. See entry G-PISTIS.
- **16:27** "The only wise God" is the reading of the critical text, and some manuscripts omit "wise". See entry G-DOXA.
